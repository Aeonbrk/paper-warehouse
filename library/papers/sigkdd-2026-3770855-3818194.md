# TRIAD: Ternary Information Routing for Multimodal Time-Series Sensing Signals

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multimodal time series captured by heterogeneous sensors are fundamental to real-world applications, yet learning representations without labels remains challenging.
- Proposes TRIAD, a framework that advances multimodal disentanglement from a binary to a ternary paradigm by separating a triad of information components: redundancy, synergy, and uniqueness.
- Experiments on synthetic and real-world benchmarks show consistent improvements over methods, with ablations, robustness tests, and interpretability analyses confirming that the learned components are functionally specialized.
