# Fair Diversity Maximization with Few Representatives

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Diversity maximization problem is a well-studied problem where the goal is to find k diverse items.
- In such scenario show that a randomized algorithm based on padded decompositions improves the approximation ratio to √log(m) /(3m), where m is the number of labels.
- Fair diversity maximization aims to select a diverse subset of k items from a large dataset, while requiring that each group of items be well represented in the output.
