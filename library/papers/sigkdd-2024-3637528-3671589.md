# Hyper-Local Deformable Transformers for Text Spotting on Historical Maps

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Text on historical maps contains valuable information providing georeferenced historical, political, and cultural contexts.
- Recent machine learning-based text spotters (e.g., for scene images) have the potential to solve these challenges because of their flexibility in supporting various types of text instances.
- The experiment shows that PALETTE with SYNTHMAP+ outperforms SOTA text spotters on two new benchmark datasets of historical maps, particularly for long and angled text.
