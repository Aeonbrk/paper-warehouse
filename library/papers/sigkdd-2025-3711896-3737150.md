# Temporal Restoration and Spatial Rewiring for Source-Free Multivariate Time Series Domain Adaptation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Source-Free Domain Adaptation (SFDA) aims to adapt a pre-trained model from an annotated source domain to an unlabelled target domain without accessing the source data, thereby preserving data privacy.
- Proposes Temporal Restoration and Spatial Rewiring (TERSE), a and concise SFDA method tailored for MTS data.
- Experiments on three real-world time series datasets demonstrate the effectiveness and versatility of our approach.
