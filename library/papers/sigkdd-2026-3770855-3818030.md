# StablePFN: Stable Prediction with Causal-Aware Tabular Foundation Model

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Pre-trained tabular prediction models based on Prior-Data Fitted Networks (PFNs), such as TabPFN and LimiX, have achieved progress in supervised learning, demonstrating immense potential across real-world scenarios and diverse downstream tasks.
- Proposes StablePFN, a tabular foundation model that integrates explicit causal awareness with stable predictive modeling.
- Experiments on both synthetic and real-world benchmarks demonstrate that StablePFN outperforms baselines in cross-environment prediction settings, particularly in challenging high-bias scenarios.
