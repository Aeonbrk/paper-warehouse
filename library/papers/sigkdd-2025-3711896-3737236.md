# Incomplete Multi-View Drug Recommendation via Multi-Level Representation Learning and Curriculum Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The drug recommendation task aims to provide and safe prescription decision support for clinical treatment based on patients' past Electronic Health Records (EHR).
- Emphasize the problems of incompleteness in practical drug recommendation and propose the Incomplete Multi-View Drug Recommendation model via Multi-Level Representation Learning and Curriculum Learning named IMDR.
- Systematic evaluation on two large-scale real-world medical datasets, MIMIC-III and MIMIC-IV, demonstrates that IMDR reduces the Drug-Drug Interaction (DDI) rate by 2.97% compared to existing drug recommendation baselines.
