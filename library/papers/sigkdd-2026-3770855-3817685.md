# Semi-Supervised Text-Attributed Graph Distillation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Text-Attributed Graphs (TAGs) have emerged as an expressive data model for integrating graph topology with rich textual semantics.
- Proposes STAD, a unified semi-supervised framework guided by the Wasserstein Distance (WSD).
- Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that STAD achieves a performance-compression trade-off in terms of both GNN- and LLM-based downstream tasks, enabling and efficient TAG learning or analytics.
