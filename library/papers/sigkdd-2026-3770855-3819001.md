# Compass: Navigating Global Marine Lead Data Integration through Expert-Guided LLM Agent

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Marine lead (Pb) and its isotopes are critical tracers for ocean circulation and anthropogenic pollution, yet in-situ observations remain costly and sparse.
- Introduces an expert-guided adaptation approach that enables LLMs to perform rigorous scientific data extraction without fine-tuning.
- Beyond standard metrics, Compass demonstrates reliability through multi-layered validation, achieving 92% accuracy as confirmed through expert manual verification.
