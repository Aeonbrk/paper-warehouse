# Physics-Guided Learning of Meteorological Dynamics for Weather Downscaling and Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Weather forecasting is essential but remains computationally intensive and physically incomplete in traditional numerical weather prediction (NWP) methods.
- Proposes PhyDL-NWP, a physics-guided deep learning framework that integrates physical equations with latent force parameterization into data-driven models.
- Deep learning (DL) models offer efficiency and accuracy but often ignore physical laws, limiting interpretability and generalization.
