# Error Bounds for the Network Scale-Up Method

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Epidemiologists and social scientists have used the Network Scale-Up Method (NSUM) for over thirty years to estimate the size of a hidden sub-population within a social network.
- This method involves querying a subset of network nodes about the number of their neighbors belonging to the hidden sub-population.
- Our theoretical analysis is supported by an set of numerical experiments designed to determine the effect of the sample size on the accuracy of the estimates in both synthetic and real networks.
