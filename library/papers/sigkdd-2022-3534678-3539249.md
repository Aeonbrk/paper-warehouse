# GPPT: Graph Pre-training and Prompt Tuning to Generalize Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Despite the representation learning of graph neural networks (GNNs), the supervised training of GNNs notoriously requires large amounts of labeled data from each application.
- Proposes a transfer learning paradigm to generalize GNNs, namely graph pre-training and prompt tuning (GPPT).
- The experiments on eight benchmark datasets report higher performance for GPPT, delivering an average improvement of 4.29% in few-shot graph analysis and accelerating the model convergence up to 4.32X.
