# Disentangled Multi-interest Representation Learning for Sequential Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Much effort has been devoted to modeling users' multi-interests (aka multi-faceted preferences) based on their behaviors, aiming to accurately capture users' complex preferences.
- Proposes a generic multi-interest method for sequential recommendation, achieving disentangled representation learning of diverse interests technically and theoretically.
- Experiments on three real-world datasets show that our method outperforms methods and can flexibly integrate with existing multi-interest models as a plugin to enhance their performances.
