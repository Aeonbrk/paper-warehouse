# Explicit-Implicit Entity Alignment Method in Multi-modal Knowledge Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multi-modal entity alignment (MMEA) aims to find the equivalent entities between multi-modal knowledge graphs (MMKGs).
- Introduces a conflict-aware soft pseudo-labeling method to further optimize semi-supervised learning for implicit modality.
- Experiments have demonstrated that our proposed paradigm and algorithm achieve the performance on five real-world MMEA datasets.
