# Mitigating Privacy Risks in Graph Condensation from a Hyperbolic Geometry Perspective

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph condensation reduces large graphs into smaller synthetic ones for efficient training and potential privacy protection.
- Proposes a graph condensation framework (HDGC) that investigates privacy issues in graph condensation from a hyperbolic geometric perspective.
- While existing studies demonstrate graph condensation's resilience against membership inference attacks (MIAs), key questions remain unanswered: Can the common MIAs' accuracy truly represent the privacy-preserving capabilities of graph condensation?
