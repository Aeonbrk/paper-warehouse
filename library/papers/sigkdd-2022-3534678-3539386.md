# Open-Domain Aspect-Opinion Co-Mining with Double-Layer Span Extraction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The aspect-opinion extraction tasks extract aspect terms and opinion terms from reviews.
- Proposes an Open-Domain Aspect-Opinion Co-Mining (ODAO) method with a Double-Layer span extraction framework.
- The results on four benchmark datasets for aspect-opinion co-extraction and pair extraction tasks show that ODAO can achieve competitive or even better performance compared with the fully supervised methods.
