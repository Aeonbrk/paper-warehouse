# Spatial Hierarchical Meta-Learning for Single-Point Map Matching

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Inferring the actual road segment purely based on one positioning point, known as single-point map matching (SMM), is vital for many urban applications, e.g., ride-hailing and geo-tagging.
- Treat each region as an individual SMM task to tackle the heterogeneity, and propose Spatial Hierarchical Meta-Learning for SMM (SHSMM) to learn the shared knowledge across tasks.
- Experiments as well as case studies based on two real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
