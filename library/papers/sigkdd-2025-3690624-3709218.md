# Wedjat: Detecting Sophisticated Evasion Attacks via Real-time Causal Analysis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Traffic encryption has been widely adopted to protect the confidentiality and integrity of Internet traffic.
- Develops Wedjat, which utilizes a causal network to model benign packet interactions among relevant flows, such that it recognizes abnormal causality that represents malicious traffic and disrupted causality incurred by evasion attacks.
- The experimental results demonstrate that Wedjat achieves an accuracy of 0.957 F1-score when detecting various advanced attacks.
