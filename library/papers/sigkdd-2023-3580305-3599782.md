# Binary Embedding-based Retrieval at Tencent

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large-scale embedding-based retrieval (EBR) is the cornerstone of search-related industrial applications.
- Proposes a binary embedding-based retrieval (BEBR) engine equipped with a recurrent binarization algorithm that enables customized bits per dimension.
- Experiments on offline benchmarks and online A/B tests demonstrate the efficiency and effectiveness of our method, saving 30% ~ 50% index costs with almost no loss of accuracy at the system level1 is.
