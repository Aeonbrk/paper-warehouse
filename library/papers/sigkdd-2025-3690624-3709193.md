# Path Complex Neural Networks for Sequential Process Activities Classification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Process mining aims to uncover, track, and enhance real-world workflows by deriving insights from event logs commonly found in modern information systems.
- The proposed framework, Path Complex Neural Networks (PCNN), leverages the advantages of topological deep learning and obtains representations for higher-order complexes inductively.
- Additionally, evaluated the results with four real-world benchmark datasets and found that PCNN outperforms existing models in analysing sequential and complex process data.
