## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Researchers recently started developing deep learning models capable of handling non-Euclidean data.
- Proposes the non-Euclidean Hybrid Learning (NEHL) method, a causal discovery algorithm relying on the concept of the ball covariance recently introduced in the statistics field.
- Finally, experimental results on the generated data and real-world data demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed NEHL method.
