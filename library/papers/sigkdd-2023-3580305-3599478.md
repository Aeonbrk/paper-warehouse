## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spectral methods for graph neural networks (GNNs) have achieved great success.
- Develops an ω-bandlimited downsampling approach to split input graph into subgraphs for the reduction of high-frequency components, then perform spectral graph filters on subgraphs to achieve node embeddings with different frequency bands.
