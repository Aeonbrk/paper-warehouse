# Diffusion-Based Cloud-Edge-Device Collaborative Learning for Next POI Recommendations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The rapid expansion of Location-Based Social Networks (LBSNs) has highlighted the importance of next Point-of-Interest (POI) recommendations, which leverage historical check-in data to predict users' next POIs to visit.
- Introduces a collaborative learning framework, Diffusion-Based Cloud-Edge-Device Collaborative Learning for Next POI Recommendations (DCPR), leveraging the diffusion model known for its success across various domains.
- Our evaluation with two real-world datasets demonstrates DCPR's performance in recommendation accuracy, efficiency, and adaptability to new users and regions, marking a step forward in on-device POI recommendation technology.
