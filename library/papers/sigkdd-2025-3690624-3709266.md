# TSINR: Capturing Temporal Continuity via Implicit Neural Representations for Time Series Anomaly Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Time series anomaly detection aims to identify unusual patterns in data or deviations from systems' expected behavior.
- Proposes a time series anomaly detection method based on implicit neural representation (INR) reconstruction, named TSINR, to address this challenge.
- As a result, the proposed TSINR method achieves the advantage of capturing the temporal continuity and thus is more sensitive to discontinuous anomaly data.
