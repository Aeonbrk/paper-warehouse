# Select and Trade: Towards Unified Pair Trading with Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Pair trading is one of the most statistical arbitrage strategies which seeks a neutral profit by hedging a pair of selected assets.
- Designs a hierarchical reinforcement learning framework to jointly learn and optimize two sub-tasks.
- Experimental results on real-world stock data demonstrate the effectiveness of our method on pair trading compared with both existing pair selection and trading methods.
