# Merlin: Multi-View Representation Learning for Robust Multivariate Time Series Forecasting with Unfixed Missing Rates

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multivariate Time Series Forecasting (MTSF) involves predicting future values of multiple interrelated time series.
- Proposes Multi-View Representation Learning (Merlin), which can help existing models achieve semantic alignment between incomplete observations with different missing rates and complete observations in MTS.
- Merlin is capable of effectively enhancing the robustness of existing models against unfixed missing rates while preserving forecasting accuracy.
