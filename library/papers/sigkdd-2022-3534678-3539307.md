# End-to-End Semi-Supervised Ordinal Regression AUC Maximization with Convolutional Kernel Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Convolutional kernel networks (CKN) have been proposed to solve image classification tasks, and have shown competitive performance over classical neural networks while being easy to train and robust to overfitting.
- Proposes a nested alternating minimization algorithm to solve the non-convex objective.
- Experimental results demonstrate that our S^2 CKNOR achieves the best AUC results on various real-world datasets.
