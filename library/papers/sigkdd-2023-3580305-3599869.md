# Multi-factor Sequential Re-ranking with Perception-Aware Diversification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Feed recommendation systems, which recommend a sequence of items for users to browse and interact with, have gained popularity in practical applications.
- Proposes a general re-ranking framework named Multi-factor Sequential Re-ranking with Perception-Aware Diversification~(MPAD) to jointly optimize accuracy and diversity for feed recommendation in a sequential manner.
- Traditional methods that mainly focus on improving the accuracy of recommended items are suboptimal for feed recommendations because they may recommend highly similar items.
