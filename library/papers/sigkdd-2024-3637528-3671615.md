# DAG: Deep Adaptive and Generative $K$-Free Community Detection on Attributed Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Community detection on attributed graphs with rich semantic and topological information offers great potential for real-world network analysis, especially user matching in online games.
- Proposes a Deep Adaptive and Generative model~(DAG) for community detection without specifying the prior K.
- Offline experiments on five public datasets and a real-world online mobile game dataset report higher performance for our DAG over the existing (SOTA) methods.
