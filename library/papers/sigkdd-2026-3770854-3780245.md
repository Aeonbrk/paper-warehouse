# LoFT-LLM: Low-Frequency Time-series Forecasting with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Time-series forecasting in real-world applications such as finance and energy often faces challenges due to limited training data and complex, noisy temporal dynamics.
- Proposes LoFT-LLM, a frequency-aware forecasting pipeline that integrates low-frequency learning with semantic calibration via a large language model (LLM).
- Experiments on financial and energy datasets demonstrate that LoFT-LLM outperforms strong baselines under both full-data and few-shot regimes, delivering accuracy, robustness, and interpretability.
