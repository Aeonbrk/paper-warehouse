# Towards Context-Aware Traffic Classification via Time-Wavelet Fusion Network

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Encrypted traffic classification occupies a role in cybersecurity and network management.
- Proposes TrafficScope, a time-wavelet fusion network based on Transformer to enhance the performance of encrypted traffic classification.
- Extensively evaluate TrafficScope compared with 7 baselines based on four groups of real-world traffic datasets, the results show that TrafficScope outperforms existing methods.
