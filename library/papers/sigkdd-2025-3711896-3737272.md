# UoMo: A Universal Model of Mobile Traffic Forecasting for Wireless Network Optimization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Mobile traffic forecasting allows operators to anticipate network dynamics and performance in advance, offering substantial potential for enhancing service quality and improving user experience.
- Proposes a Universal model for Mobile traffic forecasting (UoMo), aiming to handle diverse forecasting tasks of short/long-term predictions and distribution generation across multiple cities to support network planning and optimization.
- Evaluations on 9 real-world datasets demonstrate that UoMo outperforms current models in various forecasting tasks and zero/few-shot learning.
