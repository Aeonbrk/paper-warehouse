# FAT: Frequency-Aware Pretraining for Enhanced Time-Series Representation Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent advancements in time-series forecasting have highlighted the importance of frequency-domain modeling.
- Proposes FAT, a pretraining framework that learns generalizable Frequency-Aware Time-series representations through self-supervised learning.
- Experiments on 14 benchmark datasets across regression and classification tasks show that FAT consistently achieves performance while maintaining robustness across diverse backbone models, outperforming existing pretraining methods.
