# Vistar: Enhancing the Perception Capability of LLMs under Imprecise IMU-Text Alignment

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Introduces Vistar, a self-supervised framework for inertial measurement unit (IMU) signal perception designed for large language models (LLMs).
- During offline training, Vistar uses pretrained vision and language encoders as anchors to learn IMU encoders via hierarchical cross-modal contrastive learning, establishing both inter- and intra-sample alignment.
- The core idea of Vistar is to achieve accurate IMU signal perception through collaborations between offline cross-modal alignment and online retrieval-augmented generation.
