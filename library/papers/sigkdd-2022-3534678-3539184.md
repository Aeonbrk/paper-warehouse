# Crowdsourcing with Contextual Uncertainty

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies a crowdsourcing setting where need to infer the latent truth about a task given observed labels together with context in the form of a classifier score.
- Presents Theodon, a hierarchical non-parametric Bayesian model, developed and deployed at Meta, that captures both the prevalence of label categories and the accuracy of labelers as functions of the classifier score.
