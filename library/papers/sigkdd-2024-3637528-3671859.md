# Fast Computation of Kemeny’s Constant for Directed Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Kemeny's constant for random walks on a graph is defined as the mean hitting time from one node to another selected randomly according to the stationary distribution.
- Proposes two approximation algorithms for estimating Kemeny's constant on directed graphs with theoretical error guarantees.
- Numerical experiments on real-world networks report comparisons with our algorithms over baseline methods in terms of efficiency and accuracy.
