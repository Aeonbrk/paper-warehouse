# A Multi-Stage Structural Captioning Framework for Enhancing Chinese Image-to-Video Generation in Baidu

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The rapid advancement of video generation technology, particularly in the domain of image-to-video generation, is transforming both personal and industrial applications.
- Proposes a optimization framework for a multi-expert, structured video captioning model, specifically designed to enhance the performance of Chinese image-to-video tasks in Baidu's business scenarios.
- First, construct a structured video caption dataset by combining a large language model (LLM) with human annotations, and introduce a hallucination-aware adaptive GRPO algorithm.
