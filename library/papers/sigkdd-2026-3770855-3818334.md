# CanniUplift: A Holistic Framework for Mitigating Seller and Incentive Cannibalization in E-commerce Uplift Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Personalized incentive allocation is vital for e-commerce, where uplift modeling is the standard for estimating Individual Treatment Effects (ITE).
- Proposes CanniUplift, a unified framework to mitigate these dual-source cannibalization effects.
- Successfully deployed online, our framework achieved a 4.08% relative increase in platform-wide incremental GMV (ΔGMV) over the production baseline and improved ROI in online A/B tests, proving in driving global platform growth.
