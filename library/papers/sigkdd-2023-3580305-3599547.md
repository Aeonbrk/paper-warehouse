# When Rigidity Hurts: Soft Consistency Regularization for Probabilistic Hierarchical Time Series Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Probabilistic hierarchical time-series forecasting is an important variant of time-series forecasting, where the goal is to model and forecast multivariate time-series that have hierarchical relations.
- Close both these gaps and propose PROFHiT, a probabilistic hierarchical forecasting model that jointly models forecast distributions over the entire hierarchy.
- PROFHiT provides 41-88% better performance in accuracy and better calibration over a wide range of dataset consistency.
