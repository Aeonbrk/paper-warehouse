# Awaken the Giant: Activating LLMs via Deep Model Guidance for Boundary-aware Medication Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate and safe medication recommendations from Electronic Health Records (EHRs) are essential for clinical decision support.
- Proposes a boundary-aware medication recommendation framework (GiantMed) that activates the potential of LLM "giant" under deep model guidance.
- Experiments on two real-world EHR datasets demonstrate that GiantMed1 achieves accuracy while reducing DDI rates.
