# SafeImpute: Reliable Clinical Data Imputation via Conformal Selection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Clinical care often relies on key laboratory indicators, yet real-world patient visits are sparse and tests are ordered irregularly, leading to pervasive missingness.
- Proposes SafeImpute, a reliable imputation framework for irregular and sparse clinical longitudinal records.
- While many imputation methods improve average accuracy, they provide limited guidance on which imputed values are reliable enough for high-stakes downstream use.
