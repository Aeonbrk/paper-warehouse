# Reinforcement Learning with Scenario-Context Rollout in Portfolio Management

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- When economic structures and market dynamics shift, classic portfolio rebalancing algorithms often suffer from unstable and degraded performance.
- Explores reinforcement learning (RL) and propose Scenario-Context Rollout (SCR), a macroeconomics-guided feedback mechanism to produce a distribution of next-day joint returns under potential economic shocks.
- As a result, incorporating scenario-based rewards from rollouts introduces a reward-transition mismatch in temporal-difference (TD) learning, destabilizing RL critic training.
