# MSSRNet: Manipulating Sequential Style Representation for Unsupervised Text Style Transfer

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Unsupervised text style transfer task aims to rewrite a text into target style while preserving its main content.
- Additionally, an adversarial training framework integrated with teacher-student learning is introduced to enhance training stability and reduce the complexity of high-dimensional optimization.
- The results of our experiments demonstrate the efficacy of our method in terms of clearly improved style transfer accuracy and content preservation in both two-style transfer and multi-style transfer settings.
