# C-iVAE: Causal Structure-Aware Identifiable VAE for Tabular Data with Limited Environments

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Identifiable Variational Autoencoders (iVAEs) provide theoretical guarantees for decoupled representation learning, but their requirement for auxiliary environments grows linearly with latent dimension (O(dk)), which is often impractical in real-world scenarios.
- Proposes the Causal Structure-Aware Identifiable Variational Autoencoder (C-iVAE), extending the identifiability framework to static tabular data domains where causal graphs are often known or partially accessible.
- Experiments demonstrate C-iVAE's exceptional robustness in intervention generalization and DAG noise settings, achieving performance gains on real-world datasets.
