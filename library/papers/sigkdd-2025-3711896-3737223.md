# FoRAGe: High-CTR Food Image Synthesis with Retrieval-Augmented Diffusion Model

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- High Click-Through Rate (CTR) imagery has proven commercial value for food delivery platforms, driving a need for strategies to generate visually compelling images.
- Proposes FoRAGe, a high-CTR Food image Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline leveraging ControlNet based on Stable Diffusion.
- Despite advancements in diffusion models, inpainting new backgrounds does not guarantee high CTR, and fine-tuning diffusion models for this purpose is prohibitively expensive for the fast-paced online food delivery advertising sector.
