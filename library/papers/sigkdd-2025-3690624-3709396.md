# LinkSAGE: Optimizing Job Matching Using Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Presents LinkSAGE, an framework that integrates Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) into large-scale personalized job matching systems, designed to address the complex dynamics of LinkedIn's professional network.
- This methodology decouples the training of the GNN model from that of existing Deep Neural Network (DNN) models, eliminating the need for frequent GNN retraining while maintaining up-to-date graph signals in near real-time, allowing.
- The subsequent nearline inference system serves the GNN encoder within a real-world setting, reducing online latency and obviating the need for costly real-time GNN infrastructure.
