# Road Planning for Slums via Deep Reinforcement Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Millions of slum dwellers suffer from poor accessibility to urban services due to inadequate road infrastructure within slums, and road planning for slums is critical to the sustainable development of cities.
- Presents a deep reinforcement learning based approach to automatically layout roads for slums.
- Experiments on real-world slums in different countries verify the effectiveness of our model, which can improve accessibility by 14.3% against existing baseline methods.
