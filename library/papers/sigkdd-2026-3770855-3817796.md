# UniRank: End-to-End Domain-Specific Reranking of Hybrid Text-Image Candidates

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Reranking is a critical component in many information retrieval pipelines.
- Proposes UniRank, a VLM-based reranking framework that natively scores and orders hybrid text-image candidates without any modality conversion.
- Experiments on scientific literature retrieval and design patent search demonstrate that UniRank consistently outperforms baselines, improving Recall@1 by 8.9% and 7.3%, respectively.
