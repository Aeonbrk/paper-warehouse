# From GPS Points to Travel Patterns: Flexible and Semantic Trajectory Generation with LLMs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Urban trajectories play a crucial role in modeling urban dynamics and supporting various smart city applications.
- Proposes HTP, which Hierarchically generates Travel patterns first and then generates GPS Points by using large language models (LLMs), rather than directly generating GPS points.
- Experiments on two real-world datasets show that HTP outperforms the strongest baseline by an average of 29.78% in terms of generation quality.
