# ProtoMix: Augmenting Health Status Representation Learning via Prototype-based Mixup

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the widespread adoption of electronic health records (EHR) data, deep learning techniques have been broadly utilized for various health prediction tasks.
- Proposes a Prototype-based Mixup method, dubbed ProtoMix, which combines prior knowledge of intrinsic data features from subtype centroids (i.e., prototypes) to guide the synthesis of new samples.
- Experimental evaluations on three real-world datasets demonstrate the efficacy of ProtoMix.
