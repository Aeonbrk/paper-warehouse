# MLT-Dedup: Efficient Large-Scale Online Video Deduplication via Multi-Level Representations and Spatial-Temporal Matching

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The explosive growth of user-generated video content on online platforms is accompanied by the emergence of numerous near-duplicate videos—videos that are identical or highly similar but differ by partial edits.
- Proposes MLT-Dedup, an efficient large-scale online video deduplication framework with Multi-Level representations and spatial-Temporal matching.
- Experiments on a real-world large-scale platform demonstrate that MLT-Dedup reduces online repetition rates by 91% under 90% precision.
