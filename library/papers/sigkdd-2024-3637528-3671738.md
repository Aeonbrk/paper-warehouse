# PolygonGNN: Representation Learning for Polygonal Geometries with Heterogeneous Visibility Graph

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Polygon representation learning is essential for diverse applications, encompassing tasks such as shape coding, building pattern classification, and geographic question answering.
- Our study introduces a comprehensive framework specifically designed for learning representations of polygonal geometries, particularly multipolygons.
- Experiments on five real-world and synthetic datasets demonstrate its ability to capture informative representations for polygonal geometries.
