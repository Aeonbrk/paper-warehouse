# Bid2X: Revealing Dynamics of Bidding Environment in Online Advertising from A Foundation Model Lens

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes a bidding foundation model Bid2X to learn this fundamental function from data in various scenarios.
- In real-world applications, Bid2X increased GMV by 4.65% and ROI by 2.44% in online A/B tests, paving the way for the bidding foundation model in computational advertising.
