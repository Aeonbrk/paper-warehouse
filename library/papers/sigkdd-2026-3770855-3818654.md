# PCA-OS: A Planetary Climate Adaptation Operating System

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Predictive climate machine learning is increasingly good at forecasting hazards, but hazard maps alone do not decide what to do, where, when, for whom, and under which futures.
- Proposes PCA-OS (Planetary Climate Adaptation Operating System), a decision-support operating abstraction built on an intervention-aware global causal knowledge graph.
- Outline AdaptBench, an evaluation suite where systems can fail for inequitable or maladaptive recommendations despite high predictive accuracy.
