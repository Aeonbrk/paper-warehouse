# Offline Behavioral Data Selection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Behavioral cloning is a widely adopted approach for offline policy learning from expert demonstrations.
- Proposes a simple yet method, Stepwise Dual Ranking (SDR), which extracts a compact yet informative subset from large-scale offline behavioral datasets.
- Uncover the striking data saturation in offline behavioral data: policy performance rapidly saturates when trained on a small fraction of the dataset.
