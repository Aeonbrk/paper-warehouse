# DA^2-Unlearn: Dual-Adaptive Forget-Repair-Based Recommendation Unlearning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modern recommender systems increasingly face recommendation unlearning requests, where specific user-item interactions must be revoked due to interest drift, accidental feedback, data poisoning, or privacy concerns.
- Proposes a dual-adaptive forget-repair-based recommendation unlearning framework, DA2-Unlearn.
- Experiments on both real-world data with genuine revoking feedback and simulated datasets demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves better unlearning effectiveness than existing methods, and meanwhile maintains comparable recommendation utility.
