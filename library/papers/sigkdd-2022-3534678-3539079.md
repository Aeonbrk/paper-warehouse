# Representative Routes Discovery from Massive Trajectories

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies how to find the k most representative routes over large scale trajectory data, which is a fundamental operation that benefits various real-world applications, such as traffic monitoring and public transportation planning.
- Since each edge in a route may be close to several trajectories, further proposes a coverage-first algorithm to locate the edges with the greatest coverage gain in the solution route set.
- Finally, comprehensive experiments over two real-world datasets are conducted to verify the effectiveness and efficiency of our proposed algorithms.
