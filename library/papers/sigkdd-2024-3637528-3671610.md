# Hierarchical Knowledge Guided Fault Intensity Diagnosis of Complex Industrial Systems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Fault intensity diagnosis (FID) plays a role in monitoring and maintaining mechanical devices within complex industrial systems.
- Proposes a hierarchical knowledge guided fault intensity diagnosis framework (HKG) inspired by the tree of thought, which is amenable to any representation learning methods.
- Experiments are performed on four real-world datasets from different industrial domains (three cavitation datasets from SAMSON AG and one existing publicly) for FID, all showing results and outperform recent FID methods.
