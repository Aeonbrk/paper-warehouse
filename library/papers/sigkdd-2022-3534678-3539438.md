# Toward Learning Robust and Invariant Representations with Alignment Regularization and Data Augmentation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Data augmentation has been proven to be an technique for developing machine learning models that are robust to known classes of distributional shifts (e.g., rotations of images).
- Motivated by a proliferation of options of alignment regularizations, seek to evaluate the performances of several popular design choices along the dimensions of robustness and invariance, for which introduce a new test procedure.
- Our synthetic experiment results speak to the benefits of squared ℓ2 norm regularization.
