# Cross-Contrastive Clustering for Multimodal Attributed Graphs with Dual Graph Filtering

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multimodal Attributed Graphs (MMAGs) are an expressive data model for representing the complex interconnections among entities that associate attributes from multiple data modalities (text, images, etc.).
- Proposes the Dual Graph Filtering (DGF) scheme, which incorporates a feature-wise denoising component into node representation learning, thereby addressing the limitations of traditional graph filters adopted in the extant multi-view graph clustering.
- Our comprehensive experiments on eight benchmark MMAG datasets exhibit that DGF is able to outperform a wide range of baselines consistently and in terms of clustering quality measured against ground-truth labels.
