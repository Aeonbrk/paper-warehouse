# MicrobeQuest: A Context-Aware Multimodal Benchmark for Microbiology Information Extraction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- AI for Science (AI4S) is rapidly advancing scientific discovery, yet its progress critically depends on the availability of large-scale, high-quality structured scientific data and reliable evaluation benchmarks.
- Introduces MicrobeQuest, the first comprehensive multimodal benchmark for microbial information extraction tasks, comprising 11,877 expert-validated, context-aware query–response pairs.
- Although numerous IE models have been proposed, their effectiveness in microbiological settings remains difficult to assess due to the lack of a standardized, domain-specific evaluation benchmark.
