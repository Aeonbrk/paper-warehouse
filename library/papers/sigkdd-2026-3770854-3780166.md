# Stein-type Estimator Assisted by Dynamics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Estimating the equilibrium parameters of environmental systems is a fundamental task, yet it is often hampered by sparse and noisy sensor data.
- Introduces STEADY, an estimator that generalises Stein's paradox by integrating physical knowledge.
- Validate our claims on synthetic data and demonstrate STEADY's utility on the global Argo ocean float dataset, showing that it effectively filters noise to reveal the ''North Atlantic Warming Hole''.
