# EuraGovExam: A Multilingual Multimodal Benchmark from Real-World Civil Service Exams

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Presents EuraGovExam, a multilingual and multimodal benchmark sourced from real-world civil service examinations across five representative Eurasian regions: South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, India, and the European Union.
- This design demands that models perform layout-aware, cross-lingual reasoning directly from visual input.
- Evaluation results show that even vision-language models (VLMs) achieve only 86% accuracy, underscoring the benchmark's difficulty and its power to diagnose the limitations of current models.
