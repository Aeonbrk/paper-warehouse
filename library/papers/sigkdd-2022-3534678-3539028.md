# Precision CityShield Against Hazardous Chemicals Threats via Location Mining and Self-Supervised Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the unprecedented development of industrialization and urbanization, many hazardous chemicals have become an indispensable part of our daily life.
- Proposes a system named as CityShield to discover hidden HCLs and classify their risk levels based on trajectories of hazardous chemicals transportation vehicles.
- Experiments and case studies on two large-scale real-world datasets collected from Nantong demonstrated the effectiveness of the proposed system.
