# Contrastive Learning with Complex Heterogeneity

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the advent of big data across multiple high-impact applications, are often facing the challenge of complex heterogeneity.
- Proposes a unified heterogeneous learning framework, which combines both the weighted unsupervised contrastive loss and the weighted supervised contrastive loss to model multiple types of heterogeneity.
- Experimental results on real-world data sets demonstrate the effectiveness and the efficiency of the proposed framework modeling multiple types of heterogeneity.
