# Multi-View Clustering for Open Knowledge Base Canonicalization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Open information extraction (OIE) methods extract plenty of OIE triples from unstructured text, which compose large open knowledge bases (OKBs).
- Proposes CMVC, a unsupervised framework that leverages these two views of knowledge jointly for canonicalizing OKBs without the need of manually annotated labels.
- Reports the superiority of our framework through experiments on multiple real-world OKB data sets against methods.
