# Diffusion-Scheduled Denoising Autoencoders for Anomaly Detection in Tabular Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Anomaly detection in tabular data remains challenging due to complex feature interactions and the scarcity of anomalous examples.
- Proposes the Diffusion-Scheduled Denoising Autoencoder (DDAE), a framework that integrates diffusion-based noise scheduling and contrastive learning into the encoding process to improve anomaly detection.
- Our method outperforms in semi-supervised settings and achieves competitive results in unsupervised settings, improving PR-AUC by up to 65% (9%) and ROC-AUC by 16% (6%) over autoencoder (diffusion) model baselines.
