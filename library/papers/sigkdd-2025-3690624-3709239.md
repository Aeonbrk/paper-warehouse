# Augmented Contrastive Clustering with Uncertainty-Aware Prototyping for Time Series Test Time Adaptation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Test-time adaptation aims to adapt pre-trained deep neural networks using solely online unlabelled test data during inference.
- Proposes Augmented Contrastive Clustering with Uncertainty-aware Prototyping (ACCUP), a straightforward yet TTA method for time series data.
- Existing TTA methods, originally designed for visual tasks, may not effectively handle the complex temporal dynamics of real-world time series data, resulting in suboptimal adaptation performance.
