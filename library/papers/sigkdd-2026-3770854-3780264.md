# MergeRec: Model Merging for Data-Isolated Cross-Domain Sequential Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modern recommender systems trained on domain-specific data often struggle to generalize across multiple domains.
- Proposes a new framework, MergeRec, based on model merging under a new and realistic problem setting termed data-isolated cross-domain sequential recommendation, where raw user interaction data cannot be shared across domains.
- Compared to conventional model merging methods, MergeRec consistently achieves performance, with average improvements of up to 17.21% in Recall@10, highlighting the potential of model merging as a scalable and approach for building universal recommender.
