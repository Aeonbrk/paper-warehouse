# Incremental Cognitive Diagnosis for Intelligent Education

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Cognitive diagnosis, aiming at providing an approach to reveal the proficiency level of learners on knowledge concepts, plays an important role in intelligent education area and has recently received more and more attention.
- Proposes a framework, Incremental Cognitive Diagnosis (ICD), to tailor cognitive diagnosis into the online scenario of intelligent education.
- In the real scenario, the data is collected online, where learners, test items and interactions usually grow continuously, which can rarely meet the stationary condition.
