# Revisiting Graph Autoencoders as Implicit Contrastive Learners

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph autoencoders (GAEs) and graph contrastive learning (GCL) are two major paradigms for self-supervised representation learning on graphs, yet they are often studied in isolation and treated as fundamentally different approaches.
- Introduces a unified formulation that highlights contrastive view design as a central and previously less explored dimension in GAEs.
