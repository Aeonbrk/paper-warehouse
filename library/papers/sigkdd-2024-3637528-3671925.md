# Harm Mitigation in Recommender Systems under User Preference Dynamics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Considers a recommender system that takes into account the interplay between recommendations, the evolution of user interests, and harmful content.
- Model the impact of recommendations on user behavior, particularly the tendency to consume harmful content.
- Experiment on a semi-synthetic movie recommendation setting initialized with real data and observe that our policies outperform baselines at simultaneously maximizing CTR and mitigating harm.
