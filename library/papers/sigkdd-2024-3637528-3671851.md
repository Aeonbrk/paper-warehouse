# Knowledge Distillation with Perturbed Loss: From a Vanilla Teacher to a Proxy Teacher

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Knowledge distillation is a popular technique to transfer knowledge from a large teacher model to a small student model.
- Argue that such a learning objective is sub-optimal because there exists a discrepancy between the teacher's output distribution and the ground truth label distribution.
- Experiments on six public benchmark datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of PTLoss with teachers of different scales.
