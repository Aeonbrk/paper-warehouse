# Orchestrating Reasoning and Reaction: An Asynchronous Hierarchical Framework for LLM-driven Traffic Signal Control

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Network-wide coordinated Traffic Signal Control (TSC) is critical for enhancing urban mobility.
- Existing approaches face a fundamental trade-off: traditional Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) is often hindered by a myopic observational scope, while Large Language Model (LLM) agents are constrained by high inference costs and spatial-topological hallucinations.
- Evaluations on real-world datasets demonstrate that Astra consistently outperforms baseline methods, offering efficiency, robustness, and generalization across diverse urban scenarios.
