# DeMIC: Decentralized Meta In-Context Learning with Refiner-Guided Adaptation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In-Context Learning (ICL) empowers Large Language Models to adapt to tasks, presenting a solution for privacy-preserving distributed scenarios.
- Proposes DeMIC, a decentralized meta-learning framework designed to collaboratively optimize the intrinsic in-context adaptation mechanism under task heterogeneity and dynamic network topologies.
- Experiments demonstrate generalization of DeMIC on new tasks, improving accuracy by 11.76% and F1-score by 10.65% on average over all settings.
