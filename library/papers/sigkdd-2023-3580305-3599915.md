# Towards Equitable Assignment: Data-Driven Delivery Zone Partition at Last-mile Logistics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The popularity of online e-commerce has promoted the rapid development of last-mile logistics in recent years.
- First design a learning-based workload prediction model to estimate service time given a partition plan that consists of unseen courier-zone matching scenarios.
- Real-world deployment results at JD Logistics also verify the effectiveness of equitable-assignment aware delivery zone partition, with a 2.2% increase in service on-time rate compared to state-of-practice partition solutions.
