# UNITE: A Unified Framework for Accurate and Efficient Origin-Destination and Route Travel Time Estimation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Travel Time Estimation (TTE) on road networks is crucial for modern location-based services.
- Develops a Segment Travel Time Estimator (mSTE) that leverages the shared state embedding, builds a local spatio-temporal travel graph to capture traffic dynamics.
- Experiments on large-scale real-world datasets demonstrate that UNITE consistently outperforms methods in both accuracy and efficiency.
