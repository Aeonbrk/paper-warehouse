# INDUS-SDE: A Language Model for Scientific Content Curation and Discovery

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Scientific content curation from organizing, tagging, classifying, to connecting scientific knowledge is essential for accelerating discovery but cannot scale through manual expert effort alone.
- Presents INDUS-SDE, a language model designed to learn from expert-curated scientific content and extend curation capabilities at scale.
- Validate INDUS-SDE on NASA's Science Discovery Engine (SDE), a real-world platform aggregating scientific content across five NASA science divisions.
