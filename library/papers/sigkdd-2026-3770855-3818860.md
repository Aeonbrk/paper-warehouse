# Epistemic-Aware Vision-Language Foundation Model for Fetal Ultrasound Interpretation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent medical vision-language models have shown promise on tasks such as VQA, report generation, and anomaly detection.
- Proposes Salient Epistemic Disentanglement (SED), which injects an expert-curated bipartite graph into the model to decouple view-disease associations and to steer preference selection along clinically faithful steps via reinforcement learning.
- To train FetalMind at scale, curate FetalSigma-1M dataset, the first large-scale fetal ultrasound report corpus, comprising 20K reports from twelve medical centers, addressing the scarcity of domain data.
