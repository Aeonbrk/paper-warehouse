# Few-shot Learning for Trajectory-based Mobile Game Cheating Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the emerging of smartphones, mobile games have attracted billions of players and occupied most of the share for game companies.
- To handle such issues, in this paper introduce a mobile game cheating detection framework, namely FCDGame, to detect the cheats under the few-shot learning framework.
- Experiments on two real online games show that FCDGame achieves almost 10% improvements in detection accuracy with only few fine-tuned samples.
