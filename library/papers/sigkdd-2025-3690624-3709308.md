# Seeing the Unseen in Micro-Video Popularity Prediction: Self-Correlation Retrieval for Missing Modality Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Micro-video popularity prediction (MVPP) plays a crucial role in numerous real-world applications, including product marketing and recommendation systems.
- Proposes SCRAG, a Self-Correlation Retrieval-Augmented Generative framework designed to enhance missing-modality robustness in MVPP.
- Experiments conducted on three real-world datasets demonstrate that SCRAG consistently outperforms baselines, underscoring its effectiveness in handling incomplete modalities and improving the accuracy of micro-video popularity prediction.
