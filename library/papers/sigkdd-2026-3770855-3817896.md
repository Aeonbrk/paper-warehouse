# AI-Assisted Variance Reduction in Randomized Experiments

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Generative AI and large language models can produce realistic predictions of human behavior from rich, unstructured inputs with little to no task-specific training data.
- A benefit of this approach is a ''do no harm'' property whereby the adjusted estimator reverts to the unadjusted difference in means when predictions are uninformative.
- Reports these ideas in simulations and three empirical applications: a survey mega-study, an email marketing A/B test, and a large-scale technology platform experiment.
