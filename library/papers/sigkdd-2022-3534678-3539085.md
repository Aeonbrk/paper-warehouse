# Packet Representation Learning for Traffic Classification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the surging development of information technology, to provide a high quality of network services, there are increasing demands and challenges for network analysis.
- Proposes a framework to tackle the problem of packet representation learning for various traffic classification tasks.
- Reports evaluation showing the learned packet representation of our proposed framework can outperform the baseline methods on popular downstream classification tasks by a wide margin in both the close-world and open-world scenario.
