# Shift-Robust Molecular Relational Learning with Causal Substructure

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Molecular relational learning, whose goal is to predict the interaction behavior between molecular pairs, got a surge of interest in molecular sciences due to its wide range of applications.
- Proposes CMRL that is robust to the distributional shift in molecular relational learning by detecting the core substructure that is causally related to chemical reactions.
- Experiments on various tasks with real-world and synthetic datasets report higher performance for CMRL over baseline models.
