# Rethinking Generalization in Graphs: A Hierarchical Interaction Perspective for Generalist Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the increasing heterogeneity of social networks and online interaction systems, generalist graph anomaly detection (GAD) has become essential for identifying abnormal and fraudulent behaviors in complex environments.
- Proposes HIerarchical Interaction MOdeling for zero-shot generalist GAD (termed HIMO-GAD).
- Experiments on multiple real-world graph datasets demonstrate that HIMO-GAD consistently outperforms baselines in strict zero-shot settings, achieving up to a 10% improvement in key evaluation metrics and exhibiting strong generalization across heterogeneous graph domains.
