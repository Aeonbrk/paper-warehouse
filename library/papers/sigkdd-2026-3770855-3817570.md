# MKG-RAG-Bench: Benchmarking Retrieval in Multimodal Knowledge Graph–Augmented Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over knowledge graphs has emerged as a approach for grounding large language models, yet existing benchmarks largely overlook the challenges of retrieval in multimodal knowledge graph RAG (MKG-RAG).
- Introduces MKG-RAG-Bench, a cross-domain benchmark explicitly designed to evaluate retrieval in MKG-RAG.
