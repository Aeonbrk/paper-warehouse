# SAASBench: A synthetic antibody–antigen specificity benchmark

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate computational prediction of antibody-antigen binding affinity and specificity is critical for accelerating the design of next-generation therapeutics.
- SAASBench provides a framework for evaluating the model's ability to estimate the specificity of a candidate antibody in relevant settings.
- Existing antibody-antigen benchmarks largely focus on affinity prediction or docking accuracy on known binders, and therefore do not directly evaluate antibody specificity.
