# SQChip-EM: A Qiskit-Metal-Based Layout-to-EM Simulation Dataset for Superconducting Quantum Chips

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Translating superconducting quantum chip layouts into physically meaningful simulation records is a critical step in quantum hardware development, yet current datasets rarely capture the electromagnetic (EM) effects involved in this process.
- Proposes SQChip-EM, an open layout to EM simulation dataset based on Qiskit Metal that links parameterized layout designs to Ansys HFSS and Q3D simulation results.
