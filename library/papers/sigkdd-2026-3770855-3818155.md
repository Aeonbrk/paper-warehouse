# Adaptive Geometry Routing for Vision-Language Understanding

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Vision language models face a fundamental geometry trade-off: Euclidean representations excel at instance-level discrimination, while hyperbolic representations naturally encode semantic hierarchies.
- Introduces Adaptive Geometry Routing (AGR), a framework that addresses this via a four-phase training curriculum : (1) Isolation hyperbolic-only training stabilizes hierarchical structure.
- On ViT-B, AGR achieves 38.8% COCO T2I R@5 (+6.5pp over MERU), 64.7% Flickr30K I2T R@5 (+11.3pp), and 32.6% ImageNet accuracy (+9.3pp), demonstrating that phased curriculum training enables stable hybrid geometry learning for vision-language understanding.
