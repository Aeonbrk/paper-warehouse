# QuaMap: A Multi-Backend Benchmark Dataset for Quantum Circuit Mapping and Learning-Based Compiler Evaluation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Mapping quantum circuits to specific hardware backends is a crucial step in quantum program compilation, yet current benchmarks rarely capture the backend-aware effects of this process.
- Presents QuaMap, the first large-scale, open benchmark dataset that systematically links 75 representative quantum algorithms with over 210,000 hardware-aware transpiled circuits on 10 IBM Quantum devices.
