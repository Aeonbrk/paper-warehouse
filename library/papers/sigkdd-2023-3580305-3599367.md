# GMOCAT: A Graph-Enhanced Multi-Objective Method for Computerized Adaptive Testing

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Computerized Adaptive Testing (CAT) refers to an online system that adaptively selects the best-suited question for students with various abilities based on their historical response records.
- Proposes a Graph-Enhanced Multi-Objective method for CAT (GMOCAT).
- Firstly, three objectives, namely quality, diversity and novelty, are introduced into the Scalarized Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning framework of CAT.
