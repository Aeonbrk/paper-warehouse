# Deep-learning Causal Retrieval Optimization for Efficient e-commerce Distribution in Pinterest

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Pinterest is where people turn inspiration into action as users browse ideas, then take steps toward realization, often by discovering shoppable content.
- A deep multi-task model jointly predicts outcomes and uplift of multiple events, trained with a doubly-robust pseudo-outcome alongside calibrated outcome losses for stable, single-robust uplift learning.
- A linear-time offline replay is designed to select thresholds and forecast policy impact with extremely high consistency with online results.
