# NILMFormer: Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring that Accounts for Non-Stationarity

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Millions of smart meters have been deployed worldwide, collecting the total power consumed by individual households.
- Current (SotA) solutions for NILM are based on deep-learning (DL) and operate on subsequences of an entire household consumption reading.
- The non-stationary nature of real-world smart meter data leads to a drift in the data distribution within each segmented window, which affects model performance.
