# Force-Recovered Neural Operators for Coupled Structures with Test-Time Closure Calibration

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Coupled engineering systems, such as braced excavations and industrial hall frames, are governed by explicit interface constraints and uncertain closures, including stiffness, connector compliance, and model-form choices.
- Learning-based surrogates can violate kinematic compatibility or depend on unstable multiplier tuning.
- On a full-scale industrial-hall benchmark, transfer initialization reduces displacement wRMSE from 108.98 to 0.49 mm and support relative-displacement tail error from Δp99 = 337.48 to 0.918 mm relative to scratch training.
