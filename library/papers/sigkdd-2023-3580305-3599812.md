# ECGGAN: A Framework for Effective and Interpretable Electrocardiogram Anomaly Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Heart is the most important organ of the human body, and Electrocardiogram (ECG) is an essential tool for clinical monitoring of heart health and detecting cardiovascular diseases.
- Proposes ECGGAN, a reconstruction-based ECG anomaly detection framework.
- Experimental results on two real-world datasets and their mixed-set confirm that our method achieves performance than baselines in terms of precision, recall, F1-score, and AUC.
