# Orthogonality Matters: Invariant Time Series Representation for Out-of-distribution Classification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Previous works for time series classification tend to assume that both the training and testing sets originate from the same distribution.
- Introduces a model called Invariant Time Series Representation (ITSR).
- The results on four real-world datasets underscore the superiority of ITSR over methods and demonstrate the critical role of maintaining orthogonality between invariant and relevant features.
