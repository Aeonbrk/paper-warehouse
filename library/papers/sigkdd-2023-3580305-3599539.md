# User-Regulation Deconfounded Conversational Recommender System with Bandit Feedback

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent conversational recommender systems (CRSs) have achieved considerable success on addressing the cold-start problem.
- To better model the relations and understand such bias, view CRSs from a causal perspective and introduce a structural causal model (SCM) that identifies the confounding effect of key-term-level user preference.
- Inspired by our causal view, devise an online backdoor adjustment approximation to alleviate the confounding effect when making item recommendations.
