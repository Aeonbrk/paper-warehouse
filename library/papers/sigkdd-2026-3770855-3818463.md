# Pinterest Canvas: Large-Scale Image Generation at Pinterest

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While recent image generation models demonstrate a ability to handle a wide variety of image generation tasks, this flexibility makes them hard to control via prompting or simple inference adaptation alone, rendering them unsuitable.
- Introduces Pinterest Canvas, our large-scale image generation system built to support image editing and enhancement use cases at Pinterest.
- Online A/B experiments demonstrate that our enhanced images receive a 18.0% and 12.5% engagement lift, respectively, and comparisons with human raters further validate that our models outperform third-party models on these tasks.
