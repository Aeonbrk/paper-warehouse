# Efficient Distributed Approximate k-Nearest Neighbor Graph Construction by Multiway Random Division Forest

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- K-nearest neighbor graphs, shortly k-NN graphs, are widely used in many data mining applications like recommendation, information retrieval, and similarity search.
- Proposes MRDF (Multiway Random Division Forest), a scalable distributed algorithm that constructs highly accurate k-NN graph from numerous high-dimensional vectors quickly.
- Experimental results on real-world datasets show that MRDF outperforms the distributed algorithms with up to 7.6 times faster speed and up to 56%p better accuracy than the second best results.
