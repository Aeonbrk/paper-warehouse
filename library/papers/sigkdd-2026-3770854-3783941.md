# Data-Centric Dual-Layer Adversarial Learning for Fraud Detection under Label Delay and Concept Drift

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Banks often uncover new fraud cases by tracing connections from previously confirmed ones.
- Proposes a Data-Centric Dual-Layer Adversarial Framework (DDAF) that jointly addresses LD and CD by improving data robustness in a backbone-agnostic way.
- On public datasets, DDAF improves AUC by 3.4 points and F1 by 3.5 points over strong baselines.
