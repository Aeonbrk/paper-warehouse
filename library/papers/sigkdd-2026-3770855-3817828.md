# Node4All: Learning Node Representation Beyond Datasets

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Node representation learning has advanced rapidly, yet most existing methods rely on per-dataset training and hyperparameter tuning.
- Proposes a self-supervised learning based on a series of synthetic graphs.
- This dataset-specific optimization comes from the difficulty of designing reusable graph models that generalize across diverse graph datasets.
