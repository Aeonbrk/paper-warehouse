# End-to-End Inventory Prediction and Contract Allocation for Guaranteed Delivery Advertising

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Guaranteed Delivery (GD) advertising plays an essential part in e-commerce marketing, where the ad publisher signs contracts with advertisers in advance by delivery of advertising impressions to fulfill targeting requirements for advertisers.
- Proposes the Neural Lagrangian Selling (NLS) model to jointly predict the impression inventory and optimize the contract allocation of advertising impressions with a unified learning objective.
- Previous research on GD advertising mainly focused on online serving yet overlooked the importance of contract allocation at the GD selling stage.
