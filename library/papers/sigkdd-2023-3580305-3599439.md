# Multi-Grained Multimodal Interaction Network for Entity Linking

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multimodal entity linking (MEL) task, which aims at resolving ambiguous mentions to a multimodal knowledge graph, has attracted wide attention in recent years.
- Proposes a Multi-GraIned Multimodal InteraCtion Network (MIMIC) framework for solving the MEL task.
- Experimental results on three public benchmark datasets demonstrate that our solution outperforms various baselines, and ablation studies verify the effectiveness of designed modules.
