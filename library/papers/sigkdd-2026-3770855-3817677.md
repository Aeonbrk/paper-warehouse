# Explaining Black-Box Language Models: Learning to Optimize Linguistically-Structured Word Subsets

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- As deep language models (DLMs) are increasingly deployed in high-stakes domains such as healthcare, understanding their decision rationale becomes paramount for ensuring trust, safety, and accountability.
- Proposes a method that explains predictions of DLMs by selecting a small, informative subset of input words.
- To enhance interpretability and align with human linguistic intuition, integrate graph-structured knowledge into this selection process, supporting coherent subsets that result in explanations both highly informative and cognitively meaningful to end-users.
