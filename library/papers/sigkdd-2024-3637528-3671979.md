# CASA: Clustered Federated Learning with Asynchronous Clients

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Clustered Federated Learning (CFL) is an emerging paradigm to extract insights from data on IoT devices.
- Through iterative client clustering and model aggregation, CFL adeptly manages data heterogeneity, ensures privacy, and delivers personalized models to heterogeneous devices.
- Evaluations on standard benchmarks show that CASA outperforms representative baselines in model accuracy and achieves 2.28-6.49× higher convergence speed.
