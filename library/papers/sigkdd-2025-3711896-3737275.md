# Aligning and Balancing ID and Multimodal Representations for Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large-scale recommendation systems mainly rely on sparse ID features, struggling with data sparsity.
- Comprehensively investigate the two problems and proposes a framework named AB-Rec to align and balance ID and multimodal representations learning for recommendation.
- Finally, conduct offline experiments on four datasets and an A/B test on an online video platform, demonstrating the effectiveness and scalability of our proposed method.
