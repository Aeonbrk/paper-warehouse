# HiBench: Benchmarking LLMs Capability on Hierarchical Structure Reasoning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Structure reasoning is a fundamental capability of large language models (LLMs), enabling them to reason about structured commonsense and answer multi-hop questions.
- Proposes HiBench, the first framework designed to systematically benchmark the hierarchical reasoning capabilities of LLMs from initial structure generation to final proficiency assessment.
- It also plays a key role in various real-world tasks, such as information extraction and decision-making.
