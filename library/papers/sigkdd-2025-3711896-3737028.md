# LitFM: A Retrieval Augmented Structure-aware Foundation Model For Citation Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the advent of large language models (LLMs), managing scientific literature via LLMs has become a direction of research.
- Proposes LitFM, the first literature foundation model designed for a wide variety of practical downstream tasks on domain-specific literature, with a focus on citation information.
- Introduces new large-scale literature citation benchmark datasets on three academic fields, featuring sentence-level citation information and local context.
