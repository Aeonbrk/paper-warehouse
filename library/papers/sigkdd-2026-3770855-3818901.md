# DSPR: Dual-Stream Physics-Residual Networks for Trustworthy Industrial Time Series Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate forecasting of industrial time series requires balancing predictive accuracy with physical plausibility under non-stationary operating conditions.
- Proposes DSPR (Dual-Stream Physics-Residual Networks), a forecasting framework that explicitly decouples stable temporal patterns from the complex residual dynamics.
- Existing data-driven models often achieve strong statistical performance but struggle to respect the dynamic interaction structures and transport delays inherent in real-world systems.
