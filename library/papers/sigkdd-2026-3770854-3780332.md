# Mixture of Meta-Policies for Cross-Environment Meta-Reinforcement Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Meta-Reinforcement Learning (Meta-RL) aims to enable agents to rapidly adapt to new tasks by leveraging prior experience from related ones.
- Proposes Mixture of Meta-Policies (MoMP), a modular and scalable Meta-RL framework designed for knowledge transfer across structurally heterogeneous environments.
- Existing approaches typically assume consistent state and action spaces across tasks, limiting the ability of Meta-RL methods to generalize to environments with diverse morphologies, dynamics, and reward structures, common in real-world applications.
