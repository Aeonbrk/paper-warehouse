# Predicting Information Pathways Across Online Communities

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The problem of community-level information pathway prediction (CLIPP) aims at predicting the transmission trajectory of content across online communities.
- Analyzes these datasets to construct community influence graphs (CIGs) and develop a dynamic graph frame- work, INPAC (Information Pathway Across Online Communities).
- Addresses CLIPP by collecting large-scale, multi-modal datasets to examine the diffusion of online YouTube videos on Reddit.
