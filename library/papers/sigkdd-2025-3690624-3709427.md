## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In the e-commerce domain, the accurate extraction of attribute-value pairs (e.g., Brand: Apple) from product titles and user search queries is crucial for enhancing search and recommendation systems.
- Introduces GenToC, a model designed for training directly with partially-labeled data, eliminating the necessity for a fully annotated dataset.
