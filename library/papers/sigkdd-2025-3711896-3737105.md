# Revisiting Clustering of Neural Bandits: Selective Reinitialization for Mitigating Loss of Plasticity

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Clustering of Bandits (CB) methods enhance sequential decision-making by grouping bandits into clusters based on similarity and incorporating cluster-level contextual information, demonstrating effectiveness and adaptability in applications like personalized streaming recommendations.
- Proposes Selective Reinitialization (SeRe), a bandit learning framework that dynamically preserves the adaptability of CNB algorithms in evolving environments.
- Experiments on six real-world recommendation datasets demonstrate that SeRe-enhanced CNB algorithms can effectively mitigate the loss of plasticity with lower regrets, improving adaptability and robustness in dynamic settings.
