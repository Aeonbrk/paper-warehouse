# EdgeWatch: Collaborative Investigation of Data Integrity at the Edge based on Blockchain

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Mobile edge computing (MEC) offers the infrastructure for improving data caching performance structurally by deploying edge servers at the network edge within users' close geographic proximity.
- Meanwhile, EDI (edge data integrity) investigation must be performed by edge servers collaboratively at the edge to avoid excessive backhaul network traffic.
- The results demonstrate the high precision, efficiency, and robustness of EdgeWatch.
