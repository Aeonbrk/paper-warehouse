# Personalizing Marketplace Policies with Competing Objectives and Constrained Experiments: Evidence from a Job Marketplace

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Two-sided marketplaces connect distinct user groups whose interests often conflict---improving outcomes on one side could degrade the other side's experience.
- Deploy an integrated framework for personalizing free-value thresholds---a policy governing the scope of complimentary services for job listings---across a two-sided job marketplace connecting millions of employers and job seekers.
- Presents production deployment, where post-launch data confirms both extrapolation accuracy and guardrail compliance.
