# ReCDA: Concept Drift Adaptation with Representation Enhancement for Network Intrusion Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The deployment of learning-based models to detect malicious activities in network traffic flows is challenged by concept drift.
- Proposes ReCDA, a Concept Drift Adaptation method with Representation enhancement, which consists of a self-supervised representation enhancement stage and a weakly-supervised classifier tuning stage.
- Conduct comprehensive evaluations on several benchmark datasets under varying degrees of concept drift.
