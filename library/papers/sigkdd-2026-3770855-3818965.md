# LLMSynthor: Macro-Aligned Micro-Records Synthesis with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Macro-aligned micro-records are essential for simulations in social science and urban studies.
- Proposes LLMSynthor, a framework that transforms a pre-trained LLM into a macro-aware simulator capable of synthesizing realistic micro-records aligned with given macro-statistics.
- While Large Language Models (LLMs) can generate realistic micro-records using rich real-world priors learned from vast corpora, naive record-by-record sampling is inefficient and fails to enforce alignment with target macro-statistics.
