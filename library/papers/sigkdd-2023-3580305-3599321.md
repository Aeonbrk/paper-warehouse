# Efficient and Effective Edge-wise Graph Representation Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph representation learning (GRL) is a tool for graph analysis, which has gained massive attention from both academia and industry due to its performance in various real-world applications.
- Moreover, to our knowledge, there is a paucity of research devoted to edge representation learning.
- Despite such methods can be used to derive edge representations by regarding edges as nodes, they suffer from sub-par result utility in practical edge-wise applications, such as financial fraud detection and review spam combating.
