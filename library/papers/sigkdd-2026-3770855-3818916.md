# StageGuard: Physiologically Constrained Sleep Staging

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Automated sleep staging is increasingly used in large-scale studies and downstream scientific analyses to derive sleep-architecture endpoints, including total sleep time, REM latency, sleep efficiency, and bout-duration statistics.
- Proposes StageGuard, a plug-and-play, backbone-agnostic structured-inference framework that wraps any neural sleep-staging backbone with physiology-informed priors.
- Deep learning models achieve epoch-level accuracy approaching inter-rater agreement, yet often produce hypnograms that violate physiological invariants, such as rare state transitions (e.g., direct Wake -> REM) or excessively fragmented sequences.
