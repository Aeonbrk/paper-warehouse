# BTTackler: A Diagnosis-based Framework for Efficient Deep Learning Hyperparameter Optimization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Hyperparameter optimization (HPO) is known to be costly in deep learning, especially when leveraging automated approaches.
- Proposes Bad Trial Tackler (BTTackler), a HPO framework that introduces training diagnosis to identify training problems automatically and hence tackles bad trials.
- Many trials may encounter severe training problems, such as vanishing gradients and insufficient convergence.
