# Fast and Accurate Online Coupled Matrix-Tensor Factorization via Frequency Regularization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- How can efficiently and accurately factorize multi-source data in dynamic and real-time environments?
- Proposes FOCAL (Frequency-regularized Online Coupled Approximation for Low-rank factorization), an efficient CP decomposition method designed to enhance online coupled matrix-tensor factorization.
- Furthermore, FOCAL integrates frequency regularization into an online CMTF framework, which mitigates overfitting and improves accuracy.
