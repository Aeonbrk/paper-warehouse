# Truss-based Why-not Community Search

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Investigates a new problem of truss-based why-not community search.
- Prove that the truss-based why-not community search problem is NP-hard and propose two efficient heuristic algorithms: the expansion-based algorithm and the simulation-based algorithm.
- Experiments on real-world graphs demonstrate the efficiency and effectiveness of our proposed methods and optimizations.
