# Adaptive Domain Inference Attack with Concept Hierarchy

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With increasingly deployed deep neural networks in sensitive application domains, such as healthcare and security, it's essential to understand what kind of sensitive information can be inferred from these models.
- The ADI method utilizes the concept hierarchy extracted from the public and private datasets that the attacker can access and applies a algorithm to adaptively tune the likelihood of leaf concepts in the hierarchy.
