# Macro Graph of Experts for Billion-Scale Multi-Task Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph-based multi-task learning at billion-scale presents a challenge, as different tasks correspond to distinct billion-scale graphs.
- Introduces the Macro Graph of Experts (MGOE) framework, the first approach capable of leveraging macro graph embeddings to capture task-specific macro features while modeling the correlations between task-specific experts.
- Offline experiments conducted on three public benchmark datasets demonstrate its superiority over multi-task learning methods, establishing MGOE as a breakthrough in multi-task graph-based recommendation.
