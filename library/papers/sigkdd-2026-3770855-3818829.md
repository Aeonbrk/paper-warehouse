# Completing Multimodal Biomedical Knowledge Graphs with Schema-Aware Modality Imputation and Alignment

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Biomedical knowledge graphs (BKGs) integrate heterogeneous biomedical entities and relations and have become a key abstraction for tasks such as drug repurposing.
- Proposes BioMICA, a multimodal biomedical KGC method with schema-aware generative modality imputation and cross-modal alignment.
- To bridge these gaps, first augment two existing BKGs with textual and biomedical modalities to build two multimodal BKGs, and derive three benchmark datasets for multimodal biomedical KGC.
