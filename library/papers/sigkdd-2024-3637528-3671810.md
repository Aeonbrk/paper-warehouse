# LogParser-LLM: Advancing Efficient Log Parsing with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Logs are ubiquitous digital footprints, playing an indispensable role in system diagnostics, security analysis, and performance optimization.
- These results demonstrate the method's high efficiency and accuracy, outperforming current log parsers, including pattern-based, neural network-based, and existing LLM-enhanced approaches.
- In evaluations on the LogPub benchmark, involving an average of 3.6 million logs per dataset across 14 datasets, our LogParser-LLM requires only 272.5 LLM invocations on average, achieving a 90.6% F1 score for grouping.
