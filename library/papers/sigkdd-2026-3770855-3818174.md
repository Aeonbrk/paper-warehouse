# Beyond One-Size-Fits-All: Adaptive Subgraph Denoising for Zero-Shot Graph Learning with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph-based tasks in the zero-shot setting remain a challenge due to data scarcity and the inability of traditional Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) to generalize to unseen domains or label spaces.
- Proposes SSR-RL, a two-stage reinforcement learning framework that explicitly regulates sampling and selection operations within the proposed SSR pipeline designed for adaptive subgraph denoising.
