# Engagement-Driven Content Generation with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large Language Models (LLMs) demonstrate persuasive capabilities in one-on-one interactions, but their influence within social networks, where interconnected users and complex opinion dynamics pose unique challenges, remains underexplored.
- Proposes a pipeline using reinforcement learning with simulated feedback, where the network's response to LLM-generated content (i.e., the reward) is simulated through a formal engagement model.
