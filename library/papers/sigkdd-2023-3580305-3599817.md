# Evolve Path Tracer: Early Detection of Malicious Addresses in Cryptocurrency

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the boom of cryptocurrency and its concomitant financial risk concerns, detecting fraudulent behaviors and associated malicious addresses has been drawing research effort.
- Presents Evolve Path Tracer, which consists of Evolve Path Encoder LSTM, Evolve Path Graph GCN, and Hierarchical Survival Predictor.
- Investigates the effectiveness and generalizability of Evolve Path Tracer on three real-world malicious address datasets.
