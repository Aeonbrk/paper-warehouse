# Conversational Dueling Bandits in Generalized Linear Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Conversational recommendation systems elicit user preferences by interacting with users to obtain their feedback on recommended commodities.
- Introduces relative feedback-based conversations into conversational recommendation systems through the integration of dueling bandits in generalized linear models (GLM) and proposes a conversational dueling bandit algorithm called ConDuel.
- Such systems utilize a multi-armed bandit framework to learn user preferences in an online manner and have received great success in recent years.
