# AHA: Scalable Alternative History Analysis for Operational Timeseries Applications

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Many operational systems collect high-dimensional timeseries data about users/systems on key performance metrics.
- Designs and implement a system, called AHA (Alternative History Analytics), that overcomes both challenges to provide cost efficiency and fidelity for high-dimensional data.
- Reports that AHA provides 100% accuracy for a broad range of downstream tasks and up to 85x lower total cost of ownership (i.e., compute + storage) compared to conventional methods.
