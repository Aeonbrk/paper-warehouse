# Comprehensive Fair Meta-learned Recommender System

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In recommender systems, one common challenge is the cold-start problem, where interactions are very limited for fresh users in the systems.
- Proposes a comprehensive fair meta-learning framework, named CLOVER, for ensuring the fairness of meta-learned recommendation models.
- Reports the effectiveness of CLOVER on the representative meta-learned user preference estimator on three real-world data sets.
