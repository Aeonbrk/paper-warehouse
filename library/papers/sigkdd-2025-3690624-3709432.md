# Disclosing Actual Controller based on Equity Knowledge Graph Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Disclosing Actual Controllers (ACs) of a company has been the basis for financial risk governance.
- Proposes an AC disclosure method based on Equity Knowledge Graph Learning (EKGL).
- Experiments and a case study on the EKG constructed from real datasets demonstrate that EKGL outperforms baselines by achieving 0.33 improvement in F1 score and reducing time cost.
