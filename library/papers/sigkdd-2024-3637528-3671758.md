# Where Have You Been? A Study of Privacy Risk for Point-of-Interest Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- As location-based services (LBS) have grown in popularity, more human mobility data has been collected.
- The collected data can be used to build machine learning (ML) models for LBS to enhance their performance and improve overall experience for users.
- Our experimental evaluation using two real-world mobility datasets demonstrates that current POI recommendation models are vulnerable to our attacks.
