# Beyond Fixed Variables: Expanding-variate Time Series Forecasting via Flat Scheme and Spatio-temporal Focal Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multivariate Time Series Forecasting (MTSF) has long been a key research focus.
- Introduces a Spatio-temporal Focal Learning strategy that incorporates a negative filter to resolve potential conflicts between contrastive learning and graph representation.
- To evaluate the effectiveness of STEV, benchmark EVTSF performance on three real-world datasets from various domains and compare it against three potential solutions employing (SOTA) MTSF models tailored for EVSTF.
