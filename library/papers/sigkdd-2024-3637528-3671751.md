# ImputeFormer: Low Rankness-Induced Transformers for Generalizable Spatiotemporal Imputation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Missing data is a pervasive issue in both scientific and engineering tasks, especially for the modeling of spatiotemporal data.
- Empirical results provide strong conviction that incorporating time series primitives, such as low-rankness, can substantially facilitate the development of a generalizable model to approach a wide range of spatiotemporal imputation problems.
- Reports its superiority in terms of accuracy, efficiency, and versatility in heterogeneous datasets, including traffic flow, solar energy, smart meters, and air quality.
