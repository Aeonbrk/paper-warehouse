# Diffusion-based Spatio-temporal Interpolation with Dynamic Sensor Sets

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Tackle spatio-temporal interpolation for virtual sensors in sparse, partially observed, and dynamically changing networks.
- Introduces DynaSTI, a diffusion-based generative framework that is fully inductive to unseen locations, trains directly on incomplete observations, and remains without retraining when sensor networks change with time.
- Our contributions are threefold: (i) a unified conditioning strategy that yields calibrated predictive distributions and robust performance under severe input-sensor dropout.
