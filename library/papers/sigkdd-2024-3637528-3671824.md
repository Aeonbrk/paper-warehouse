# Popularity-Aware Alignment and Contrast for Mitigating Popularity Bias

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Collaborative Filtering~(CF) typically suffers from the challenge of popularity bias due to the uneven distribution of items in real-world datasets.
- Conduct an empirical analysis of popularity bias and propose Popularity-Aware Alignment and Contrast (PAAC) to address two challenges.
- This bias leads to a accuracy gap between popular and unpopular items.
