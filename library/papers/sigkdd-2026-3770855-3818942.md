# MACReD: A Multi-Agent Collaborative Reasoning Framework for Reaction Diagram Parsing

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Parsing chemical reaction diagrams from scientific literature is challenging due to heterogeneous layouts, intertwined visual elements, and difficulty in integrating visual recognition with chemically consistent reasoning.
- Proposes MACReD, a hierarchical multi-agent framework that coordinates specialized agents for molecular perception, arrow understanding, text extraction, and reaction reconstruction within a unified VLM-guided architecture.
- Experiments on the RxnScribe benchmark show that MACReD achieves F1 performance under both Hard and Soft Match criteria, reaching 75.2% and 84.6%, respectively.
