# EHRBench: An Automated and Reliable EHR-based Benchmark for Clinical Decision Making with LLMs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Clinical decision-making (CDM) is central to real-world clinical workflows, where clinicians infer diagnoses, select treatments, or anticipate future health outcomes under incomplete evidence.
- Introduces EHRBench, an automated and reliable EHR-grounded benchmark for evaluating LLM-based clinical decision-making at scale.
- LLMs are increasingly used to support these decisions due to strong language capabilities, broad biomedical knowledge, and efficiency, yet the reliability of LLMs on real-world clinical decision tasks remains insufficiently understood.
