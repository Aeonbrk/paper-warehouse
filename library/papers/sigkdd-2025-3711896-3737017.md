# Learn to Refine: Synergistic Multi-Agent Path Optimization for Lifelong Conflict-Free Navigation of Autonomous Vehicles

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Lifelong Multi-Agent Path Finding (LMAPF) focuses on planning conflict-free paths for agents, like autonomous vehicles, that are continuously assigned new tasks.
- Proposes a Encoder-Decoder based neural network to learn a collaborative optimization policy through multi-agent reinforcement learning.
- In addition, leverage dual transformations to augment trajectory samples during online learning, enhancing both the sample utilization rate and overall learning stability.
