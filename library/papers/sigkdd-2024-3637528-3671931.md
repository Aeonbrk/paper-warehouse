# Large Language Models meet Collaborative Filtering: An Efficient All-round LLM-based Recommender System

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Collaborative filtering recommender systems (CF-RecSys) have shown successive results in enhancing the user experience on social media and e-commerce platforms.
- This approach yields two advantages: (1) model-agnostic, allowing for integration with various existing CF-RecSys, and (2) efficiency, eliminating the fine-tuning typically required for LLM-based recommenders.
- Our experiments on various real-world datasets report higher performance for A-LLMRec in various scenarios, including cold/warm, few-shot, cold user, and cross-domain scenarios.
