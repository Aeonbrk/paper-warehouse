# Disagreement-Aware Subgraph Federated Learning via Uncertainty-Guided Local-Global Alignment

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Subgraph federated learning (subgraph FL) enables collaborative graph neural network training without sharing raw graph data, but suffers from severe Non-IID distributions and structural fragmentation.
- Proposes FedDUA, a disagreement-aware and uncertainty-guided framework for subgraph FL.
- Experiments on six real-world datasets demonstrate that FedDUA consistently outperforms the subgraph FL methods across varying numbers of clients.
