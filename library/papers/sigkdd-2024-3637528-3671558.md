# TrajRecovery: An Efficient Vehicle Trajectory Recovery Framework based on Urban-Scale Traffic Camera Records

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate vehicle trajectory recovery enables providing indispensable data foundations in intelligent urban transportation.
- Existing methods face two challenges: i) the inability to process city-wide vehicle trajectories, and ii) the dependence on a substantial amount of accurate GPS trajectories for model training, leading to poor generalization ability.
