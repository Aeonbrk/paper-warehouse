# TH-Bench: Evaluating Evading Attacks via Humanizing AI Text on Machine-Generated Text Detectors

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- As Large Language Models (LLMs) advance, Machine-Generated Texts (MGTs) have become increasingly fluent, high-quality, and informative.
- Unfortunately, existing attacks generally lack a unified and comprehensive evaluation framework, as they are assessed using different experimental settings, model architectures, and datasets.
- Introduces the Text-Humanization Benchmark (TH-Bench), the first comprehensive benchmark to evaluate evading attacks against MGT detectors.
