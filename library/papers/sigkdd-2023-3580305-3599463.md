# Pattern Expansion and Consolidation on Evolving Graphs for Continual Traffic Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spatiotemporal graph convolutional networks are becoming popular in the field of traffic flow prediction and improve prediction accuracy.
- And proposes an efficient and continual learning framework to achieve continuous traffic flow prediction without the access to historical graph data, namely Pattern Expansion and Consolidation based on Pattern Matching based (PECPM).
- Construct experiments on real-world traffic datasets to demonstrate the competitive performance, efficiency, and strong generalization ability of PECPM.
