# AlzheimerODE: A Knowledge-Guided Generative Model for Alzheimer’s Disease Progression

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurately modeling the progression of Alzheimer's disease (AD) is essential for understanding disease mechanisms and optimizing therapeutic windows, yet it is impeded by the sparse, irregular, and heterogeneous nature of clinical longitudinal data.
- Presents AlzheimerODE, a knowledge-guided generative framework that learns structured disease dynamics as a reaction–diffusion flow in continuous time.
- Empirical evaluations on longitudinal neuroimaging datasets demonstrate that our method consistently outperforms baselines across trajectory accuracy, temporal alignment, spatial pattern consistency, and distributional fidelity.
