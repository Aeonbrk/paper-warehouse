# Taming the Domain Shift in Multi-source Learning for Energy Disaggregation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM) is a cost-energy disaggregation means to estimate the energy consumption of individual appliances from a central load reading.
- Learning-based methods are the new trends in NILM implementations but require large labeled data to work properly at end-user premises.
- Conduct experiments on three real-world residential energy datasets to evaluate the effectiveness of HLD-MDAN, showing that it is to other methods in single-source and multi-source learning scenarios.
