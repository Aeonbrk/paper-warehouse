# InfRL: Inference-time Reinforcement Learning for Research Idea Optimization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large language models (LLMs) possess latent knowledge yet remain largely static at inference.
- Formally model iterative research idea optimization as a finite-horizon Markov Decision Process and propose InfRL (Inference-time Reinforcement Learning), a framework designed for policy improvement at inference without updating model weights.
- The code and the dataset use are provided at: https://github.com/amir-hassan25/InfRL.
