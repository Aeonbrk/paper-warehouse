# Leveraging Label Distributions as Anchors to Enhance Video Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In video recommendation systems, accurately predicting watch time is crucial for enhancing user engagement and retention.
- Proposes LDA, a prediction paradigm that optimizes instance representations by explicitly leveraging label distributions as anchors within the model, enabling more accurate and robust predictions.
- Offline experiments on two datasets and large-scale online A/B testing on a short-video platform with over 300 million DAUs demonstrate the consistent superiority of LDA in watch time prediction.
