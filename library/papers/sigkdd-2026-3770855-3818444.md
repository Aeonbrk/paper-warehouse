# MatchLM2Lite: A Scalable MLLM-to-Lite Framework for Reproduced Content Identification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Content moderation is critical for online video platforms to ensure content safety, protect creators, and sustain positive user experiences.
- Presents MatchLM2Lite, a real-time, production-grade reproduced content identification (RCI) system that leverages the understanding of a multimodal large language model (MLLM) distilled into a small and fast-inference model.
- This design allows MatchLite to deliver low-latency, high-throughput inference on video pairs while preserving much of MatchLM's accuracy, making it suitable for integration into real-time recommendation systems.
