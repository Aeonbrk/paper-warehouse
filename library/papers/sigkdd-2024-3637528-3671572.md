# Controllable Multi-Behavior Recommendation for In-Game Skins with Large Sequential Model

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online games often house virtual shops where players can acquire character skins.
- To overcome these limitations, utilize the pretraining capabilities of Large Sequential Models (LSMs) coupled with a stimulus prompt mechanism and build a controllable multi-behavior recommendation (CMBR) model.
- Through offline experiments and online A/B tests, validate our method outperforms baseline models, exhibiting about a tenfold improvement on various metrics during the offline test.
