# Reliable Confidence Intervals for Information Retrieval Evaluation using Generative A.I.

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The traditional evaluation of information retrieval (IR) systems is generally very costly as it requires manual relevance annotation from human experts.
- Proposes two methods based on prediction-powered inference and conformal risk control that utilize computer-generated relevance annotations to place reliable confidence intervals (CIs) around IR evaluation metrics.
- Generated relevance annotations are not immune to (systematic) errors, and as a result, directly using them for evaluation produces unreliable results.
