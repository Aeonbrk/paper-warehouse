# Mitigating Data Imbalance in Time Series Classification Based on Counterfactual Minority Samples Augmentation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Imbalanced Time-Series Classification is a critical, yet challenging task across a spectrum of real-world applications.
- Proposes a CounterFactual Augmentation Minority Generation (CFAMG) method based on generative models that aims to discover the causal factors that determine different classes from a causality perspective.
- As a result, the generated samples may fail to enhance class separability, thereby limiting improvements in classification performance.
