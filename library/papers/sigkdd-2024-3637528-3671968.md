# Predicting Long-term Dynamics of Complex Networks via Identifying Skeleton in Hyperbolic Space

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Learning complex network dynamics is fundamental for understanding, modeling, and controlling real-world complex systems.
- Proposes the Dynamics-Invariant Skeleton Neural Network (DiskNet), which identifies skeletons of complex networks based on the renormalization group structure in hyperbolic space to preserve both topological and dynamics properties.
- Experiments across three representative dynamics as well as five real-world and two synthetic networks demonstrate the performances of the proposed DiskNet.
