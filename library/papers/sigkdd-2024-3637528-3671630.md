## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Presents Bias-mitigating Hard Negative Sampling (BHNS), a negative sampling strategy tailored to identify and adjust for false negatives, building upon our original False Negative Estimation algorithm.
- Furthermore, comparative analyses on public dataset showcase its domain-agnostic potential for diverse applications.
