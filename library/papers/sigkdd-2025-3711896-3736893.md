# Contrastive Text-enhanced Transformer for Cross-Domain Sequential Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Cross-domain sequential recommendation (CDSR) aims to enhance recommendation performance in a target domain by leveraging user sequential preferences from a source domain.
- Proposes a semantic-behavior contrastive learning approach to enhance cross-domain preference learning and knowledge transfer.
- Experiments on three real-world datasets demonstrate that our CTT outperforms the models by an average of 5.86% on HR@5.
