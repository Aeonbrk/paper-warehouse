# Abductive Learning for Neuro-Symbolic Grounded Imitation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent learning-to-imitation methods have shown promise in planning by imitating within the observation-action space, yet they remain constrained in open environments, especially for long-horizon tasks.
- Draw inspiration from abductive learning and introduce a framework ABductive Imitation Learning (ABIL) that integrates the benefits of data-driven learning and symbolic-based reasoning, enabling long-horizon planning.
- In contrast, while traditional symbolic planning excels in such tasks through logical reasoning over human-defined symbolic spaces, it struggles with high-dimensional visual inputs encountered in real-world scenarios.
