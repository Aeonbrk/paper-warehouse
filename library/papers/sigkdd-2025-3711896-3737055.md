# MOSS: Multi-Objective Optimization for Stable Rule Sets

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Presents MOSS, a multi-objective optimization framework for constructing stable sets of decision rules.
- Develops a specialized cutting plane algorithm in our framework to rapidly compute the Pareto frontier between these two objectives, and our algorithm scales to problem instances beyond the capabilities of commercial optimization solvers.
- MOSS incorporates three important criteria for interpretability: sparsity, accuracy, and stability, into a single multi-objective optimization framework.
