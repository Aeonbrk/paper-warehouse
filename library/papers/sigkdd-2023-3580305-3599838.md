# Hierarchical Projection Enhanced Multi-behavior Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Various types of user behaviors are recorded in most real-world recommendation scenarios.
- The key module, Projection-based Transfer Network (PTN), uses the projection mechanism to "explicitly" model the correlations of upstream and downstream behaviors, refines the upstream behavior representations.
- Offline experiments on public and industrial datasets and online A/B test further verify the effectiveness of HPMR in modeling the associations from upstream to downstream and alleviating the negative transfer.
