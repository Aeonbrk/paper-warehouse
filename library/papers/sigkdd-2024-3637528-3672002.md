# Capturing Homogeneous Influence among Students: Hypergraph Cognitive Diagnosis for Intelligent Education Systems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes a hypergraph cognitive diagnosis model (HyperCDM) to address these challenges and effectively capture the homogeneous influence.
- Experiments on both offline and online real-world datasets show that HyperCDM achieves performance in terms of interpretability and capturing homogeneous influence effectively, and is competitive in generalization.
