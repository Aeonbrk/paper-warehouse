## Abstract-derived synopsis

- As increasingly capable open-weight large language models (LLMs) are deployed, improving their tamper resistance against unsafe modifications, whether accidental or intentional, becomes critical to minimize risks.
- Introduces TamperBench, the first unified framework to systematically evaluate the tamper resistance of LLMs.
