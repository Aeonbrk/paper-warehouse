# Habitat Inference from Biodiversity Field Notes

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Systematic land-cover products derived from remote sensing extend back only a few decades, leaving major gaps for earlier periods.
- Introduces a benchmark that aggregates habitat-note text into spatial grid-cell documents with proxy supervision from land-cover maps, and present an analysis of text-based predictors and graph-enriched classification pipelines for inferring standard habitat classes.
