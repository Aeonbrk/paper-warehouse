# CalCo: A Hierarchical Bayesian Framework for Scalable Human-LLM Hybrid Labeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs), data annotation workflows are increasingly shifting toward hybrid systems where LLM-generated labels complement or replace human effort.
- Effectively incorporating these non-deterministic signals into existing human labeling processes remains a challenge, particularly in complex industrial settings involving multiple correlated tasks and diverse model architectures.
- Reports the efficacy of our approach through empirical results on both simulated and real-world datasets, showing that CalCo outperforms baseline methods in a wide range of applications.
