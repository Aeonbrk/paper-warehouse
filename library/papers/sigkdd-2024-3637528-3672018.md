# Learn Together Stop Apart: an Inclusive Approach to Ensemble Pruning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Gradient Boosting is a leading learning method that builds ensembles and adapts their sizes to particular tasks, consistently delivering top-tier results across various applications.
- The DSP method uses a decision tree to partition the data based on learning curves, while ISP leverages the dataset's geometric and target distribution characteristics.
- Introduces an adaptive approach to early stopping in Gradient Boosting, addressing data heterogeneity by assigning different stop moments to different data regions at inference time while still training a common ensemble on the entire.
