# A Practical Upper Bound on Selection Bias Effects in Medical Prediction Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Selection bias is a common and often unavoidable aspect of real-world data that challenges the generalizability of machine learning models.
- Proposes a upper bound on the worst-case model performance on the target population under the realistic setting where the selection mechanism and the target population data are only partially observed.
- Reports the validity and practical utility of our method through experiments on fully synthetic data, semi-synthetic data derived from the All of Us Research Program, and real-world selection bias in MIMIC-IV.
