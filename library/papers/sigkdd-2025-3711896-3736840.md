# ALSA: Context-Sensitive Prompt Privacy Preservation in Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The prompting capability of large language models (LLMs) offers substantial convenience to users across diverse backgrounds.
- Among various studies, text anonymization is considered an approach to preventing privacy leakage in prompts through text substitution.
- Experiments on five benchmark datasets report comparisons with ALSA over baselines in terms of accuracy, privacy preservation, and semantic integrity.
