# Bias Detection via Maximum Subgroup Discrepancy

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Bias evaluation is fundamental to trustworthy AI, both in terms of checking data quality and in terms of checking the outputs of AI systems.
- Proposes a new notion of distance, the Maximum Subgroup Discrepancy (MSD).
- In testing data quality, for example, one may study the distance of a given dataset, viewed as a distribution, to a given ground-truth reference dataset.
