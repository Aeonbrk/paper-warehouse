# TrustGLM: Evaluating the Robustness of GraphLLMs Against Prompt, Text, and Structure Attacks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Inspired by the success of large language models (LLMs), there is a research shift from traditional graph learning methods to LLM-based graph frameworks, formally known as GraphLLMs.
- Introduces TrustGLM, a comprehensive study evaluating the vulnerability of GraphLLMs to adversarial attacks across three dimensions: text, graph structure, and prompt manipulations.
- Through experiments on six benchmark datasets from diverse domains, our findings reveal that GraphLLMs are highly susceptible to text attacks that merely replace a few semantically similar words in a node's textual attribute.
