# Graph Neural Network Training and Data Tiering

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have shown success in learning from graph-structured data, with applications to fraud detection, recommendation, and knowledge graph reasoning.
- Provide a method to statistically analyze and identify more frequently accessed data ahead of GNN training.
- Our data tiering method not only utilizes the structure of input graph, but also an insight gained from actual GNN training process to achieve a higher prediction result.
