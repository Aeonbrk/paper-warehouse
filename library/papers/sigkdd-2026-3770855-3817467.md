# OmniEarth: A Benchmark for Evaluating Vision–Language Models in Geospatial Tasks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Vision–Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated perception and reasoning capabilities on general-domain tasks, leading to growing interest in their application to Earth observation.
- Introduces OmniEarth, a benchmark for evaluating RSVLMs under realistic Earth observation scenarios.
- A systematic benchmark for comprehensively evaluating remote sensing vision–language models (RSVLMs) remains lacking.
