# TAG: Toward Accurate Social Media Content Tagging with a Concept Graph

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Although conceptualization has been widely studied in semantics and knowledge representation, it is still challenging to find the most accurate concept terms to tag fast-growing social media content.
- Further proposes a graph-graph matching framework that demonstrates abstraction and generalization performance by better utilizing both the structural information in the concept graph and logic interactions between semantic units in the natural language sentence.
- Presents TAG, a high-quality concept matching dataset consisting of 10,000 labeled pairs of fine-grained concepts and web-styled natural language sentences, mined from open-domain social media content.
