# PMKLC: Parallel Multi-Knowledge Learning-based Lossless Compression for Large-Scale Genomics Database

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Learning-based lossless compressors play a crucial role in large-scale genomic database backup, storage, transmission, and management.
- Proposes a Parallel Multi-Knowledge Learning-based Compressor (PMKLC) with four crucial designs: 1) proposes an automated multi-knowledge learning-based compression framework as compressors' backbone to enhance compression ratio and robustness.
- Benchmark PMKLC-S/M and 14 baselines (7 traditional and 7 leaning-based) on 15 real-world datasets with different species and data sizes.
