# MBrain: A Multi-channel Self-Supervised Learning Framework for Brain Signals

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Brain signals are important quantitative data for understanding physiological activities and diseases of human brain.
- Proposes to study the self-supervised learning (SSL) framework for brain signals that can be applied to pre-train either SEEG or EEG data.
- Experiments of seizure detection on both EEG and SEEG large-scale real-world datasets demonstrate that our model outperforms several time series SSL and unsupervised models, and has the ability to be deployed to clinical practice.
