# TiWeaver: Unified Temporal Dynamics Modeling via Contextual Patching

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multivariate time series forecasting plays a critical role in real-world applications, including weather prediction, stock analysis, and health monitoring.
- Proposes TiWeaver, a unified framework designed to handle temporal dynamics and fine-grained inter-channel dependencies adaptively.
- Evaluates TiWeaver on 12 real-world time series datasets, where it achieves performance, outperforming existing methods up to 25%.
