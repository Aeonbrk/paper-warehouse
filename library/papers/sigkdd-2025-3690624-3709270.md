# Electron-Informed Coarse-Graining Molecular Representation Learning for Real-World Molecular Physics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Various representation learning methods for molecular structures have been devised to accelerate data-driven chemistry.
- Proposes a method for learning electron-informed molecular representations without additional computation costs by transferring readily accessible electron-level information about small molecules to large molecules of our interest.
- The proposed method achieved prediction accuracy on benchmark datasets containing experimentally observed molecular physics.
