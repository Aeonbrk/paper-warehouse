# GraphMind: Unveiling Scientific Reasoning through Contextual Graphs for Novelty Assessment

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Assessing scientific novelty is inherently complex, requiring evaluation of both a paper's internal structure and its contribution within the broader research landscape.
- Proposes GraphMind, a model that jointly processes micro- and macro-level structures for novelty prediction and rationale generation.
- Introduces SciNova, a benchmark containing 3,063 papers from both ICLR and NeurIPS, with full content, bibliographies, and peer review scores for novelty prediction.
