# On the Role of Anticausal Direction in LLM-based Data Synthesis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly used to generate synthetic data.
- The research motivates us to propose XSyn, a generative framework that transforms anticausal and causal synthetic data to better match real-world distributions.
- Our study finds that models trained on causal synthetic data consistently outperform those trained on anticausal data.
