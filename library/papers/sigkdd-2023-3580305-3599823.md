# Fair Multilingual Vandalism Detection System for Wikipedia

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Presents a design of the system aimed at supporting the Wikipedia community in addressing vandalism on the platform.
- Furthermore, our model outperforms ORES, ensuring that the results provided are not only more accurate but also less biased against certain groups of contributors.
- To achieve this, collected a massive dataset of 47 languages, and applied advanced filtering and feature engineering techniques, including multilingual masked language modeling to build the training dataset from human-generated data.
