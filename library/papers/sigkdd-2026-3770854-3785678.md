# MM-SpuBench: Towards Better Understanding of Spurious Biases in Multimodal LLMs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spurious bias, a tendency to exploit spurious correlations between superficial input attributes and prediction targets, has revealed a severe robustness pitfall in classical machine learning problems.
- Introduces MM-SpuBench, a comprehensive, human-verified benchmark dataset consisting of image-class pairs annotated with core and spurious attributes, grounded in our taxonomy of nine distinct types of spurious correlations.
