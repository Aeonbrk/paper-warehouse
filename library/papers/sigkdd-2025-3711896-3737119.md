# SDE: A Simplified and Disentangled Dependency Encoding Framework for State Space Models in Time Series Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Advancements in deep learning have spurred the development of numerous models for Long-term Time Series Forecasting (LTSF).
- Proposes SDE (Simplified and Disentangled Dependency Encoding), a framework designed to enhance the capability of SSMs for LTSF.
- Experiments on nine real-world benchmark datasets demonstrate that SDE-enhanced SSMs consistently outperform time series forecasting models.
