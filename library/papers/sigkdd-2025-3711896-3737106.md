# Revisiting Feature Interactions from the Perspective of Quadratic Neural Networks for Click-through Rate Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Hadamard Product (HP) has long been a cornerstone in click-through rate (CTR) prediction tasks due to its simplicity, effectiveness, and ability to capture feature interactions without additional parameters.
- Proposes the Multi-Head Khatri-Rao Product as a alternative to HP and a Self-Ensemble Loss with dynamic ensemble capability within the same network to enhance computational efficiency and performance.
