# MapAgent: An Industrial-Grade Agentic Framework for City-scale Lane-level Map Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Lane-level maps are critical infrastructure for autonomous driving and lane-level navigation, yet constructing and maintaining standardized lane networks for hundreds of cities remains highly labor-intensive.
- Proposes MapAgent, an industrial-grade agentic architecture that augments a vectorization backbone for specification-compliant lane-map production.
- Recent end-to-end vectorized mapping methods can predict lane geometry and topology directly from sensor data, but they typically treat mapping specifications and traffic regulations as implicit, dataset-dependent supervision.
