# Agent-Didactic Spaces: Mining Multi-Stakeholder Trade-Offs in Educational Pathway Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Educational pathway recommenders (systems that suggest courses, credentials, or degree sequences) serve multiple stakeholders: learners pursuing personal growth, institutions safeguarding curricular coherence, and labor markets signaling skill demand.
- Proposes Agent-Didactic Spaces (ADS), a framework that preserves the full Pareto structure of educational decisions rather than scalarizing it away.
- Preliminary results on a benchmark of 18,057 university courses, 1,016 occupations, and 56 institutional missions show that preserving the Pareto frontier surfaces 10-50× more contestable alternatives than weighted-sum baselines, while remaining computationally practical.
