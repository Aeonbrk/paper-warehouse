# TEMPER: Capturing Consistent and Fluctuating TEMPoral User Behaviour for EtheReum Phishing Scam Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Phishing scams on the Ethereum network have become a serious threat, especially with the influx of new users into the cryptocurrency market.
- Introduces TEMPER, a sequential learning framework designed to jointly capture the subtle distinctions between long- and short-term user behaviours and their correlations to provide more comprehensive insights.
- Through experimentation on three real-world Ethereum datasets, TEMPER demonstrates its efficacy by achieving a 3-4% improvement in the F1-Score compared to existing baseline models, representing a advancement in Ethereum phishing user detection.
