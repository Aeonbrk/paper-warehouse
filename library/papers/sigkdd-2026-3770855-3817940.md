# From Uniform to Learned Graph Priors: Diffusion for Structure Discovery

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Neural relational inference (NRI) methods discover interaction graphs from trajectories through variational reasoning on discrete potential edges.
- Proposes Diff-prior, a diffusion-parameterized adaptive prior used to calibrate latent graph distribution rather than generate graphs.
- This systemic misalignment does not match the real-world systems and yields diffuse and indecisive edge posteriors limiting the reliability of structural discovery.
