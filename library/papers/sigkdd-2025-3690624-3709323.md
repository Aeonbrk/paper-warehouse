# AutoSTF: Decoupled Neural Architecture Search for Cost-Effective Automated Spatio-Temporal Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spatio-temporal forecasting is a critical component of various smart city applications, such as transportation optimization, energy management, and socio-economic analysis.
- Proposes AutoSTF, a decoupled automatic neural architecture search framework for cost-automated spatio-temporal forecasting.
- Experiments on eight datasets report higher performance for AutoSTF in terms of both accuracy and efficiency.
