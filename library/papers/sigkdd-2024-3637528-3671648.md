# Learning to Rank for Maps at Airbnb

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- As a two-sided marketplace, Airbnb brings together hosts who own listings for rent with prospective guests from around the globe.
- Both these interfaces, since their inception, have used the same ranking algorithm that orders listings by their booking probabilities and selects the top listings for display.
- Our iterative and experiment-driven approach led us through a path full of twists and turns, ending in a unified theory for the two interfaces.
