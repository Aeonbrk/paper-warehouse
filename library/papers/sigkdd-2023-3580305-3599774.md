# Assisting Clinical Decisions for Scarcely Available Treatment via Disentangled Latent Representation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) is an essential life-supporting modality for COVID-19 patients who are refractory to conventional therapies.
- Proposes Treatment Variational AutoEncoder (TVAE), a approach for individualized treatment analysis.
- Evaluates TVAE on two real-world COVID-19 datasets: an international dataset collected from 1651 hospitals across 63 countries, and a institutional dataset collected from 15 hospitals.
