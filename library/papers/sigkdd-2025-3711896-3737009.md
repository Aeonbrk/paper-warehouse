# Incorporating Retrieval-based Causal Learning with Information Bottlenecks for Interpretable Molecular Graph Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have gained considerable traction for modeling molecular structures and predicting properties, but their interpretability remains a challenge in understanding chemical behaviors.
- In this study, have developed a interpretable causal GNN framework that combines retrieval-based causal learning with Graph Information Bottleneck (GIB) theory.
- While transparent methods can enhance GNN predictions, they typically compromise on explanation precision.
