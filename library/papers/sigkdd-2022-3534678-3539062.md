# Mixture of Virtual-Kernel Experts for Multi-Objective User Profile Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In industrial applications like online advertising and recommendation systems, diverse and accurate user profiles can greatly help improve personalization.
- The usual approach is to take a certain action as the objective, and introduce multiple independent Two-Tower models to predict the possibility of users' action on tags (known as CTR or CVR prediction).
- Apply the model in Tencent Advertising System, where both online and offline evaluations show that our method has a improvement compared with the existing ones and brings about an obvious lift to actual advertising.
