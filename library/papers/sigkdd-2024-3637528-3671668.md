# FreQuant: A Reinforcement-Learning based Adaptive Portfolio Optimization with Multi-frequency Decomposition

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- How can leverage inherent frequency features of stock signals for portfolio optimization?
- Proposes FreQuant (Adaptive Portfolio Optimization via Multi-Frequency Quantitative Analysis), an deep RL framework for portfolio optimization that fully operates in the frequency domain, tackling the limitations of time domain-focused models.
- Empirical validation on diverse real-world trading datasets underscores the performance of FreQuant, showing its superiority in terms of profitability.
