# Improving Group Robustness on Spurious Correlation via Evidential Alignment

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Deep neural networks often learn and rely on spurious correlations, i.e., superficial associations between non-causal features and the targets.
- Theoretically justify the effectiveness of our method as capable of learning the patterns of biased models and debiasing the model without requiring any spurious correlation annotations.
- While it can yield high overall accuracy during training, it degrades generalization on more diverse scenarios where such correlations do not hold.
