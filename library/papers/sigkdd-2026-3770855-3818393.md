# Edge-Cloud Collaborative Multimodal Dehazing for UAV Vision

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) applications, dense haze causes strong scattering in the visible (VIS) spectrum, reducing the visibility of small ground-level objects.
- Proposes an edge–cloud collaborative framework in which VIS serves as the anchor stream.
- Conduct experiments on multiple public datasets, achieve performance, and further validate its effectiveness in real-world scenarios.
