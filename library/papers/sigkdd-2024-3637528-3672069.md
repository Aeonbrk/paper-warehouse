# Masked LoGoNet: Fast and Accurate 3D Image Analysis for Medical Domain

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Standard modern machine-learning-based imaging methods have faced challenges in medical applications due to the high cost of dataset construction and, thereby, the limited labeled training data available.
- Introduces a new neural network architecture, termed LoGoNet, with a tailored self-supervised learning (SSL) method to mitigate such challenges.
- Benchmark comparisons with eight models highlight LoGoNet's performance in both inference time and accuracy.
