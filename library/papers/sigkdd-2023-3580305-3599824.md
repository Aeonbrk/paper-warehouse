# FairCod: A Fairness-aware Concurrent Dispatch System for Large-scale Instant Delivery Services

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Have been witnessing a rapid prevalence of instant delivery services (e,g., UberEats, Instacart, and Eleme) due to their convenience and timeliness.
- Designs a Dynamic Advantage Actor-Critic algorithm with Fairness constrain (DA2CF).
- More importantly, extensively evaluate our FairCod system based on one-month real-world data consisting of 36.38 million orders from 42,000 couriers collected by one of the largest instant delivery companies in China.
