# Fusing Multimodal Signals on Hyper-complex Space for Extreme Abstractive Text Summarization (TL;DR) of Scientific Contents

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The realm of scientific text summarization has experienced progress due to the availability of annotated brief summaries and ample data.
- Presents mTLDRgen, an encoder-decoder-based model that employs a dual-fused hyper-complex Transformer combined with a Wasserstein Riemannian Encoder Transformer, to dexterously capture the intricacies between different modalities in a hyper-complex latent geometric space.
- Introduces mTLDR, a first-of-its-kind dataset for the aforementioned task, comprising videos, audio, and text, along with both author-composed summaries and expert-annotated summaries.
