# ClinicalAgents: Multi-Agent Orchestration for Clinical Decision Making with Dual-Memory

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated potential in healthcare, they often struggle with the complex, non-linear reasoning required for accurate clinical diagnosis.
- Introduces ClinicalAgents, a multi-agent framework designed to simulate the cognitive workflow of expert clinicians.
- Reports evaluation showing ClinicalAgents achieves the best performance among evaluated baselines, enhancing both diagnostic accuracy and explainability compared to strong single-agent and multi-agent baselines.
