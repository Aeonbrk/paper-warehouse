# Mitigating Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning Shortcuts with Data-Driven Knowledge Augmentation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent advancements in neuro-symbolic learning (NeSy) have shown promise in integrating deep learning with symbolic reasoning, offering both interpretability and generalization.
- Presents a method called DKA to address this issue.
- The prevalence of reasoning shortcuts, where the NeSy system predicts incorrect intermediate concepts while maintaining high final accuracy, poses a substantial challenge.
