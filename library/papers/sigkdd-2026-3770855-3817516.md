# DTBench: A Synthetic Benchmark for Document-to-Table Extraction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Document-to-table (Doc2Table) extraction derives structured tables from unstructured documents under a target schema, enabling reliable and verifiable SQL-based data analytics.
- Presents DTBench, a synthetic benchmark that adopts a proposed two-level taxonomy of Doc2Table capabilities, covering 5 major categories and 13 subcategories.
- Argue that a capability-aware benchmark is essential for systematic evaluation.
