# Are You Using Reliable Graph Prompts? Trojan Prompt Attacks on Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Prompt Learning (GPL) has been introduced as a approach that uses prompts to adapt pre-trained GNN models to specific downstream tasks without requiring fine-tuning of the entire model.
- Introduces a finetuning-resistant poisoning approach that maintains the effectiveness of the backdoor even after downstream model adjustments.
- TGPA injects backdoors into graph prompts without modifying pre-trained GNN encoders and ensures high attack success rates and clean accuracy.
