# Multi-objective Optimization of Notifications Using Offline Reinforcement Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Mobile notification systems play a major role in a variety of applications to communicate, send alerts and reminders to the users to inform them about news, events or messages.
- Proposes an end-to-end offline reinforcement learning framework to optimize sequential notification decisions.
- Illustrate our fully-deployed system and demonstrate the performance and benefits of the proposed approach through both offline and online experiments.
