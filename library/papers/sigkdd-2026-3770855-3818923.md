# Neural Gaussian Radio Fields for Channel Estimation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate channel state information (CSI) is a critical bottleneck in modern wireless networks, with pilot overhead consuming 11% to 21% of transmission bandwidth and feedback delays causing severe throughput degradation under mobility.
- Introduces neural Gaussian radio fields (nGRF), a physics-informed framework that reframes neural field design by replacing view-dependent rasterization with direct complex-valued aggregation in 3D space.
- For large-scale outdoor environments where implicit methods fail, nGRF achieves 28.32 dB SNR, demonstrating that structured representations supplemented by domain physics can fundamentally outperform generic deep learning architectures.
