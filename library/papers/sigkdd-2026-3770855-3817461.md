# Enhancing Biomedical AI Foundations: Genomic Literature Knowledge Base Boosts LLMs' Mastery of Biomedical Literature

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Peered-reviewed literature provides reliable biomedical knowledge, which is essential for large language models (LLMs) in solving complex biomedical problems.
- It supports diverse applications, including reinforcement learning, link prediction, and semantic embedding.
- Eight LLMs achieve average accuracy gains of 27.5% on PubMedQA-HC, 24.8% on PubMedQA-Artificial, and 6.0% on BioASQ.
