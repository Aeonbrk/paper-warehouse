# Towards Learning Disentangled Representations for Time Series

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Progress has been made toward learning efficient time series representations in recent years, but the learned representations often lack interpretability and do not encode semantic meanings by the complex interactions of many latent factors.
- Proposes Disentangle Time-Series, a disentanglement enhancement framework for time series data.
- Experimental results on five real-world IoT datasets demonstrate that the representations learned by DTS achieve performance in various tasks with better interpretability.
