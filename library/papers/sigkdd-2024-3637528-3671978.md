# Expander Hierarchies for Normalized Cuts on Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Expander decompositions of graphs have advanced the understanding of many classical graph problems and led to numerous fundamental theoretical results.
- Introduces the first practically efficient algorithm for computing expander decompositions and their hierarchies and demonstrate its effectiveness and utility by incorporating it as the core component in a solver for the normalized cut graph.
