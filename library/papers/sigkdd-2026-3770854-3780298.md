# Signed Proximity Matters in Graph-based Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph-based models are a technique for recommendation systems, which seek to leverage the graph structure created by user-item interactions for elevated performance.
- Proposes to upgrade the neighborhood aggregation scheme in GNNs with two notions of signed local proximity (SLP) and signed global proximity (SGP) based on weak balance theory.
- Reports evaluation showing SPGNN outperforms other unsigned and sign-aware models on six benchmark datasets with up to a gain of 19.42% in Recall and 28.18% in NDCG.
