# The Boy Who Cried Wolf: Adversarial Misclassification of Safe Inputs as Unsafe in Multimodal Guardrails

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Introduces Unsafe Induction Attacks, where adversaries distribute imperceptibly perturbed safe images that trigger guard models to reject legitimate user requests, causing a ''Boy Who Cried Wolf'' effect that degrades service availability and erodes trust.
