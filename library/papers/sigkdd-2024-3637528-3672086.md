# Long-Term Vessel Trajectory Imputation with Physics-Guided Diffusion Probabilistic Model

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Maritime traffic management increasingly relies on vessel position information provided by terrestrial and satellite networks of the Automatic Identification System (AIS).
- Introduces a diffusion-based model for generating missing trajectories, where observed trajectory modeling with transformer encoding architecture and embeddings of both historical vessel trajectory and external factors serve as conditional information.
- Comprehensive experiments and analysis on a real-world AIS dataset confirm the effectiveness of our proposed approach.
