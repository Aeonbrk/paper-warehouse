# Learning Graph Rationales to Compress Long Chains of Thought in Multimodal Reasoning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent advances in multimodal large reasoning models (MLRMs) have demonstrated impressive capabilities on complex multimodal tasks, yet their reliance on long Chain-of-Thoughts (CoTs) often leads to redundant reasoning and high computational cost.
- After constructing the reasoning graph, GRACE applies a graph rationale learning approach with a selector–predictor architecture.
- Experiments on multiple multimodal benchmarks show that GRACE substantially improves inference efficiency and interpretability without sacrificing accuracy.
