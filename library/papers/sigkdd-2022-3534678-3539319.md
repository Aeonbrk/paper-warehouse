# On Structural Explanation of Bias in Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have shown satisfying performance in various graph analytical problems.
- Proposes a post-hoc explanation framework to identify two edge sets that can maximally account for the exhibited bias and maximally contribute to the fairness level of the GNN prediction for any given node, respectively.
- Experiments on real-world datasets validate the effectiveness of the proposed framework towards delivering structural explanations for the bias of GNNs.
