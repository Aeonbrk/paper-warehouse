# Imputation-based Time-Series Anomaly Detection with Conditional Weight-Incremental Diffusion Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Existing anomaly detection models for time series are primarily trained with normal-point-dominant data and would become ineffective when anomalous points intensively occur in certain episodes.
- Designs a new denoising diffusion-based imputation method to enhance the imputation performance of missing values with conditional weight-incremental diffusion.
- Experimental results on real-world datasets show that DiffAD performs better than benchmarks.
