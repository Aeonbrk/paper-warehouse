# Improved Active Covering via Density-Based Space Transformation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies active covering, a variant of the active-learning problem that involves labeling (or identifying) all of the examples with a positive label.
- Proposes a couple of algorithms, namely Density-Adjusted Non-Adaptive (DANA) learner and Density-Adjusted Adaptive (DAA) learner, that query the labels according to a distance function that is adjusted by the density function.
- Our experiments show that our champion algorithm DAA consistently improves over the prior work on some standard benchmark datasets, including those used by the previous work, as well as a couple of data sets.
