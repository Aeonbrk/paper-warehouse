# Enhanced Emotion-aware Music Recommendation via Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The profound psychological interplay between music and human affect underscores the critical role of emotion-aware modeling in music recommendation systems.
- Presents the Large-Language-Model-based emotion-aware music recommendation model(LEMON), a framework that harnesses Large Language Models to address two fundamental challenges: extracting multi-faceted emotional semantics from music content and dynamically aligning users' transient emotional states with.
- Evaluations on industrial and public datasets demonstrate LEMON's superiority, achieving 4.22% AUC improvements over baselines.
