# Co-Evolution of Large Language Models and Configuration Strategies to Enhance Surrogate-Assisted Evolutionary Algorithm

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Surrogate-assisted evolutionary algorithms (SAEAs) are well-suited for optimizing computationally expensive black-box problems in diverse real-world scenarios.
- The sample efficiency of SAEAs depends largely on the configuration of the surrogate model and sampling criteria.
- Empirically validate the efficacy of CoE-SAEA by comparing it to algorithms across various benchmark problems and a real-world traffic signal optimization task.
