# Unbiased Locally Private Estimator for Polynomials of Laplacian Variables

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Presents a mechanism to debias polynomial functions computed from locally differentially private data.
- Our proposed recursive algorithm debiases these functions, with a calculation time of O(r n log n), where r is the polynomial degree and n is the number of users.
- The results show that our method not only eliminates bias, but also provides at least 100 times more accuracy than previous works.
