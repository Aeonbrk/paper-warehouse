# Reducing Exposure to Harmful Content via Graph Rewiring

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Most media content consumed today is provided by digital platforms that aggregate input from diverse sources, where access to information is mediated by recommendation algorithms.
- Introduces Gamine, a fast greedy algorithm that can reduce the exposure to harmful content with or without quality constraints on recommendations.
- Through experiments on synthetic data and real-world data from video recommendation and news feed applications, confirm the effectiveness, robustness, and efficiency of Gamine in practice.
