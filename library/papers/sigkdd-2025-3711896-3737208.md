# DiMA: An LLM-Powered Ride-Hailing Assistant at DiDi

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- On-demand ride-hailing services like DiDi, Uber, and Lyft have transformed urban transportation, offering unmatched convenience and flexibility.
- Introduces DiMA, an LLM-powered ride-hailing assistant deployed in DiDi Chuxing.
- Since its deployment in the DiDi application, DiMA has demonstrated exceptional performance, achieving 93% accuracy in order planning and 92% in response generation during real-world interactions.
