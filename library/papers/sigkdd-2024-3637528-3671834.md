# FUGNN: Harmonizing Fairness and Utility in Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Fairness-aware Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) often face a challenging trade-off, where prioritizing fairness may require compromising utility.
- Proposes FUGNN, a spectral graph learning approach that harmonizes the conflict between fairness and utility.
- Experiments on six real-world datasets report higher performance for FUGNN over baseline methods.
