# ChessFraud: Exploring the Capabilities of Human-Aligned Models for Cheating Detection in Online Chess

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Human-aligned chess models, designed to mimic human decision-making rather than maximize engine strength, pose a challenge for online fair-play enforcement.
- Introduces ChessFraud, the first public benchmark for move-level cheating detection, providing 505 tournament games with ground-truth annotations from a controlled environment where engine usage was explicitly logged.
- Construct ChessFraud-Synth, a large-scale synthetic dataset derived from 12,000 Lichess games.
