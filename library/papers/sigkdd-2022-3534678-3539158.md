# Graph-based Multilingual Language Model: Leveraging Product Relations for Search Relevance

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The large-scale nature of product catalog and the changing demands of customer queries makes product search a challenging problem.
- Proposes Structure-Aware multilingual LAnguage Model (SALAM), that utilizes a language model along with a graph neural network, to extract region-specific semantics as well as relational information for the classification of query-product pairs.
- Our model is first pre-trained on a large region-agnostic dataset and behavioral graph data and then fine-tuned on region-specific versions to address the sparsity.
