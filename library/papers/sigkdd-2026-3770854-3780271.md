# Continuous-Time Counterfactual Quantile Learning for Risk-Sensitive Policy Optimization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies the problem of Continuous-Time Counterfactual Quantile Learning (CT-CQL) for risk-sensitive policy optimization.
- Proposes CT-CQL, a framework built upon a identification theory and featuring three key components: (1) modeling full counterfactual outcome distributions via Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs) governed by the Fokker–Planck Equation (FPE).
- Experiments on widely-used benchmarks and the real-world MIMIC-III dataset demonstrate the validation and superiority of the proposed method.
