# Stop Treating Collisions Equally: Qualification-Aware Semantic ID Learning for Recommendation at Industrial Scale

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Semantic IDs (SIDs) are compact discrete representations derived from multimodal item features, serving as a unified and efficient abstraction for both conventional ID-based recommendation and emerging generative recommendation.
- Proposes Qualification-Aware Semantic ID Learning (QuaSID), an end-to-end framework that learns collision-qualified SIDs by selectively repelling qualified conflict pairs and scaling the repulsion strength by collision severity.
- In a 5-day online A/B test on Kuaishou e-commerce, deploying QuaSID-learned SIDs in retrieval and ranking yields measurable business impact: with a 5% traffic split (covering over 20 million users), QuaSID increases ranking GMV-S2.
