# How Far Can You Grow? Characterizing the Extrapolation Frontier of Graph Generative Models for Materials Science

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Every generative model for crystalline materials harbors a critical structure size beyond which its outputs quietly become unreliable; call this the extrapolation frontier.
- Despite its direct consequences for nanomaterial design, this frontier has never been systematically measured.
- Introduces RADII, a radius-resolved benchmark of ∼75,000 crystal-derived nanoparticle structures (33--11,298 atoms) that treats radius as a continuous scaling knob to trace generation quality from in-distribution to out-of-distribution regimes under leakage-free splits.
