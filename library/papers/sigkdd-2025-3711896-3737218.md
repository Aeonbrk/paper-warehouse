# FedMetro: Efficient Metro Passenger Flow Prediction via Federated Graph Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Metro passenger flow prediction is crucial for urban transportation management.
- Introduces a federated dynamic graph learning approach with cross-attention mechanisms to capture spatial-temporal correlations in passenger flow.
- Evaluations on three real-world metro AFC datasets demonstrate that FedMetro outperforms baseline methods, achieving up to 17.08% higher accuracy while reducing federated inference communication overhead by 77.99%.
