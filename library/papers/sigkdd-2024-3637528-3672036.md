# DipDNN: Preserving Inverse Consistency and Approximation Efficiency for Invertible Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Consistent bi-directional inferences are the key for many machine learning applications.
- Without consistency, inverse learning-based inferences can cause fuzzy images, erroneous control signals, and cascading failure in SCADA systems.
- As a result, strictly guarantee the consistency of global inverse modeling without harming DNN's capability for universal approximation.
