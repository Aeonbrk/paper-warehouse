# RCTD: Reputation-Constrained Truth Discovery in Sybil Attack Crowdsourcing Environment

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Sybil attacks are a prevalent concern within the realm of crowdsourcing, underscoring the significance of quality control in this domain.
- Proposes a method called RCTD (Reputation-Constrained Truth Discovery), which introduces a similarity metric between the rankings of workers' weights and the refined approval rates.
- Experimental results on real-world datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves more accurate inference of true results in the Sybil attack environment compared to the methods.
