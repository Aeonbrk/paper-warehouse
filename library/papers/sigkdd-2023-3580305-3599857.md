# Learning Multivariate Hawkes Process via Graph Recurrent Neural Network

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Presents a approach for modeling and predicting patterns of events in time-series learning, named graph recurrent temporal point process (GRTPP).
- Prior research has focused on using deep learning techniques, such as recurrent neural networks (RNNs) or attention-based sequential data embedding, on modeling the time-varying intensity of events.
- The proposed GRTPP model offers improved effectiveness and explainability compared to previous models, as demonstrated through empirical evaluations on five real-world datasets and the actual credit card transaction dataset.
