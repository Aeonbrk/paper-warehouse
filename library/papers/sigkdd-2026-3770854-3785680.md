# Automated Annotation of Privacy Information in User Interactions with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Users interacting with large language models (LLMs) under their real identifiers often unknowingly risk disclosing private information.
- Design evaluation metrics at the levels of privacy leakage, extracted privacy phrase, and privacy information.
- A gap remains between current performance and the requirements of real-world LLM applications, motivating future research into more local privacy detection methods grounded in our dataset.
