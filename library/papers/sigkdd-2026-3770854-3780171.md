# Mixture-of-RAG: Integrating Text and Tables with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large language models (LLMs) achieve optimal utility when their responses are grounded in external knowledge sources.
- Proposes MixRAG, a three-stage framework: (i) hierarchy row-and-column-level (H-RCL) representation that preserves hierarchical structure and heterogeneous relationship, (ii) an ensemble retriever with LLM-based reranking for evidence alignment.
- Real-world documents, such as annual reports, scientific papers, and clinical guidelines, frequently combine narrative content with complex, hierarchically structured tables.
