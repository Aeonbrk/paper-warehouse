# Sparse Binary Transformers for Multivariate Time Series Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Compressed Neural Networks have the potential to enable deep learning across new applications and smaller computational environments.
- Our model achieves favorable results across three time series learning tasks: classification, anomaly detection, and single-step forecasting.
- Apply sparse and binary-weighted Transformers to multivariate time series problems, showing that the lightweight models achieve accuracy comparable to that of dense floating-point Transformers of the same structure.
