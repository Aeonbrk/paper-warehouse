# TRACER: Physics-Guided Causal Evidence Construction for Zero-Shot Traffic Anomaly Diagnosis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Identifying the root causes of non-recurrent traffic congestion is critical for maintaining urban mobility and resilience.
- Presents TRACER (Traffic Root Anomaly Causal Evidence Reasoner), a training-free agent framework for zero-shot traffic diagnosis.
- Experiments on SUMO simulations and the PeMS-BAY dataset demonstrate that TRACER achieves a 32.6% relative improvement in Hit@1 accuracy over baselines, while reducing inference time by 85.7%.
