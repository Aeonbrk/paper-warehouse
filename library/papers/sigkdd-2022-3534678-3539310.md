# Variational Graph Author Topic Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While Variational Graph Auto-Encoder (VGAE) has presented ability to learn representations for documents, most existing VGAE methods do not model a latent topic structure and therefore lack semantic interpretability.
- Proposes a Variational Graph Author Topic Model for documents to integrate both semantic interpretability and authorship and venue modeling into a unified VGAE framework.
