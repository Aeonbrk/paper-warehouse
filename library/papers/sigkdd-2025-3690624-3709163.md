# On Measuring Unnoticeability of Graph Adversarial Attacks: Observations, New Measure, and Applications

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Adversarial attacks are allegedly unnoticeable.
- Introduces HideNSeek, a learnable measure for graph attack noticeability.
- Using six real-world graphs, empirically demonstrate that HideNSeek effectively alleviates the observed limitations, and LEO (i.e., our learnable edge scorer) outperforms eleven competitors in distinguishing attack edges under five different attack methods.
