# Auto-Validate by-History: Auto-Program Data Quality Constraints to Validate Recurring Data Pipelines

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Data pipelines are widely employed in modern enterprises to power a variety of Machine-Learning (ML) and Business-Intelligence (BI) applications.
- Proposes Auto-Validate-by-History (AVH) that can automatically detect DQ issues in recurring pipelines, leveraging rich statistics from historical executions.
- Formalize this as an optimization problem, and develop constant-factor approximation algorithms with provable precision guarantees.
