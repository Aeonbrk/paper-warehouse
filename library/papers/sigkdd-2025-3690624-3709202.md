# Probabilistic Hypergraph Recurrent Neural Networks for Time-series Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Leveraging graph structures for time-series forecasting has been studied due to their relationship modeling between nodes and their associated time-series.
- Introduces a model, Probabilistic Hypergraph Recurrent Neural Networks (PHRNN), which leverages node-hyperedge dynamics for accurate time-series forecasting.
- Forecasting results from experiments show that PHRNN outperforms graph and hypergraph baselines on real-world datasets.
