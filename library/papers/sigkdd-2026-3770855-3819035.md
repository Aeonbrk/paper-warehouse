# VILLA: Versatile Information Retrieval from Scientific Literature Using Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The lack of high-quality ground truth datasets to train machine learning (ML) models impedes the potential of artificial intelligence for science research.
- Develops a new, multi-step retrieval augmented generation (RAG) framework called VILLA for SIE.
- In parallel, curate a dataset of 629 mutations in ten influenza A virus proteins obtained from 293 scientific publications to serve as ground truth for the mutation extraction task.
