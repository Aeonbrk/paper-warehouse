# FakeI2V-Bench: Benchmarking the Applicability of Image-level Deepfake Detectors for Deepfake Video Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent advances in video generation models have intensified the deepfake threat, yet the current deepfake video detection benchmarks remain underdeveloped.
- Presents IV-Bridge, a general framework that enhances the applicability of image-level deepfake detectors to videos.
- Presents FakeI2V-Bench: Benchmarking the Applicability of Image-level Deepfake Detectors for Deepfake Video Detection, a benchmark for evaluating video-level deepfake detectors in challenging scenarios, with a particular focus on systematically assessing the performance of image-level.
