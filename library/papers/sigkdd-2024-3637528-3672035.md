# GAugLLM: Improving Graph Contrastive Learning for Text-Attributed Graphs with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies self-supervised graph learning for text-attributed graphs (TAGs) where nodes are represented by textual attributes.
- Introduces GAugLLM, a framework for augmenting TAGs.
- Empirical results across five benchmark datasets spanning various domains underscore our framework's ability to enhance the performance of leading contrastive methods (e.g., BGRL, GraphCL, and GBT) as a plug-in tool.
