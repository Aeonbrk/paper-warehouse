# Broken Memories: Detecting and Mitigating Memorization in Diffusion Models with Degraded Generations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While diffusion models excel at generating high-quality images, their tendency to memorize training data poses privacy and copyright risks.
- Proposes a principled, on-the-fly framework for step-wise detection and adaptive mitigation.
- Experiments on Stable Diffusion 1.4 demonstrate that our method achieves an AUC >0.999 detection performance and a 0.0% memorization rate after mitigation with negligible overhead (≈0.01s per image).
