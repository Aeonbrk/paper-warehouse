# CATGX: Causal-Aware Temporal Graph Explanation via Scalable Motif Sampling and Adjustment

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Interpreting predictions of temporal graph neural networks (TGNNs) is challenging: structural patterns are entangled with temporal dynamics and node-level activity.
- Proposes CATGX, a confounder-aware framework for explaining temporal graph predictions through motif-level reasoning.
- Reports evaluation showing CATGX strikes a good balance to generate explanations that are faithful, grounded and confounder-aware, and outperform existing TGNN explainers in efficiency with 83x speed up.
