# Simplicial SMOTE: Oversampling Solution to the Imbalanced Learning Problem

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Oversampling Technique) is the established geometric approach to random oversampling to balance classes in the imbalanced learning problem, followed by many extensions.
- Its idea is to introduce synthetic data points of the minor class, with each new point being the convex combination of an existing data point and one of its k-nearest neighbors.
- Generalize and evaluate simplicial extensions of the classic Borderline SMOTE, Safe-level SMOTE, and ADASYN algorithms, all of which outperform their graph-based counterparts.
