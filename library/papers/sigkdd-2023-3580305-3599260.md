# Augmenting Recurrent Graph Neural Networks with a Cache

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While graph neural networks (GNNs) provide a way to learn structured representations, it remains challenging to learn long-range dependencies in graphs.
- Proposes a general approach for augmenting recurrent GNNs with a cache memory to improve their expressivity, especially for modeling long-range dependencies.
- Reports that the Cache-GNNs outperforms other models on synthetic datasets as well as tasks on real-world datasets that require long-range information.
