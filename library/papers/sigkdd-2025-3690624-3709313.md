# Contextual Generative Auction with Permutation-level Externalities for Online Advertising

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online advertising has become a core revenue driver for internet industry, with ad auctions playing a crucial role in ensuring platform revenue and advertiser incentives.
- Introduces Contextual Generative Auction (CGA), a framework that incorporates permutation-level externalities in multi-slot ad auctions.
- Offline and online experiments demonstrate that CGA enhances platform revenue and CTR compared to existing methods, and effectively approximates the optimal auction with nearly maximal revenue and minimal regret.
