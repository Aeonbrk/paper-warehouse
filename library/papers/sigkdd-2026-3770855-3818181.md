# LCATS: LLM-Guided Constraint-Aware Tabular Data Synthesis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Tabular data synthesis must satisfy two complementary quality criteria.
- Proposes LLM-Guided Constraint-Aware Tabular Data Synthesis (LCATS), an end-to-end framework that decouples global distribution modeling from local constraint enforcement.
- Experiments on seven benchmark datasets demonstrate that LCATS achieves strong overall performance against diffusion-based, LLM-based, and hybrid baselines in ?-precision, ?-recall, and downstream predictive performance, while producing zero constraint violations.
