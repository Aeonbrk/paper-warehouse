# Think Before Action: Learning Suction Health for Reliable Grasp Evaluation in Logistics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Evaluating suction cup grasp success before handling packages prevents costly drops and improves reliability in automated logistics, where large-scale operations handle packages with varying weights, sizes, and materials.
- Presents GraspGuard, a pre-grasp evaluation framework that enables logistics robots to think before they act by inferring suction health from historical grasp outcomes.
- They fail to account for the impact of suction cup health on grasp success, limiting their effectiveness in real-world logistics scenarios.
