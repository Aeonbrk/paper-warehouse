# Invariant Structure Learning with Pre-trained Language Models for Spatio-temporal Graph

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While Pre-trained Language Models (PLMs) have demonstrated potential in spatiotemporal forecasting, their deployment in critical real-world systems is hindered by a fundamental vulnerability, brittleness under distribution shifts.
- Proposes InvSTG-PLM, an invariance-aware spatiotemporal graph learning framework that grounds PLMs in invariant structures.
