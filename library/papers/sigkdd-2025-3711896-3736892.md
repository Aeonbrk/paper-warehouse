# Contrastive Graph Condensation: Advancing Data Versatility through Self-Supervised Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the increasing computation of training graph neural networks (GNNs) on large-scale graphs, graph condensation (GC) has emerged as a solution to synthesize compact, substitute graphs of the large-scale original graphs for efficient GNN.
- Introduces Contrastive Graph Condensation (CTGC), which adopts a self-supervised surrogate task to extract critical, causal information from the original graph and enhance the cross-task generalizability of the condensed graph.
