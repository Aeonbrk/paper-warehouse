# Experimentation Accelerator: Interpretable Insights and Creative Recommendations for A/B Testing with Content-Aware ranking

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modern online experimentation faces two bottlenecks: scarce traffic forces tough choices on which variants to test, and post-hoc insight extraction is manual, inconsistent, and often content-agnostic.
- Presents a unified framework to (i) prioritize which variants to test, (ii) explain why winners win, and (iii) surface targeted opportunities for new, higher-potential variants.
- Then compute an opportunity index combining attribute importance (from the ranker) with under-expression in the current experiment to flag missing, high-impact attributes.
