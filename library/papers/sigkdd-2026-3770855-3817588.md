# T-ECD: A Large-Scale Cross-Domain E-Commerce Dataset for Industrial Recommender Systems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Introduces T-ECD, an open large-scale dataset for recommender systems research, containing over 135 billion interactions from 44 million users and 30 million items.
- Crucially, the dataset maintains cross-domain consistency through aligned user and brand identifiers, enabling research on transfer learning, cross-domain recommendation, and multi-task modeling under realistic industrial conditions.
- The dataset is derived from anonymized real-world data within a unified banking ecosystem and spans five interconnected e-commerce domains: marketplace, retail, payments, promotional offers, and reviews.
