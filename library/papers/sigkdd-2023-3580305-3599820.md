# Explicit Feature Interaction-aware Uplift Network for Online Marketing

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- As a key component in online marketing, uplift modeling aims to accurately capture the degree to which different treatments motivate different users, such as coupons or discounts, also known as the estimation of individual.
- Proposes an explicit feature interaction-aware uplift network (EFIN) to address these two problems.
- Conduct experiments on two public datasets and one product dataset to verify the effectiveness of our EFIN.
