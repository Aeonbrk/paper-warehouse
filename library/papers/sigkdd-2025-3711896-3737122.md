# Self-Tuning Self-Supervised Image Anomaly Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Self-supervised learning (SSL) has emerged as a paradigm that presents supervisory signals to real-world problems, bypassing the cost of manual labeling.
- Introduces ST-SSAD, the first unsupervised approach to end-to-end augmentation tuning for SSAD.
- Recent works have reported that the choice of a data augmentation function has impact on the accuracy of SSAD, posing augmentation search as an essential but nontrivial problem due to lack of labeled validation.
