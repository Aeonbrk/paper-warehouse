# GDendrite: On Heterophilous Graph Contexts Mining with Versatile Neural Dendrites Framework

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- A challenge for Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) lies in accurately performing node classification tasks on heterophilous graphs.
- Introduces the GDendrite framework, which dynamically adjusts GNN's output response to the various contexts.
- Our empirical studies, conducted across 13 homophilous and heterophilous benchmark datasets, show that our framework enhances the performance of traditional GNNs in heterophilous settings and outperforms models in most cases.
