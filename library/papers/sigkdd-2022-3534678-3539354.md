# Make Fairness More Fair: Fair Item Utility Estimation and Exposure Re-Distribution

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The item fairness issue has become one of the concerns with the development of recommender systems in recent years, focusing on whether items' exposures are consistent with their utilities.
- Presents a fairness-aware re-distribution framework and conduct abundant simulation experiments, adopting fair utility to improve fairness and overall recommendation performance at the same time.
- Firstly, conduct a large-scale random exposure experiment to collect the fair utility in a real-world recommender application.
