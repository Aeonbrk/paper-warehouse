# Effective and Robust Single-cell Cross-omics Annotation via Vector-Quantized Autoencoders

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Integrating unpaired single-cell RNA-seq and ATAC-seq data remains challenging due to the profound differences in data sparsity and noise between them.
- Presents a discrete representation learning based method for and robust cross-omics cell-type annotation, which is called scCoA-VQA --- the abbreviation of single-cell Cross-omics Annotation via Vector-Quantized Autoencoders.
- Experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets show that scCoA-VQA consistently outperforms existing methods in accuracy, F1-Macro, and robustness to extreme sparsity.
