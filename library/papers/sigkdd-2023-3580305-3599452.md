# One-shot Joint Extraction, Registration and Segmentation of Neuroimaging Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Brain extraction, registration and segmentation are indispensable preprocessing steps in neuroimaging studies.
- Proposes a unified end-to-end framework, called JERS, to jointly optimize the extraction, registration and segmentation tasks, allowing feedback among them.
- Empirical results on real-world datasets demonstrate that our proposed method performs exceptionally in the extraction, registration and segmentation tasks.
