# A Context based Personalized Deep Network for Nearby Flight Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the flourishing development of aviation and the convenience of booking flights online, nearby flight recommendation has become the core business of Online Travel Platforms (OTPs).
- Presents a Context Potential Gain Learning (CPGL) component, where the important cost between target nearby flight and context flights are emphasized and learned.
- Offline experiments on a production dataset and a world-scale online A/B test at Fliggy.
