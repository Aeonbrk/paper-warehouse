# RMEval: Bridging Reward Model Evaluation and Policy Evaluation through Ranking Consistency

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Reward Models (RMs) are widely used to guide the alignment of Large Language Models (LLMs) in Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF).
- Proposes RMEval, a benchmark that revisits reward model evaluation through the lens of global ranking consistency.
- In current practice, reward models are predominantly evaluated using local metrics such as pairwise accuracy on validation sets.
