# Deformation Localization Theory Guided Transfer Learning for Mining-induced Seismicity Risk Zone Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Mining-induced seismicity (MIS) poses a critical challenge to deep mining safety.
- A deformation localization theory-guided transfer learning (DLT-TL) method is proposed for MIS risk zone prediction.
- Finally, two case studies demonstrate that DLT-TL outperforms baselines, achieving F1-score gains of 6.53% and 2.28% on the source laboratory and target field datasets, respectively.
