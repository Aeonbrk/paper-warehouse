# A Serial Two-Stage Framework for Robust Multimodal Fake News Detection via Adaptive Reasoning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The proliferation of social media has created fertile ground for misinformation, a challenge further intensified by recent advances in generative artificial intelligence.
- Proposes DAR-Lite, a serial two-stage framework that rethinks the detection pipeline through explicit decoupling of representation denoising and contextual reasoning.
- Despite substantial progress, real-world deployment of multimodal fake news detection models remains constrained by an ''impossible triangle'' of accuracy, inference efficiency, and robustness.
