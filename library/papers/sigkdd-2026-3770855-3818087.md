# Expectations vs. Realities: The Cost of MSE-Optimal Forecasting Under Conditional Uncertainty

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multi-step time series forecasting (MSF) is commonly evaluated using point-wise error metrics such as mean squared error (MSE), implicitly treating the conditional mean as a sufficient target.
- This establishes a fundamental, model-agnostic trade-off between point accuracy and marginal realism in MSF evaluation.
- Using controlled stochastic dynamical systems and nine real-world forecasting benchmarks, empirically characterize the resulting accuracy--realism frontier and quantify the practical cost of MSE-only model selection.
