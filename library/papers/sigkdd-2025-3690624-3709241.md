# Large-Scale Spectral Graph Neural Networks via Laplacian Sparsification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) play a role in graph-based tasks for their proficiency in representation learning.
- Proposes ''Spectral Graph Neural Networks with Laplacian Sparsification (SGNN-LS)'', a graph spectral sparsification method to approximate the propagation patterns of spectral GNNs.
- The results show that our method yields results in comparison with the corresponding approximated base models, especially on dataset Ogbn-papers100M(111M nodes, 1.6B edges) and MAG-scholar-C (2.8M features).
