# Persona Identification in E-Commerce with Scarce Labels and In-Context Graph Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- User personas play a crucial role in shaping shopping behavior, making persona identification an essential task for e-commerce platforms to personalize user experiences.
- Proposes TRIPER, a TRIpartite graph neural network specifically designed to enhance multi-label PERsona classification along with in-context inference capabilities.
- Evaluations on our real-world dataset demonstrate that TRIPER achieves high predictive accuracy in user persona identification, exhibits strong generalization over time, and effectively learns from limited labeled data, outperforming baselines.
