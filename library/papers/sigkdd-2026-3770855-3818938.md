# Hybrid Graph Learning Reconstructs Global Ocean Oxygen Spatiotemporal Changes

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Climate change and anthropogenic activities have exacerbated hypoxic conditions in the global ocean, posing a serious threat to marine ecosystems.
- Here present Jingwei, a 4D spatiotemporal graph transfer learning model that reconstructs ocean oxygen levels globally over the past six decades on a 1◦ x 1◦ grid, covering depths of 0-5500 meters.
- Alongside, have released an open-source online platform (https://jingwei.acemap.info/ https://jingwei.acemap.info/), providing data visualization, resource sharing and ongoing updates.
