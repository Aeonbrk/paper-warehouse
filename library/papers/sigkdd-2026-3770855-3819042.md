# Driving Reaction Trajectories via Latent Flow Matching

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent advances in reaction prediction have achieved near-saturated accuracy on standard benchmarks (e.g., USPTO), yet most models formulate the task as a one-shot mapping from reactants to products, offering limited insight into the underlying.
- Procedural alternatives introduce stepwise generation but often rely on mechanism-specific supervision, discrete symbolic edits, and computationally expensive inference.
- LatentRxnFlow combines strong predictive accuracy with transparency, diagnosability, and uncertainty awareness, moving reaction prediction toward more trustworthy high-throughput discovery workflows.
