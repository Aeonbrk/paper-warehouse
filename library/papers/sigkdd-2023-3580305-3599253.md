# Adaptive Disentangled Transformer for Sequential Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Sequential recommendation aims at mining time-aware user interests through modeling sequential behaviors.
- Studies the problem of layer-wise disentanglement for Transformer architectures and propose the Adaptive Disentangled Transformer (ADT) framework, which is able to adaptively determine the optimal degree of disentanglement of attention heads within different layers.
- Experiments on various real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed ADT framework.
