# An Adaptive Tabular-guided Layerwise Alignment and Synthesis Network for Multimodal Neuroimaging and Clinical Phenotype Fusion

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Brain disorders involve abnormalities spanning multiple biological and phenotypic scales that require the integration of multiple data sources, including neuroimaging and structured clinical data, to achieve accurate diagnosis.
- Proposes an Adaptive Tabular-guided Layerwise Alignment and Synthesis Network (ATLAS-Net) for multimodal neuroimaging and tabular data integration.
- Experiments on 8 brain disorders demonstrate that ATLAS-Net consistently outperforms state-of-the-arts in classification performance, cross-dataset generalization, and robustness to realistic spatial noise perturbations.
