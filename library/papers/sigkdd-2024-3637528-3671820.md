# Revisiting Local PageRank Estimation on Undirected Graphs: Simple and Optimal

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes a simple and optimal algorithm, BackMC, for local PageRank estimation in undirected graphs: given an arbitrary target node t in an undirected graph G comprising n nodes and m edges, BackMC accurately estimates.
- Compared to the previously best upper bound of O(log n ⋅ min(dt, m1/2)) (VLDB '23).
- Conduct experiments on various large-scale real-world and synthetic graphs, where BackMC consistently shows performance.
