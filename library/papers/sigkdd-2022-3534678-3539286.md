# JuryGCN: Quantifying Jackknife Uncertainty on Graph Convolutional Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) has exhibited strong empirical performance in many real-world applications.
- Proposes the first frequentist-based approach named JuryGCN in quantifying the uncertainty of GCN.
- The vast majority of existing works on GCN primarily focus on the accuracy while ignoring how confident or uncertain a GCN is with respect to its predictions.
