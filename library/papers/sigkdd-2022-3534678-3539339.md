# Local Evaluation of Time Series Anomaly Detection Algorithms

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Specific evaluation metrics for time series anomaly detection algorithms have been developed to handle the limitations of the classical precision and recall.
- Such metrics are heuristically built as an aggregate of multiple desirable aspects, introduce parameters and wipe out the interpretability of the output.
- Reports that an adversary algorithm can reach high precision and recall on almost any dataset under weak assumption.
