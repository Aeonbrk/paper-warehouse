# A Fraudulent Blind Shipment Detection Framework in Logistics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- An emerging type of fraud involves malicious senders exploiting the blind shipment and cash-on-delivery (COD) mechanisms by dispatching large volumes of unsolicited, low-cost parcels.
- Designs BLOFF, a Blind shipment detection Framework for LO gistics Fraud powered by large language models (LLMs).
- Furthermore, during its real-world deployment in December 2024, BLOFF identified over 90 fraudulent parcels with a 91.4% precision.
