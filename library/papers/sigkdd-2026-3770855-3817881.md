# Lifting the Veil of Non-Stationarity in Financial Market

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Financial asset price movement prediction is inherently challenging due to the non-stationary nature of financial markets, where data distributions shift over time.
- Proposes the Market-State Jump Diffusion Framework (MSJD), which models non-stationarity through two key components: an Explicit Market-State Jump Diffusion Process (EMJD) and an Implicit Market-State Jump Diffusion Process (IMJD).
- Experiments on real-world stock and cryptocurrency datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches in the prediction of price movements.
