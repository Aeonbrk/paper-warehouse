# TeleCom-Bench: How Far Are Large Language Models from Industrial Telecommunication Applications?

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While Large Language Models have achieved integration in various vertical scenarios, their deployment in the telecommunications domain remains exploratory due to the lack of a standardized evaluation framework.
- Presents TeleCom-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark comprising 12 evaluation sets with 22,678 curated samples.
- Current telecom benchmarks primarily focus on static, foundational knowledge and isolated "atomic" skills, neglecting the equipment-specific documentation and end-to-end industrial workflows essential for real-world production systems.
