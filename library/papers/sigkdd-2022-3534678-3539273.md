# Continuous-Time and Multi-Level Graph Representation Learning for Origin-Destination Demand Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Traffic demand forecasting by deep neural networks has attracted widespread interest in both academia and industry society.
- Proposes a Continuous-time and Multi-level dynamic graph representation learning method for Origin-Destination demand prediction (CMOD).
- Experiments are conducted on two real-world datasets from Beijing Subway and New York Taxi, and the results report higher performance for our model against the approaches.
