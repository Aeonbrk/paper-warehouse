# Causal Scaffolding for Physical Reasoning: A Benchmark for Causally-Informed Physical World Understanding in VLMs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Understanding and reasoning about the physical world is the foundation of intelligent behavior, yet vision-language models (VLMs) still fail at causal physical reasoning, often producing plausible but incorrect answers.
- Introduces CausalPhys, a benchmark of over 3,000 carefully curated video- and image-based questions spanning four domains: Perception, Anticipation, Intervention, and Goal Orientation.
