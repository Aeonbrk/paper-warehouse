# Incorporating 3D Structural Information for Activity Cliff Analysis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Artificial intelligence has become a crucial tool in drug discovery, excelling in tasks such as molecular property prediction.
- The limited availability of activity cliff data for target-ligand 3D complexes constrains the predictive power of modern deep learning models.
- Introduces DockedAC, a new dataset incorporating the protein target and 3D complex structure information to enable geometric reasoning in activity cliff analysis.
