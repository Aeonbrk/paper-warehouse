# CaCuTe: Casual Cubic-Model Technique for Faster Optimization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Establish a local O(k-2) rate for the gradient update xk+1 = xk - ∇f(xk)/√(H||∇f(xk)||) under a 2H-Hessian-Lipschitz assumption.
- In the first-order setting, the technique yields a monotone, adaptive, parameter-free method that inherits the local O(k-2) rate.
