# UniHam: A Large-Scale SOC-Complete Dataset and Benchmark for Hamiltonian Learning in Materials

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate prediction of electronic Hamiltonians would enable broad property inference while avoiding the high computational cost of Density Functional Theory (DFT).
- Building on UniHam, benchmark two representative models under a standardized protocol and introduce complementary evaluation metrics that jointly assess three dimensions: (i) Hamiltonian reconstruction accuracy, (ii) out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization across composition/symmetry shifts.
- Therefore construct UniHam, a large-scale Hamiltonian dataset and benchmark suite comprising 100,000+ DFT-computed complex-valued Hermitian Hamiltonians with full SOC, covering 72 elements and a wide range of crystal geometries and symmetries (spanning diverse lattice.
