# Neural Collapse Inspired Debiased Representation Learning for Min-max Fairness

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Although machine learning algorithms demonstrate impressive performance, their trustworthiness remains a critical issue, particularly concerning fairness when implemented in real-world applications.
- The layer-peeled model is employed to decompose a network into two parts: an encoder to learn latent representation, and a subsequent classifier, with a systematic characterization of their training behaviors being conducted.
- The experimental assessments carried out on real-world benchmark datasets spanning the domains of Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, and Tabular data demonstrate that our approach outperforms existing methods in promoting the Min-max fairness notion.
