# Partial-Quasi-Newton Methods: Efficient Algorithms for Minimax Optimization Problems with Unbalanced Dimensionality

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies the strongly-convex-strongly-concave minimax optimization with unbalanced dimensionality.
- Proposes a second-order optimization algorithm, called Partial-Quasi-Newton (PQN) method, which takes the advantage of unbalanced structure in the problem to establish the Hessian estimate efficiently.
- The numerical experiments on real-world applications show the proposed PQN performs better than the methods.
