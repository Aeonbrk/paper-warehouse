# Rethinking Fair Graph Neural Networks from Re-balancing

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Driven by the representation ability of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), plentiful GNN models have been widely deployed in many real-world applications.
- Proposes FairGB, Fair Graph Neural Network via re-Balancing, which mitigates the unfairness of GNNs by group balancing.
- Experimental results on benchmark datasets show that our method can achieve results concerning both utility and fairness metrics.
