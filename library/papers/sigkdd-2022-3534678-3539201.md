# DDR: Dialogue Based Doctor Recommendation for Online Medical Service

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online medical consultation, which enables patients to remotely inquire doctors in the form of web chatting, has become an indispensable part of the social health care system.
- Proposes to simulate the dialogue embedding with patient embedding via a contrastive learning based module.
- Experimental results on a real-world data set show that the proposed DDR model can outperform recommendation-based methods.
