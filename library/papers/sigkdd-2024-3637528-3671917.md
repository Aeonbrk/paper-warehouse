# Graph Condensation for Open-World Graph Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The burgeoning volume of graph data presents computational challenges in training graph neural networks (GNNs), critically impeding their efficiency in various applications.
- Proposes open-world graph condensation (OpenGC), a robust GC framework that integrates structure-aware distribution shift to simulate evolving graph patterns and exploit the temporal environments for invariance condensation.
- In real-world scenarios, however, graphs are dynamic and constantly evolving, with new nodes and edges being continually integrated.
