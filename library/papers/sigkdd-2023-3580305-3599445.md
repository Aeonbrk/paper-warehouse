# Neural-Hidden-CRF: A Robust Weakly-Supervised Sequence Labeler

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes a neuralized undirected graphical model called Neural-Hidden-CRF to solve the weakly-supervised sequence labeling problem.
- In Neural-Hidden-CRF, can capitalize on the language model BERT or other deep models to provide rich contextual semantic knowledge to the latent ground truth sequence.
- It obtains new results on one crowdsourcing benchmark and three weak-supervision benchmarks, including outperforming the recent advanced model CHMM by 2.80 F1 points and 2.23 F1 points in average generalization and inference performance, respectively.
