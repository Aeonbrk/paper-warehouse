# Learning the Covariance of Treatment Effects Across Many Weak Experiments

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- When primary objectives are insensitive or delayed, experimenters may instead focus on proxy metrics derived from secondary outcomes.
- Present a total covariance estimator for the latter estimand under homoskedasticity, which is equivalent to a k-class estimator.
- Lastly, discuss the real-world application of our methods at Netflix.
