# ROI-Constrained Bidding via Curriculum-Guided Bayesian Reinforcement Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Real-Time Bidding (RTB) is an important mechanism in modern online advertising systems.
- Based on a Partially Observable Constrained Markov Decision Process, our method exploits an indicator-augmented reward function free of extra trade-off parameters and develops a Curriculum-Guided Bayesian Reinforcement Learning (CBRL) framework to adaptively control the.
- Experiments on a large-scale industrial dataset with two problem settings reveal that CBRL generalizes well in both in-distribution and out-of-distribution data regimes, and enjoys learning efficiency and stability.
