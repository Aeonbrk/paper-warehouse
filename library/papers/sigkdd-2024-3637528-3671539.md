# Leveraging Exposure Networks for Detecting Fake News Sources

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The scale and dynamic nature of the Web makes real-time detection of misinformation an extremely difficult task.
- Develops a machine-learning methodology for detecting fake news sources using active learning, and examine the contribution of network, audience, and text features to the model accuracy.
- Find that exposure networks provide information on considerably more sources than sharing networks (+49.6%), and that the inclusion of exposure features greatly improves classification PR-AUC in both offline (+33%) and online (+69.2%) settings.
