# CutAddPaste: Time Series Anomaly Detection by Exploiting Abnormal Knowledge

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Detecting time-series anomalies is extremely intricate due to the rarity of anomalies and imbalanced sample categories, which often result in costly and challenging anomaly labeling.
- Introduces CutAddPaste, a anomaly assumption-based approach for detecting time-series anomalies.
- Experiments on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms the approaches.
