# Dynamic Discard Deep Learning for Rice Yield Prediction on Mixed-Accuracy Datasets

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Fusing limited high-accuracy observations with abundant low-accuracy simulations is a recurring challenge in agriculture, medicine, and remote sensing.
- Proposes Dynamic Discard Deep Learning (DDL), a framework that combines soft probabilistic discard with phased training control and adaptive task weighting.
- Low-accuracy samples receive data-dependent discard probabilities capped by pmax , while high-accuracy warmup and bounded low-accuracy introduction stabilize the training loss trajectory.
