# Graph Joint Representation Learning for Sound and Scalable Instantiation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Knowledge graph reasoning is a fundamental problem in artificial intelligence.
- Proposes Rule–Graph Joint Representation Learning (RGRS), a unified neuro-symbolic framework that integrates rule-level reasoning with graph representation learning for scalable Horn rule instantiation.
- Experiments on large-scale knowledge graphs show that RGRS achieves consistently high accuracy with efficiency gains over symbolic and neuro-symbolic baselines, providing a principled bridge between symbolic reasoning and embedding-based efficiency.
