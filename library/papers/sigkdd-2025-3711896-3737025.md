# Leveraging the Power of Conversations: Optimal Key Term Selection in Conversational Contextual Bandits

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Conversational recommender systems proactively query users with relevant ''key terms'' and leverage the feedback to elicit users' preferences for personalized recommendations.
- Conversational contextual bandits, a prevalent approach in this domain, aim to optimize preference learning by balancing exploitation and exploration.
- Several limitations hinder their effectiveness in real-world scenarios.
