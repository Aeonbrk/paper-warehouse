# Battery-Sim-Agent: Leveraging LLM-Agent for Inverse Battery Parameter Estimation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Parameterizing high-fidelity "digital twins" of batteries is a critical yet challenging inverse problem that hinders the pace of battery innovation.
- Introduces a new paradigm that reframes the inverse problem as a reasoning task.
- On a systematically constructed benchmark suite spanning diverse battery chemistries, operating conditions, and difficulty levels, our agent outperforms strong BBO baselines like Bayesian optimization in identifying accurate parameters.
