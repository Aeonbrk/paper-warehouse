# Bilateral Dependency Optimization: Defending Against Model-inversion Attacks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Through using only a well-trained classifier, model-inversion (MI) attacks can recover the data used for training the classifier, leading to the privacy leakage of the training data.
- Such a minimization process conflicts with minimizing the supervised loss that aims to maximize the dependency between inputs and outputs, causing an explicit trade-off between model robustness against MI attacks and model utility on.
- Reports evaluation showing BiDO achieves the defense performance for a variety of datasets, classifiers.
