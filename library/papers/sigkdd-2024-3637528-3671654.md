# Text Matching Indexers in Taobao Search

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Product search is an important service on Taobao, the largest e-commerce platform in China.
- Develops an efficient TermRank algorithm applied to these hypergraphs, to preserve relevant items based on specific user preferences during the pruning of the inverted indexer.
- Empirical results from both offline evaluation and online A/B tests showcase the superiority of SMIND over methods, especially in commerce metrics with improvements of 1.34% in Pay Order Count and 1.50% in Gross Merchandise.
