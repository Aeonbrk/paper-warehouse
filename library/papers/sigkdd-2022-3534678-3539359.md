# Knowledge-enhanced Black-box Attacks for Recommendations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent studies have shown that deep neural networks-based recommender systems are vulnerable to adversarial attacks.
- Proposes a knowledge graph-enhanced black-box attacking framework (KGAttack) to effectively learn attacking policies through deep reinforcement learning techniques, in which knowledge graph is seamlessly integrated into hierarchical policy networks to generate fake user profiles.
- Comprehensive experiments on various real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed attacking framework under the black-box setting.
