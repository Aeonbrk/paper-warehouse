# On Missing Labels, Long-tails and Propensities in Extreme Multi-label Classification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The propensity model introduced by Jain et al has become a standard approach for dealing with missing and long-tail labels in extreme multi-label classification (XMLC).
- Exhaustively discuss the flaws of the propensity-based approach, and present several recipes, some of them related to solutions used in search engines and recommender systems, that believe constitute alternatives to be followed in XMLC.
