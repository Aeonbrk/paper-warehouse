# Modeling Time-evolving Causality over Data Streams

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Given an , semi-infinite collection of multivariate coevolving data sequences (e.g., sensor/web activity streams) whose observations influence each other, how can discover the time-changing cause-and-effect relationships in co-evolving data streams?
- Presents a streaming method, ModePlait, which is designed for modeling such causal relationships (i.e., time-evolving causality) in multivariate co-evolving data streams and forecasting their future values.
- Experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that our proposed model outperforms methods in terms of discovering the time-evolving causality as well as forecasting.
