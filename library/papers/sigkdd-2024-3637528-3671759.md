# Adapting Job Recommendations to User Preference Drift with Behavioral-Semantic Fusion Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Job recommender systems are crucial for aligning job opportunities with job-seekers in online job-seeking.
- Proposes a session-based framework, BISTRO, to timely model user preference through fusion learning of semantic and behavioral information.
- Experiments on three real-world offline recruitment datasets demonstrate the performances of our framework.
