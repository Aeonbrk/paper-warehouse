# Beyond Isolated Bidding: Campaign-Channel Coordination for Global Optimal Returns in Online Advertising

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Automated bidding is a core component of modern online advertising systems.
- Proposes Campaign–Channel Coordinated Bidding (C3Bid), a bias-aware automated bidding framework that jointly optimizes final bidding decisions by coordinating campaign and channels under a unified campaign-level objective and constraints.
- Offline experiments and large-scale online A/B tests in a real-world advertising system demonstrate consistent and gains in conversions, budget utilization, and bidding stability under CPA constraints.
