## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Presents an algorithm named AutoInterest, which is a modification of the dual gradient descent algorithm.
- In addition, provide a framework to evaluate interest rate bidding strategies on a benchmark dataset and the credit bureau dataset of actual loan applicants in South Korea.
