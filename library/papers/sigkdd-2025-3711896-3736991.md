# GraphLAMA: Enabling Efficient Adaptation of Graph Language Models with Limited Annotations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated their strong capabilities in various domains, and have been recently integrated for graph analysis as graph language models (GLMs).
- For implementation, in this paper propose GraphLAMA method, with its model backbone and learning schemes specialized for efficient tuning and inference.
- Meanwhile, the large amount of labeled data required for instruction tuning can be difficult to obtain in real-world scenarios.
