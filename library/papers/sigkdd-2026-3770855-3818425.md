# GPR: Towards a Generative Pre-trained One-Model Paradigm for Large-Scale Advertising Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online advertising recommendation is the economic engine of the digital ecosystem, yet traditional multi-stage cascading systems suffer from objective misalignment and error propagation.
- Unlike prior works, GPR introduces three key technical innovations to ensure industrial feasibility: (1) Unified Representation: utilize RQ-KMeans+ to map heterogeneous user behaviors and ad content into a shared semantic ID space.
- Online A/B testing demonstrates that GPR delivers substantial gains in GMV and CTCVR over highly optimized cascading baselines, validating the generative paradigm as a solution for large-scale advertising systems.
