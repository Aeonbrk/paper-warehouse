# SIGHP: Scalable Information-Guided Hypergraph Partitioner

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large-scale hypergraph analysis is crucial for modeling complex higher-order relationships in modern data-intensive applications, yet existing distributed computing solutions face scalability challenges.
- Introduces SIGHP (Scalable Information-Guided Hypergraph Partitioner), a approach that leverages the discovered correlation between partitioning quality and intra-partition vertex co-occurrence information.
- Our comprehensive evaluation on real-world hypergraphs demonstrates SIGHP's performance, achieving up to 40% reduction in communication costs, 60% lower memory overhead, and 10× faster partitioning speed compared to methods.
