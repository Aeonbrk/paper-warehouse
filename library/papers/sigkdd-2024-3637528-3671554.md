# Dólares or Dollars? Unraveling the Bilingual Prowess of Financial LLMs Between Spanish and English

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Despite Spanish's role in the global finance industry, a pronounced gap exists in Spanish financial natural language processing (NLP) and application studies compared to English, especially in the era of large language models.
- To bridge this gap, unveil Toisón de Oro, the first bilingual framework that establishes instruction datasets, finetuned LLMs, and evaluation benchmark for financial LLMs in Spanish joint with English.
