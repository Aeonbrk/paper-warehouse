# Diffusion-Guided Diversity for Single Domain Generalization in Time Series Classification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Single-domain generalization (SDG) in time series classification (TSC) poses challenges for current time-series domain generalization methods due to the extremely limited data available from only one source domain.
- Proposes Segment-dErived Expansion of Domains (SEED), a diffusion-based method that effectively expands domain diversity for SDG.
- In our experiments, SEED improves classification accuracy by 7.68% on average compared to the strong baselines.
