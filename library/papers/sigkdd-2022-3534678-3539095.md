# Reinforcement Learning in the Wild: Scalable RL Dispatching Algorithm Deployed in Ridehailing Marketplace

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In this study, a scalable and real-time dispatching algorithm based on reinforcement learning is proposed and for the first time, is deployed in large scale.
- The present study proposes a standalone RL-based dispatching solution that is equipped with multiple mechanisms to ensure robust and efficient on-policy learning and inference while being adaptable for full-scale deployment.
- In addition, the algorithm is deployed online in multiple cities under DiDi's operation for A/B testing and more recently, is launched in one of the major international markets as the primary mode of dispatch.
