# Trajectory-Aware Clinical Risk Prediction via Severity-Grounded Knowledge Graphs and Retrieval-Augmented Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While Electronic Health Records (EHRs) offer a wealth of clinical data, effectively augmenting a patient's records with heterogeneous external knowledge to predict the patient's clinical risk remains a challenge.
- Proposes TRACER (a trajectory-aware and clinically grounded prediction framework) that (1) constructs a medical knowledge graph enriched with severity information from medical literature, (2) retrieves clinically relevant, severity-weighted paths of a patient's progression from.
- Experiments on the MIMIC-III and MIMIC-IV datasets demonstrate large gains over baselines, with up to (28.5%) increase in Macro F1 score for the mortality prediction task, and (19.7%) increase for the readmission prediction task.
