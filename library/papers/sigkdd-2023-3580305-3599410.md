# Learning Strong Graph Neural Networks with Weak Information

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have exhibited impressive performance in many graph learning tasks.
- To fill the gap, in this paper, aim to develop an and principled approach to the problem of graph learning with weak information (GLWI).
- Experiments on eight real-world benchmark datasets demonstrate the effectiveness and efficiency of our proposed methods in various GLWI scenarios.
