# Scenario-Adaptive Feature Interaction for Click-Through Rate Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Traditional Click-Through Rate (CTR) prediction models are usually trained and deployed in a single scenario.
- Recent studies have proved that learning a unified model to serve multiple scenarios is in improving the overall performance.
- Have deployed the model in WeChat Official Account Platform and have seen more than 2.84% online CTR increase on average in three major scenarios.
