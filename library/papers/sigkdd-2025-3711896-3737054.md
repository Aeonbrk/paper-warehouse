# Monitoring Robustness and Individual Fairness

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In automated decision-making, it is desirable that outputs of decision-makers be robust to slight perturbations in their inputs, a property that may be called input-output robustness.
- Proposes runtime monitoring of input-output robustness of deployed, black-box AI models.
- Reports that the monitoring problem can be cast as the fixed-radius nearest neighbor (FRNN) search problem, which, despite being well-studied, lacks suitable online solutions.
