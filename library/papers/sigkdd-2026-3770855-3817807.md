# SafeCRS: Personalized Safety Alignment for LLM-Based Conversational Recommender Systems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Current LLM-based conversational recommender systems (CRS) primarily optimize recommendation accuracy and user satisfaction.
- Proposes SafeCRS, a safety-aware training framework that integrates Safe Supervised Fine-Tuning (Safe-SFT) with Safe Group reward–Decoupled Normalization Policy Optimization (Safe-GDPO) to jointly optimize recommendation quality and personalized safety alignment.
- Formalize this challenge as personalized CRS safety and introduce SafeRec, a new benchmark dataset designed to systematically evaluate safety risks in LLM-based CRS under user-specific constraints.
