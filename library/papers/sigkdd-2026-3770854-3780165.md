# FaST: Efficient and Effective Long-Horizon Forecasting for Large-Scale Spatial-Temporal Graphs via Mixture-of-Experts

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spatial-Temporal Graph (STG) forecasting on large-scale networks has been studied.
- Presents FaST, an and efficient framework based on heterogeneity-aware Mixture-of-Experts (MoEs) for long-horizon and large-scale STG forecasting, which unlocks oneweek-ahead (672 steps at a 15-minute granularity) prediction with thousands of nodes.
- Experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that FaST not only delivers long-horizon predictive accuracy but also achieves computational efficiency compared to baselines.
