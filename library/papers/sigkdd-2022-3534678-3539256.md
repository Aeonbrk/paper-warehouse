# pureGAM: Learning an Inherently Pure Additive Model

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Including pairwise or higher-order interactions among predictors of a Generalized Additive Model (GAM) is gaining increasing attention in the literature.
- Proposes pureGAM, an inherently pure additive model of both main effects and higher-order interactions.
- By imposing thepureness condition to constrain each component function, pureGAM is proved to be identifiable without compromising accuracy.
