# Improving Long-tail User CTR Prediction via Hierarchical Distribution Alignment

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Click-Through Rate (CTR) prediction is a fundamental task in online advertising and recommender systems, requiring the modeling of feature interactions.
- Proposes a framework that enhances long-tail user performance through hierarchical distribution alignment, hierarchical residual learning, and adaptive distrbution calibration.
- Experiments on public datasets and an online experiment demonstrate that our approach improves accuracy and fairness for long-tail users while maintaining similar or even better overall performance.
