# AdaRD: An Adaptive Response Denoising Framework for Robust Learner Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Learner modeling is a crucial task in online learning environments, where Cognitive Diagnosis Models (CDMs) are employed to assess learners' knowledge mastery levels based on recorded response logs.
- Proposes a general framework, Adaptive Response Denoising (AdaRD), designed to salvage CDMs from the influence of noisy learner-exercise responses.
- The existence of noise degrades the accuracy of diagnosis results and learner performance predictions.
