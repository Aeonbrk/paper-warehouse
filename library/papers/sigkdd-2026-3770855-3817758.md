# Conditional PED-ANOVA: Hyperparameter Importance in Hierarchical & Dynamic Search Spaces

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes conditional PED-ANOVA (condPED-ANOVA), a principled framework for estimating hyperparameter importance (HPI) in conditional search spaces, where the presence or domain of a hyperparameter can depend on other hyperparameters.
- Introduces a conditional HPI for top-performing regions and derive a closed-form estimator that accurately reflects conditional activation and domain changes.
