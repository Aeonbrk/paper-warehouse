# Enhancing Large Language Models for Mobility Analytics with Semantic Location Tokenization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The widespread adoption of location-based services has led to the generation of vast amounts of mobility data, providing opportunities to model user movement dynamics within urban environments.
- Proposes QT-Mob, a framework that enhances LLMs for mobility analytics.
- Experiments on three real-world dataset demonstrate the performance in both next-location prediction and mobility recovery tasks, outperforming existing deep learning and LLM-based methods.
