# PhyGHT: Physics-Guided HyperGraph Transformer for Signal Purification at the HL-LHC

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The High-Luminosity Large Hadron Collider (HL-LHC) at CERN will produce unprecedented datasets capable of revealing fundamental properties of the universe.
- Introduces the Physics-Guided Hypergraph Transformer (PhyGHT), a hybrid architecture that combines distance-aware local graph attention with global self-attention to mirror the physical topology of particle showers formed in proton-proton collisions.
- To validate our approach, release a simulated dataset of top-quark pair production to model extreme pileup conditions.
