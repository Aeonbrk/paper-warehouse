# DIET: Customized Slimming for Incompatible Networks in Sequential Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Due to the continuously improving capabilities of mobile edges, recommender systems start to deploy models on edges to alleviate network congestion caused by frequent mobile requests.
- Further explore the repeated modules within networks and proposes a more storage-efficient framework, DIETING, which utilizes a single layer of parameters to represent the entire network, achieving comparably excellent performance.
- The experiments across four datasets and two widely used models demonstrate the accuracy in recommendation and efficiency in transmission and storage of our framework.
