# ChemHGNN: A Hierarchical Hypergraph Neural Network for Reaction Virtual Screening and Discovery

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Reaction virtual screening and discovery are fundamental challenges in chemistry and material science, where traditional graph neural networks (GNNs) struggle to model multi-reactant interactions.
- To address key challenges—such as combinatorial explosion, model collapse, and chemically invalid negative samples—introduce a reaction center-aware negative sampling strategy (RCNS) and a hierarchical embedding approach combining molecule, reaction and hypergraph level features.
- Experiments on the USPTO dataset demonstrate that ChemHGNN outperforms HGNN and GNN baselines, particularly in large-scale settings, while maintaining interpretability and chemical plausibility.
