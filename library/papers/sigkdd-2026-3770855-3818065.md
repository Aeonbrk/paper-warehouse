# RaceMED: A Race-Aware Approach to Accurate and Fair Medication Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Clinical evidence indicates that (1) disease prevalence differs across racial groups and (2) medication prescriptions for identical diagnoses and procedures may vary across racial groups.
- Existing medication recommendation methods have not integrated these racial properties into the design of their encoder and predictor architectures.
- Reports evaluation showing RaceMED consistently outperforms ten competitors, achieving up to 12.24% improvement in accuracy while reducing performance disparities across racial groups by up to 31.77%, thereby improving fairness.
