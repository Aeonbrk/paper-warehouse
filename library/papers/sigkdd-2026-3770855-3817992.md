# Simple yet Effective Diffusion-based Graph Data Augmentation via Complementary Diffusion Transfer

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have emerged as tools for modeling relational data, demonstrating impressive performance across many downstream tasks.
- Proposes CoDiT (Complementary Diffusion Transfer), a approach that constructs two complementary diffusion views and compensates for missing diffusion signals through cross-view transfer.
- Experiments on five real-world datasets report higher performance for the proposed CoDiT over thirteen competitive baselines.
