# ClimateIQA: A New Dataset and Benchmark to Advance Vision-Language Models in Meteorology Anomalies Analysis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Meteorological heatmaps play a vital role in deciphering extreme weather phenomena, yet their inherent complexities-marked by irregular contours, unstructured patterns, and complex color variations-present unique analytical hurdles for Vision-Language Models (VLMs).
- Introduces Sparse Position and Outline Tracking (SPOT), a algorithm specifically designed to process irregularly shaped colored regions in visual data.
- Building on SPOT, construct ClimateIQA, a meteorological visual question answering (VQA) dataset, comprising 26,280 high-resolution heatmaps and 762,120 instruction samples for wind gust, total precipitation, wind chill index and heat index analysis.
