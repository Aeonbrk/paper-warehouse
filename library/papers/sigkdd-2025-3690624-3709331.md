# Dynamic Localisation of Spatial-Temporal Graph Neural Network

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spatial-temporal data, fundamental to many intelligent applications, reveals dependencies indicating causal links between present measurements at specific locations and historical data at the same or other locations.
- Introduces DynAGS, a localised ASTGNN framework aimed at maximising efficiency and accuracy in distributed deployment.
- While this approach offers higher accuracy, it presents increased computational demands.
