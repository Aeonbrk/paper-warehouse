# CALM-TS: Risk-Controlled LLM Labeling for Time-Series via Calibrated Selective Gating

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Labeling time-series events such as anomalies and system failures is expensive and subjective, and distribution drift makes label definitions evolve over time.
- Presents CALM-TS, a risk-controlled weak-supervision pipeline that bounds the labeling error rate while maximizing coverage.
- CALM-TS attains 77--81% cost reduction on MIMIC-III and Yahoo~S5 at empirical risk ≤ a=0.05 in all 10 dataset-seed pairs.
