# Doctor Specific Tag Recommendation for Online Medical Record Management

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the rapid growth of online medical platforms, more and more doctors are willing to manage and communicate with patients via online services.
- Then, construct a doctor embedding learning module to enhance the recommendation quality by integrating implicit information within text representations and considering latent tag correlations to make more accurate predictions.
- Experiment results demonstrate the effectiveness of our framework from the viewpoints of all doctors (20% improvement) or low-activity doctors (10% improvement).
