# DuET: Dual-View Tensor-to-Topology Spectral Adapter for Enhancing Sparse Tensor Factorization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Many real-world datasets, ranging from web-interaction logs to biomedical networks, can be represented as sparse tensors where most entries are unobserved.
- Proposes DuET, a model-agnostic refinement framework that stabilizes TF embeddings by separating structural denoising from tensor factorization.
- Experiments on nine real-world tensors demonstrate consistent improvements for both retrieval and completion, with gains of up to 52.1% in NDCG@10 and reductions of up to 13.8% in RMSE.
