# Stabilising Job Survival Analysis for Disability Employment Services in Unseen Environments

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In Disability Employment Services (DES), an emerging problem is to make job survival analysis stable in unseen environments without prior knowledge of these environments.
- Proposes a stable survival analysis method for the DES sector without requiring prior knowledge of deployment environments.
- The case study of Australian workers with disability shows that our method can make stable risk predictions.
