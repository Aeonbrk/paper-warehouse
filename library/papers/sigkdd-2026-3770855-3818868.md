# Learning Latent Dynamical Causal Processes for Single-Cell Perturbation Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Single-cell perturbation prediction aims to predict how cells would respond to unseen perturbations and achieve out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization, with the goal of understanding how perturbations reshape underlying cellular programs over time.
- Develops a learning framework for recovering latent cellular programs and their dynamical evolution from single-cell sequencing data.
- Additional experiments on real-world CRISPR-based single-cell sequencing perturbation data show that CITE-VAE achieves competitive genome-wide prediction accuracy and improved perturbation-sensitive performance relative to strong representative baselines, highlighting the practical utility of the proposed method.
