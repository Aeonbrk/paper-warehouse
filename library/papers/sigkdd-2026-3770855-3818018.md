# Extreme Reachability of Continuous Time Random Walks on Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Extreme events play a central role in networked stochastic processes, governing the fastest information spreading, quickest search, and earliest arrival in many real-world systems.
- Develops a unified short-time framework for extreme reachability of continuous-time random walks on networks.
- Further proposes an efficient method to compute the short-time arrival intensity on large networks and validate our predictions on both synthetic and real-world graphs.
