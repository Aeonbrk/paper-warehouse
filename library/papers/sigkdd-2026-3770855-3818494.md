# TokenMixer-Large: Scaling Up Large Ranking Models in Industrial Recommenders

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- While scaling laws for recommendation models have gained traction, existing architectures such as Wukong, HiFormer, and DHEN often struggle with sub-optimal designs and hardware under-utilization, limiting their practical scalability.
- Proposes TokenMixer-Large, a systematically evolved architecture designed for extreme-scale recommendation.
- TokenMixer-Large successfully scales to 7 billion parameters for online traffic and 15 billion in offline experiments.
