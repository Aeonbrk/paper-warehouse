# Augmenting Log-based Anomaly Detection Models to Reduce False Anomalies with Human Feedback

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the increasing complexity of modern software systems, it is essential yet hard to detect anomalies and diagnose problems precisely.
- Proposes HiLog, an human-in-the-loop log-based anomaly detection approach that integrates human knowledge to augment anomaly detection models.
- Experiment results on three large-scale industrial software systems show that our method improves over 50% precision rate on average.
