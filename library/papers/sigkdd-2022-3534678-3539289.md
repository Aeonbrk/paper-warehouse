# Joint Knowledge Graph Completion and Question Answering

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Knowledge graph reasoning plays a role in many real-world applications, such as network alignment, computational fact-checking, recommendation, and many more.
- Proposes a neural model named BiNet to jointly handle KGC and multi-hop KGQA, and formulate it as a multi-task learning problem.
- Experiment results show that BiNet outperforms methods on a wide range of KGQA and KGC benchmark datasets.
