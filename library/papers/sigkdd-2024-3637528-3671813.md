# Truthful Bandit Mechanisms for Repeated Two-stage Ad Auctions

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online advertising platforms leverage a two-stage auction architecture to deliver personalized ads to users with low latency.
- In the second stage, an auction is conducted within the subset to determine the winning ad for display, using click-through-rate predictions from the second-stage machine learning model.
- Investigates the online learning process of the first-stage subset selection policy, while ensuring game-theoretic properties in repeated two-stage ad auctions.
