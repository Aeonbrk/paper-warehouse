# Training Dense Retrievers with Multiple Positive Passages

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modern knowledge-intensive systems, such as retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), rely on retrievers to establish the performance ceiling for downstream modules.
- Unify representative objectives, including Joint Likelihood (JointLH), Summed Marginal Likelihood (SumMargLH), and Log-Sum-Exp Pairwise (LSEPair) loss, under a shared contrastive learning framework.
- Empirically, conduct evaluations on Natural Questions, MS~MARCO, and the BEIR benchmark across two realistic regimes: homogeneous LLM-annotated data and heterogeneous mixtures of human and LLM labels.
