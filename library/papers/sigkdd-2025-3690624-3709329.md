# CAPER: Enhancing Career Trajectory Prediction using Temporal Knowledge Graph and Ternary Relationship

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The problem of career trajectory prediction (CTP) aims to predict one's future employer or job position.
- Proposes a solution, named as CAPER, that solves the challenges via sophisticated temporal knowledge graph (TKG) modeling.
- The experiments on a real-world career trajectory dataset demonstrate that CAPER consistently and outperforms four baselines, two recent TKG reasoning methods.
