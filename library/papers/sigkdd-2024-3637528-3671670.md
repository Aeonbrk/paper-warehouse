# Fast and Accurate Domain Adaptation for Irregular Tensor Decomposition

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Given an irregular tensor from a newly emerging domain, how can quickly and accurately capture its patterns utilizing existing irregular tensors in multiple domains?
- Proposes Meta-P2, a fast and accurate domain adaptation method for irregular tensor decomposition.
- Experiments on real-world datasets show that Meta-P2 achieves the best performance in various downstream tasks including missing value prediction and anomaly detection tasks.
