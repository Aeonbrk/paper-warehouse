# Causal-aware Graph Neural Architecture Search under Distribution Shifts

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph neural architecture search (NAS) has emerged as a approach for autonomously designing graph neural network architectures by leveraging correlations between graphs and architectures.
- Proposes a approach, Causal-aware Graph Neural Architecture Search (CARNAS), which is capable of capturing causal graph-architecture relationship during NAS process and discovering optimal graph architecture under distribution shifts.
- This reliance, without considering causal graph-architecture relationships, limits their ability to generalize under distribution shifts that are ubiquitous in real-world graph scenarios.
