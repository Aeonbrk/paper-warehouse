# Toward Structure Fairness in Dynamic Graph Embedding: A Trend-aware Dual Debiasing Approach

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent studies successfully learned static graph embeddings that are structurally fair by preventing the effectiveness disparity of high- and low-degree vertex groups in downstream graph mining tasks.
- First identify biased structural evolutions in a dynamic graph based on the evolving trend of vertex degree and then propose FairDGE, the first structurally Fair Dynamic Graph Embedding algorithm.
