# Commonsense Knowledge Graph towards Super APP and Its Applications in Alipay

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The recently explosive growth of Super Apps brings great convenience to people's daily life by providing a wide variety of services through mini-programs, including online shopping, travel, finance, and so on.
- Develops a knowledge graph representation learning framework for SupKG, enabling various downstream applications to benefit from learned representations of entities and relations.
- Due to the considerable gap between various scenarios, the restriction of information transfer and sharing severely blocks the efficient delivery of online services, potentially affecting the user's app experience.
