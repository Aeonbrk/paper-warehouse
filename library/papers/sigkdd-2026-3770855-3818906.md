# NPSolver: Neural Poisson Solver with Iterative Physics Supervision

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Efficiently solving Poisson equations on complex, irregular domains remains a fundamental challenge in scientific computing, as classical iterative solvers often suffer from prohibitive runtime due to ill-conditioned systems.
- To better capture boundary-driven features under mixed boundary conditions, further introduce the Boundary-Aware Transolver (BA-Transolver ) architecture that explicitly separates interior and boundary tokenization.
