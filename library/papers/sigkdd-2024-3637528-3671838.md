# Unveiling Global Interactive Patterns across Graphs: Towards Interpretable Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have emerged as a prominent framework for graph mining, leading to advances across various domains.
- To bridge this gap, this paper proposes a intrinsically interpretable scheme for graph classification, termed as Global Interactive Pattern (GIP) learning, which introduces learnable global interactive patterns to explicitly interpret decisions.
- Experiments conducted on both synthetic and real-world benchmarks demonstrate that the proposed GIP yields interpretability and competitive performance to the counterparts.
