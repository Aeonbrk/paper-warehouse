# GuardFGL: Similarity-driven Federated Graph Learning with Adversarial Robustness and Membership Privacy

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The emerging Federated Graph Learning (FGL) offers collaborative training on distributed graph data.
- Proposes GuardFGL, a similarity-driven FGL that extracts minimal-sufficient information from polluted data to maintain strong adversarial robustness and protect membership privacy.
- Using the real-world testbed and benchmark graph datasets, experiments demonstrate that GuardFGL can achieve robust prediction and better protect membership privacy than methods under adversarial attacks.
