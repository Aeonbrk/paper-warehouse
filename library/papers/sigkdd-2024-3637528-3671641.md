# DUE: Dynamic Uncertainty-Aware Explanation Supervision via 3D Imputation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Explanation supervision aims to enhance deep learning models by integrating additional signals to guide the generation of model explanations, showcasing notable improvements in both the predictability and explainability of the model.
- Proposes a Dynamic Uncertainty-aware Explanation supervision (DUE\footnoteCode available at: https://github.com/AlexQilong/DUE.) framework for 3D explanation supervision that ensures uncertainty-aware explanation guidance when dealing with sparsely annotated 3D data with diffusion-based 3D interpolation.
- Our proposed framework is validated through comprehensive experiments on diverse real-world medical imaging datasets.
