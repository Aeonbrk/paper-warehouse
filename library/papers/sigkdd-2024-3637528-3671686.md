# Pre-train and Refine: Towards Higher Efficiency in K-Agnostic Community Detection without Quality Degradation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Community detection (CD) is a classic graph inference task that partitions nodes of a graph into densely connected groups.
- Proposes PRoCD (Pre-training & Refinement fOr Community Detection), a simple yet method that reformulates K-agnostic CD as the binary node pair classification.
- In addition to benefiting from the transfer ability regarding quality, the online generalization and refinement can also help achieve high inference efficiency, since there is no time-consuming model optimization.
