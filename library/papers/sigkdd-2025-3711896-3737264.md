# Supervised Learning-enhanced Multi-Group Actor Critic for Live Stream Allocation in Feed

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In the context of a short video & live stream mixed recommendation scenario, the live stream recommendation system (RS) decides whether to allocate at most one live stream to the video feed for each.
- Proposes a Supervised Learning-enhanced Multi-Group Actor Critic algorithm (SL-MGAC).
- Empirically, evaluate the SL-MGAC algorithm using offline policy evaluation (OPE) and online A/B testing.
