# MapExplorer: New Content Generation from Low-Dimensional Visualizations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Low-dimensional visualizations, or ''projection maps,'' are widely used in scientific and creative domains to interpret large-scale and complex datasets.
- Although techniques such as t-SNE and UMAP can generate these maps, there exists no systematic method for leveraging them to generate new content.
