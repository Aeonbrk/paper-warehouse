# On Finding Bi-objective Pareto-optimal Fraud Prevention Rule Sets for Fintech Applications

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Rules are widely used in Fintech institutions to make fraud prevention decisions, since rules are highly interpretable thanks to their intuitive if-then structure.
- First introduce a algorithm called SpectralRules that directly generates a compact pool of rules in Stage 1 with high diversity.
- In practice, a two-stage framework of fraud prevention decision rule set mining is usually employed in large Fintech institutions.
