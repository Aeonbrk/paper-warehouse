# Beyond Binary Preference: Leveraging Bayesian Approaches for Joint Optimization of Ranking and Calibration

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Predicting click-through rate (CTR) is a critical task in recommendation systems, where the models are optimized with pointwise loss to infer the probability of items being clicked.
- Proposes an data augmentation strategy, named Beyond Binary Preference (BBP) training framework, to address this problem.
- Reports that BBP outperforms methods in both ranking and calibration capabilities, showcasing its effectiveness in addressing the limitations of existing methods.
