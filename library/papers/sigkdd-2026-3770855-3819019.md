# MedJudge: Medical Multimodal Reward Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- As large multimodal models (LMMs) become increasingly integrated into clinical workflows, robust evaluation and alignment mechanisms are critical to ensure their reliability in decision support.
- Introduces MedJudge, a multimodal medical reward modeling method that supports interpretable, evidence-grounded, and clinically-aligned decision evaluation.
- Construct a large-scale synthetic dataset with five complementary generation heuristics, and compare three training paradigms—scalar (BT), supervised (SFT), and reasoning-augmented SFT (SFT-R)—across 8 open-source models.
