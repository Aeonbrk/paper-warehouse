# CheatAgent: Attacking LLM-Empowered Recommender Systems via LLM Agent

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large Language Model (LLM)-empowered recommender systems (RecSys) have brought advances in personalized user experience and have attracted considerable attention.
- Proposes a attack framework called CheatAgent by harnessing the human-like capabilities of LLMs, where an LLM-based agent is developed to attack LLM-Empowered RecSys.
- Experiments across three real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed attacking method.
