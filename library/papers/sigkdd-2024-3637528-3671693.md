# Routing Evidence for Unseen Actions in Video Moment Retrieval

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Video moment retrieval (VMR) is a cutting-edge vision-language task locating a segment in a video according to the query.
- Proposes a plug-and-play structure, Routing Evidence (RE), with multiple evidence-learning heads and dynamically route one to locate a sentence with an unseen action.
- Though the methods have achieved performance, they assume that training and testing samples share the same action types, hindering real-world application.
