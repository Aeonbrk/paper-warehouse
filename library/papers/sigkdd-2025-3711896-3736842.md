# An Optimization Framework for Differentially Private Sparse Fine-Tuning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Differentially private stochastic gradient descent (DP-SGD) is broadly considered to be the gold standard for training and fine-tuning neural networks under differential privacy (DP).
- Proposes a new approach for sparse fine-tuning of neural networks under DP.
- Our numerical experiments on several computer vision models and datasets show that our parameter selection method leads to better prediction accuracy, compared to full-model private fine-tuning or existing private sparse fine-tuning approaches.
