# Knowledge Graph-Enhanced Long-CoT for Complex Biomolecular Reasoning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Biomolecular scientific questions often require multi-step mechanistic reasoning over structured knowledge such as protein–protein interactions, pathways, and disease associations.
- Proposes Bio-KCoT, a knowledge-augmented long-CoT framework that emphasizes principled use of structured knowledge.
- To rigorously evaluate these capabilities, construct BioMolKGQA, a benchmark with curated multi-hop evidence paths spanning diverse biomolecular QA pairs and reasoning depths.
