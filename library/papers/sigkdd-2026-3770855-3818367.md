# Denoising Implicit Feedback for Cold-start Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Implicit feedback is widely used in recommender systems due to its accessibility and generality, yet it usually presents noisy samples (e.g., clickbait, position bias).
- Proposes a model-agnostic denoising method called DIF.
- Furthermore, to improve pseudo-label accuracy, model the confidence of pseudo-labels based on the content similarity between the cold item and warm items, and then aggregate multiple pseudo-labels for each sample.
