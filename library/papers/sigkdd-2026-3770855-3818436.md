# Integrating Weather Foundation Model and Satellite to Enable Fine-Grained Solar Irradiance Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate day-ahead solar irradiance forecasting is essential for integrating solar energy into the power grid.
- Proposes Baguan-solar, a two-stage multimodal framework that fuses forecasts from Baguan, a global weather foundation model, with high-resolution geostationary satellite imagery to produce 24-hour irradiance forecasts at kilometer scale.
- Evaluated over East Asia using CLDAS as ground truth, Baguan-solar outperforms strong baselines (including ECMWF IFS, vanilla Baguan, and SolarSeer), reducing RMSE by 16.08% and better resolving cloud-induced transients.
