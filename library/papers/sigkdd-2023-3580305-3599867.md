# Modelling Delayed Redemption with Importance Sampling and Pre-Redemption Engagement

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Rewards-based programs are popular within e-commerce online stores, with the goal of providing serendipitous incentives to delight customers.
- Presents a framework, DRESS (Delayed Redemption Entire Space Sampling), that jointly models the effect of data sparsity and delayed feedback on redemptions.
- The success of such programs relies on their ability to identify relevant rewards for customers, from a wide variety of incentives available on the online store.
