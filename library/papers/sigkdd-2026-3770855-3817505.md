- Our contributions are twofold: (1) establish the first multi-dimensional, reviewer-aligned dataset with a reproducible evaluation framework for survey reviewing.
- Proposes SurveyReview, a reviewer-aligned, multi-dimensional benchmark and dataset for survey evaluation.
