# GraphWorld: Fake Graphs Bring Real Insights for GNNs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Despite advances in the field of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), only a small number (~5) of datasets are currently used to evaluate new models.
- In the course of our work on GNN infrastructure and open-source software at Google, have sought to develop benchmarks that are robust, tunable, scalable, and generalizable.
- Further show that GraphWorld efficiently explores regions of benchmark dataset space uncovered by standard benchmarks, revealing comparisons between models that have not been historically obtainable.
