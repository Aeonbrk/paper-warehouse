# LEAP: Learning-Dynamics-Guided Open-Loop Pricing for LLM Data Acquisition

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The sustainability of AI-powered Web platforms often relies on a feedback loop: stronger models improve user experience, which can help generate interaction data for further refinement.
- Introduces LEAP (Learning-dynamics-based Economic Adaptive Pricing), a framework that formulates data acquisition as an open-loop planning problem guided by a simple proxy of learning dynamics.
- Across controlled market-simulator experiments in three domains (Medical, Finance, and Comprehension), LEAP achieves lower evaluated acquisition-cost objectives than representative heuristic and online-learning baselines in many tested settings, with reductions exceeding 30% in representative long-horizon.
