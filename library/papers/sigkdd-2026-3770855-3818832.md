# Beyond Pairwise Interactions: Equivariant Hypergraph Diffusion for Crystal Structure Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Crystal Structure Prediction (CSP) remains a fundamental challenge with implications for materials discovery and the advancement of various scientific disciplines.
- Proposes the Equivariant Hypergraph Diffusion Model (EH-Diff), a generative framework designed to exploit the symmetry-preserving properties of hypergraphs.
- Empirically, conduct experiments on four benchmark datasets, and the results demonstrate that EH-Diff outperforms CSP methods even with a single diffusion sample.
