# Temporal Multimodal Multivariate Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Introduces temporal multimodal multivariate learning, a new family of decision making models that can indirectly learn and transfer online information from simultaneous observations of a probability distribution with more than one peak or more.
- Experiments on real-world datasets ( i.e., urban traffic data and hurricane ensemble forecasting data) demonstrate the performance of the proposed targeted decision-making over the baseline prediction methods across various settings.
