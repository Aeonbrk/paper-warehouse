# HIVE: Hierarchical Generation of Integrated and Varied Ensembles for Efficient Out-of-Distribution Generalization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization in industrial Model-as-a-Service (MaaS) systems is often hindered by a trilemma of heterogeneous distribution shifts, strict inference latency budgets, and rigorous privacy constraints.
- Proposes HIVE, a model-agnostic framework that reframes OOD generalization as the construction of a functionally diverse basis set.
- Validated on a rigorously siloed dataset of 8 million OOD samples, HIVE demonstrates performance lift while strictly satisfying the efficiency mandates of high-throughput industrial environments.
