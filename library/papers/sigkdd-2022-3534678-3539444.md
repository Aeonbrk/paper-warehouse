# Improving Data-driven Heterogeneous Treatment Effect Estimation Under Structure Uncertainty

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Estimating how a treatment affects units individually, known as heterogeneous treatment effect (HTE) estimation, is an essential part of decision-making and policy implementation.
- Develops a feature selection method that considers each feature's value for HTE estimation and learns the relevant parts of the causal structure from data.
- At the same time, accounting for the causal structure of real-world data is rarely trivial since the causal mechanisms that gave rise to the data are typically unknown.
