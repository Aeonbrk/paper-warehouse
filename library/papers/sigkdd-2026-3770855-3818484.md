# SOAR: Real-Time Joint Optimization of Order Allocation and Robot Scheduling in Robotic Mobile Fulfillment Systems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Robotic Mobile Fulfillment Systems (RMFS) rely on mobile robots for automated inventory transportation, coordinating order allocation and robot scheduling to enhance warehousing efficiency.
- Proposes SOAR, a unified Deep Reinforcement Learning framework for real-time joint optimization.
- Experiments on synthetic and real-world industrial datasets, in collaboration with Geekplus, demonstrate that SOAR reduces global makespan by 7.5% and average order completion time by 15.4% on real-world industrial datasets with sub-100ms per-decision-step latency.
