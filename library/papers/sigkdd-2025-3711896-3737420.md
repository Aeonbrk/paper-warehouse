# Exploring the Potential of Foundation Models as Reliable AI Contact Centers

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The emergence of foundation models with high generalization performance increases expectations for their application in AI Contact Centers (AICC) services.
- Proposes a customized prompt engineering approach to optimize foundation models for AICC services.
- To bridge this gap, construct a Korean phone call-based customer service dataset designed to evaluate the feasibility and fairness of foundation model-based AICC systems.
