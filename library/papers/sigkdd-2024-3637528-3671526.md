# Generative Auto-bidding via Conditional Diffusion Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Auto-bidding plays a crucial role in facilitating online advertising by automatically providing bids for advertisers.
- Proposes DiffBid, a conditional diffusion modeling approach for bid generation.
- Experiments conducted on the real-world dataset and online A/B test on Alibaba advertising platform demonstrate the effectiveness of DiffBid, achieving 2.81% increase in GMV and 3.36% increase in ROI.
