# Spuriousness-Aware Meta-Learning for Learning Robust Classifiers

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spurious correlations are brittle associations between certain attributes of inputs and target variables, such as the correlation between an image background and an object class.
- Proposes a learning framework based on meta-learning, termed SPUME -- SPUriousness-aware MEta-learning, to train an image classifier to be robust to spurious correlations.
- Reports that our method is robust to spurious correlations without knowing them a priori and achieves the best on five benchmark datasets with different robustness measures.
