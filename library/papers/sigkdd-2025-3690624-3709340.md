# Future Matters for Present: Towards Effective Physical Simulation over Meshes

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Investigates the problem of learning mesh-based physical simulations, which is a crucial task with applications in fluid mechanics and aerodynamics.
- Introduces a new future-to-present learning perspective, and further develop a simple yet approach named Foresight And Interpolation (FAIR) for long-term mesh-based simulations.
- Through pyramid-like alternative propagation between the foresight step and refinement step, our proposed framework FAIR can generate accurate long-term trajectories, achieving a error reduction compared with the best baseline on four benchmark datasets.
