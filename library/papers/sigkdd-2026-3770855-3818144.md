# EPMD: A Framework for LLM-Enhanced Ad Auctions

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) to dynamically enhance ad creatives offers market value but presents a formidable mechanism design challenge.
- Then introduce the Equilibrium-Predictive Mechanism Design (EPMD) framework, which employs a dual-network architecture consisting of an Investment Policy Network (IPN) and an Equilibrium Prediction Network (EPN).
- Empirical evaluations using real-world datasets and GPT-4o-based enhancement demonstrate that EPMD increases platform revenue by up to +1.11% while ensuring market stability and incentive alignment, providing the first tractable solution for this new class.
