# Cost-Efficient Fraud Risk Optimization with Submodularity in Insurance Claim

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The fraudulent insurance claim is critical for the insurance industry.
- CEROS consists of two parts that propose: a submodular set-wise classification model called SSCM to estimate the submodular objective function, and a primal-dual algorithm with segmentation point called PDA-SP to solve the objective function.
- Finally, the trade-off coefficients output by PDA-SP that balance accuracy and cost in fraud risk verification are applied to online insurance claim decision-making.
