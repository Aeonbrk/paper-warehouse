# KGA-LM: Representation-Level Grounding for Conversational Search over Knowledge Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Integrating structured knowledge graphs (KGs) with Large Language Models (LLMs) is essential for trustworthy, knowledge intensive conversational systems.
- Proposes KGA-LM, a framework that integrates external knowledge via representation-level grounding.
- Experiments on multiple conversational benchmarks demonstrate that KGA-LM improves factual accuracy and reduces hallucination compared to prompt-linearized baselines.
