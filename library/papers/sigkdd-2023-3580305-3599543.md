# Warpformer: A Multi-scale Modeling Approach for Irregular Clinical Time Series

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Irregularly sampled multivariate time series are ubiquitous in various fields, particularly in healthcare, and exhibit two key characteristics: intra-series irregularity and inter-series discrepancy.
- Presents Warpformer, a approach that fully considers these two characteristics.
- Conduct experiments on widely used datasets and a new large-scale benchmark built from clinical databases.
