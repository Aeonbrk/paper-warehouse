# Off-Policy Evaluation and Learning for the Future under Non-Stationarity

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies the problem of future off-policy evaluation (F-OPE) and learning (F-OPL) for estimating and optimizing the future value of policies in non-stationary environments, where distributions vary over time.
- In addition, extend our estimator to develop a new policy-gradient method to proactively learn a good future policy using only historical data.
- Empirical results show that our methods substantially outperform existing methods in estimating and optimizing the future policy value under non-stationarity for various experimental setups.
