# CR-Aug: Community Risk-Guided Adaptive Augmentation for Semi-supervised Graph Anomaly Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Semi-supervised graph anomaly detection (GAD) has garnered increasing attention under a challenging setting where only a limited number of normal nodes are labeled during training.
- Proposes CR-Aug, a Community Risk-Guided Adaptive Augmentation framework, which is designed to overcome this limitation by leveraging community-specific prior knowledge more effectively.
- Consequently, the generated pseudo-anomalies exhibit limited diversity and specificity, failing to represent the complex distributions of real-world anomalies.
