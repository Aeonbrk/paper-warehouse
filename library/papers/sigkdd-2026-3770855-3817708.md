# Moment Matters: Mean and Variance Causal Graph Discovery from Heteroscedastic Observational Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Heteroscedasticity---where the variance of a variable changes with other variables---is pervasive in real data, and elucidating why it arises from the perspective of statistical moments is crucial in scientific knowledge discovery and decision-making.
- Proposes a Bayesian, moment-driven causal discovery framework that infers separate mean and variance causal graphs from observational heteroscedastic data.
