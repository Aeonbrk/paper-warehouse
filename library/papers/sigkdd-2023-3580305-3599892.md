# RankFormer: Listwise Learning-to-Rank Using Listwide Labels

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Web applications where users are presented with a limited selection of items have long employed ranking models to put the most relevant results first.
- Proposes the RankFormer as an architecture that, with a Transformer at its core, can jointly optimize a listwide assessment objective and a traditional listwise LTR objective.
- An online experiment shows that knowledge distillation can be used to find immediate practical use for the RankFormer.
