# Mitigating Spurious Correlations in Mental Health Analysis: A Contrastive Learning Approach for Cross-Domain Robustness

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes a robust framework designed to disentangle genuine clinical signals from superficial artifacts.
- Our approach integrates a contrastive learning method by using masked language modeling (MLM) with artifact-masked data and an adaptive hard-negative sampling strategy to ensure cross-domain generalization.
- Evaluations on diverse benchmarks, including real-world social media posts and LLM-generated synthetic conversational utterance datasets, demonstrate that our method outperforms baselines in out-of-distribution (OOD) settings, particularly as contextual information increases.
