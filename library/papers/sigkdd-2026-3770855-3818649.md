# Episode Spaces: Mining Reusable Knowledge from Urban Simulation Corpora for Safety-Critical Decision Support

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- End-to-end reinforcement learning has advanced urban traffic management yet it exhibits dangerous fragility in safety-critical scenarios: a PPO-trained evacuation coordinator achieves 92.5% evacuation rate in-distribution but collapses to 32.7% under a compound distribution shift.
- Introduces episode spaces: indexed, queryable libraries of complete context-action-outcome decision episodes extracted from simulation runs.
- On a richer five-zone benchmark, retrieval-only coordination achieves 69.8% where PPO degrades to 49.3%.
