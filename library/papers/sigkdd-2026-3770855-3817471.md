# Passing the Turing Test on Screen: A Benchmark for Mobile GUI Agent Humanization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The rise of autonomous GUI agents has triggered adversarial countermeasures from digital platforms, yet existing research prioritizes utility and robustness over the critical dimension of anti-detection.
- Introduces the ''Turing Test on Screen,'' formally modeling the interaction as a MinMax optimization problem between a detector and an agent aiming to minimize behavioral divergence.
- Then collect a new high-fidelity dataset of mobile touch dynamics, and conduct our analysis that vanilla LMM-based agents are easily detectable due to unnatural kinematics.
