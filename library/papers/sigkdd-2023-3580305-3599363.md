# Generative Causal Interpretation Model for Spatio-Temporal Representation Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Learning, interpreting, and predicting from complex and high-dimensional spatio-temporal data is a natural ability of humans and other intelligent agents, and one of the most important and difficult challenges of AI.
- Proposes a theoretically-grounded Generative Causal Interpretation Model (GCIM), which infers explanatory-capable microscopic causal descriptors from observational data via spatio-temporal causal representations.
- Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets show that GCIM can successfully identify latent causal descriptors and structures, and accurately predict future data.
