# On (Normalised) Discounted Cumulative Gain as an Off-Policy Evaluation Metric for Top-$n$ Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Approaches to recommendation are typically evaluated in one of two ways: (1) via a (simulated) online experiment, often seen as the gold standard, or (2) via some offline evaluation procedure.
- (Normalised) Discounted Cumulative Gain (nDCG) is one such metric that has seen widespread adoption in empirical studies.
- Our work takes a critical look at this approach, and investigates when can expect such metrics to approximate the gold standard outcome of an online experiment.
