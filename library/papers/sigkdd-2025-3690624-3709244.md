# Conservation-informed Graph Learning for Spatiotemporal Dynamics Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Data-centric methods have shown great potential in understanding and predicting spatiotemporal dynamics, enabling better design and control of the object system.
- Herein introduce the conservation-informed GNN (CiGNN), an end-to-end explainable learning framework, to learn spatiotemporal dynamics based on limited training data.
- The efficacy of our model has been verified in various spatiotemporal systems based on synthetic and real-world datasets, showing superiority over baseline models.
