# DiffCrime: A Multimodal Conditional Diffusion Model for Crime Risk Map Inference

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Crime risk map plays a crucial role in urban planning and public security management.
- Proposes a multimodal conditional diffusion method, namely, DiffCrime, to infer the crime risk map based on datasets in various domains, i.e., historical crime incidents, satellite imagery, and map imagery.
- Experiments on two real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of DiffCrime, which outperforms baselines by at least 43% and 31% in terms of RMSE, respectively.
