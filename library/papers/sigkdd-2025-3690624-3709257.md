# Noise-Resilient Point-wise Anomaly Detection in Time Series Using Weak Segment Labels

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Detecting anomalies in temporal data has gained attention across various real-world applications, aiming to identify unusual events and mitigate potential hazards.
- Formulates the above imperfect information as noisy labels and propose NRdetector, a noise-resilient framework that incorporates confidence-based sample selection, robust segment-level learning, and data-centric point-level detection for multivariate time series anomaly detection.
- Experiments on real-world multivariate time series datasets with 11 different evaluation metrics demonstrate that NRdetector consistently achieves robust results across multiple real-world datasets, outperforming various baselines adapted to operate in our setting.
