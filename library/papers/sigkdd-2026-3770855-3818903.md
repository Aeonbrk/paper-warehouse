# SimuGov: A Simulation Optimization Framework for Generative AI Governance Strategy Design

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- This study addresses a concrete challenge in Generative AI governance through the lens of AI watermarking: how to evaluate and optimize governance strategies before deployment in a bounded socio-technical setting.
- Proposes SimuGov, a simulation framework for governance strategy optimization.
- Experimental results on the AI watermark governance testbed show that SimuGov reproduces nonlinear social dynamics such as the ''chilling effect,'' discovers robust governance strategies that outperform examined heuristic and random-search baselines.
