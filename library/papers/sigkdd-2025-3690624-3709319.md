# Revisiting Cognition in Neural Cognitive Diagnosis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Cognitive diagnosis is a fundamental task in intelligent education, aiming to measure students' proficiency on knowledge concepts based on practice data.
- Revisit essence of cognition in Educational Psychology Theory and proposes a Cognition-aware Cognitive Diagnosis (CCD) model, where first introduce the Cognition factor as a bridge into the long-standing three-basic-factors (Student, Exercise, Knowledge concept) paradigm.
- In the second part, employ a basic interaction function and a slip and guess influence function, inputting our new θ, a continuous Q-matrix (generated by a siamese PLMs).
