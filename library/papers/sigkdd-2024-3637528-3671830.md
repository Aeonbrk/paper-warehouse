# Graph Cross Supervised Learning via Generalized Knowledge

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The success of GNNs highly relies on the accurate labeling of data.
- Proposes Lipshitz-regularized Mixture-of-Experts similarity network (LIME), a framework to encode new nodes and handle label noises.
- Empirical experiments validate LIME's effectiveness: observe a substantial enhancement of up to 11.34% in node classification accuracy compared to the backbone model when subjected to the challenges of label noise on classes across five.
