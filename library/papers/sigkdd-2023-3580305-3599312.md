# Domain-Guided Spatio-Temporal Self-Attention for Egocentric 3D Pose Estimation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Vision-based ego-centric 3D human pose estimation (ego-HPE) is essential to support critical applications of xR-technologies.
- Proposes a domain-guided spatio-temporal transformer model that leverages information specific to ego-views.
- Finally, demonstrate the generalization capabilities of our model to real-world HPE tasks beyond ego-views achieving 7.7% improvement on 2D human pose estimation with the Human3.6M dataset.
