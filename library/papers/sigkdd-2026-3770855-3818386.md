# DynaTree: Dynamic Agentic Retrieval Tree for Time-Sensitive News Retrieval

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) improves retrieval by integrating planning, tool use.
- Proposes DynaTree (Dynamic Agentic Retrieval Tree), a two-stage framework in which agentic RAG is used for offline semantic exploration, while online retrieval is performed without agentic inference.
- Experiments on our multi-day Syft news benchmark and multiple BEIR datasets show that DynaTree achieves recall and NDCG, consistently outperforming standard RAG and prior agentic baselines.
