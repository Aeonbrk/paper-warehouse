# T-Cell Receptor-Peptide Interaction Prediction with Physical Model Augmented Pseudo-Labeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Predicting the interactions between T-cell receptors (TCRs) and peptides is crucial for the development of personalized medicine and targeted vaccine in immunotherapy.
- Our proposed method that trains the deep neural network with physical modeling and data-augmented pseudo-labeling improves over baselines in the available two datasets.
- Proposes to extend the training dataset by physical modeling of TCR-peptide pairs.
