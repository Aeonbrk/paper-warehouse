# ANCHOR: Aligned Non-contextual and Contextual Hybridization with On-condition Calibration for Per-Base Risk Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Reliable prediction under distribution shift remains a core challenge in machine learning.
- Proposes ANCHOR, a lightweight framework for reliable prediction under distribution shift.
- Across both a public digital-twin benchmark and Inkjet4721, a wet-lab dataset from in-house designed and synthesized oligo pools spanning multiple protocols and replicates, ANCHOR consistently achieves strong ranking performance and reliable calibration under leave-one-condition-out.
