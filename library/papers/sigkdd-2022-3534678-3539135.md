# XDAI: A Tuning-free Framework for Exploiting Pre-trained Language Models in Knowledge Grounded Dialogue Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large-scale pre-trained language models (PLMs) have shown advances on various downstream tasks, among which dialogue is one of the most concerned.
- Proposes XDAI, a knowledge-grounded dialogue system that is equipped with the prompt-aware tuning-free PLM exploitation and supported by the ready-to-use open-domain external knowledge resources plus the easy-to-change domain-specific mechanism.
- Experiments including human evaluation, Turing test, and online evaluation have demonstrated the competitive performance of XDAI compared with the general PLMs and specific PLMs for dialogue.
