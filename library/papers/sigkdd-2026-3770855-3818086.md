# Online Learning to Rank under Corruption: A Robust Cascading Bandits Approach

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online learning to rank (ØLTR ) studies how to recommend a short ranked list of items from a large pool and improves future rankings based on user clicks.
- Proposes M2UCB-V, a robust algorithm that incorporates a mean-of-medians estimator, which to our knowledge is applied to bandits with corruption setting for the first time.
- Comprehensive and experiments on real-world datasets further demonstrate that our approach consistently outperforms prior methods while maintaining strong robustness.
