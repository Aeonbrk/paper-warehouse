# Addressing Intent Dissonance in Cross-Domain Distillation on YouTube

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modern recommender systems increasingly operate within mixed-interface ecosystems, transitioning users between high-intent browsing (e.g., Homepage) and low-intent browsing (e.g., infinite feeds).
- Proposes a domain-adaptive approach implemented on a multi-billion parameter model.
- Extending our prior work on zero-shot cross distillation, this paper presents a case study on adapting a large-scale shared teacher optimized for high-intent (long-form) video ranking to low-intent (short-form) video feed.
