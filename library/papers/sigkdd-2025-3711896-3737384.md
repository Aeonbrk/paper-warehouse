# FoodPuzzle: Toward Developing Large Language Models as Autonomous Flavor Scientists

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Flavor development in the food industry is increasingly challenged by the need for rapid innovation and precise flavor profile creation.
- Proposes a Scientific Agent approach, integrating in-context learning and retrieval augmented techniques to generate grounded hypotheses in the domain of food science.
- Introduces the FoodPuzzle dataset, a challenging benchmark consisting of 978 food items and 1,766 flavor molecule profiles.
