# Learning to Doubt: Forgetting Aware Learning for Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modern neural networks are often miscalibrated, assigning high confidence to predictions they have not learned stably, which leads to overconfident errors under noise, imbalance, and distribution shift.
- Proposes Forgetting-Aware Learning (FAL) using a simple ranking regularizer that leverages forgetting events during training as a temporal signal of epistemic uncertainty.
- Empirically, FAL improves calibration and confidence ranking while preserving accuracy.
