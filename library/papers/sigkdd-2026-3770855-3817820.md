# HyperPatch: Sequential Knowledge Editing Under N-ary Structural Drift

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large Language Models (LLMs) rely on Knowledge Editing (KE) to maintain temporal validity, yet real-world knowledge is inherently n-ary.
- HyperPatch preserves event integrity through three phases: (i) Structural Prior Initialization, establishing a topology-aware embedding space via contrastive learning on a Hypergraph Neural Network (HGNN) to capture high-order correlations.
- On the MQuAKE-CF and MQuAKE-T benchmarks, HyperPatch achieves relative gains in Hop-wise Accuracy (H-Acc) of 96.24% and 21.06% over the strongest baseline, respectively.
