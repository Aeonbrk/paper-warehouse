# A Neuro-Symbolic Approach to Symbol Grounding for ALC-Ontologies

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Neuro-symbolic computing aims to integrate neural learning with symbolic reasoning to address the fundamental challenge of symbol grounding.
- Introduces EmALC , a neuro-symbolic framework that bridges neural perception with symbolic logic through differentiable fuzzy semantics.
- Experimental evaluation demonstrates EmALC's effectiveness: on ontology revision tasks, it achieves 100% success rate in correcting masked groundings while preserving semantic integrity.
