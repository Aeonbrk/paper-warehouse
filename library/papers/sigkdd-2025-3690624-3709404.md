# YaART: Yet Another ART Rendering Technology

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In the rapidly progressing field of generative models, the development of efficient and high-fidelity text-to-image diffusion systems represents a frontier.
- This study introduces YaART, a production-grade text-to-image cascaded diffusion model aligned to human preferences using Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF).
- During the development of YaART, especially focus on the choices of the model and training dataset sizes, the aspects that were not systematically investigated for text-to-image cascaded diffusion models before.
