# Latent Diffusion-based Data Augmentation for Continuous-Time Dynamic Graph Model

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Continuous-Time Dynamic Graph (CTDG) precisely models evolving real-world relationships, drawing heightened interest in dynamic graph learning across academia and industry.
- Proposes Conda, a latent diffusion-based GDA method tailored for CTDGs.
- Experimentation across six widely used real-world datasets reports performance improvements of our approach, particularly in scenarios with limited historical data.
