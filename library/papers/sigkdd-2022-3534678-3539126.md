# User Behavior Pre-training for Online Fraud Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The outbreak of COVID-19 burgeons newborn services on online platforms and simultaneously buoys multifarious online fraud activities.
- Proposes to pre-train user behavior sequences, which consist of orderly arranged actions, from the large-scale unlabeled data sources for online fraud detection.
- Experiments on three downstream transaction and user-level online fraud detection tasks demonstrate that our UB-PTM is able to outperform the designing for specific tasks.
