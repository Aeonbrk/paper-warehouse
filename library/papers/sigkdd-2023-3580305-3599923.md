# UA-FedRec: Untargeted Attack on Federated News Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- News recommendation is essential for personalized news distribution.
- Designs a news similarity perturbation method to make representations of similar news farther and those of dissimilar news closer to interrupt news modeling.
- Experiments on two real-world datasets show that UA-FedRec can effectively degrade the accuracy of existing federated news recommendation methods, even when defense is applied.
