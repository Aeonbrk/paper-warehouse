# Robust Spatiotemporal Traffic Forecasting with Reinforced Dynamic Adversarial Training

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Machine learning-based forecasting models are commonly used in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) to predict traffic patterns and provide city-wide services.
- Proposes a reinforcement learning-based method to learn the optimal node selection strategy for adversarial examples, which simultaneously strengthens the dynamic attack defense capability and reduces the model overfitting.
- Evaluates our approach on two real-world traffic datasets and demonstrate its superiority over other baselines.
