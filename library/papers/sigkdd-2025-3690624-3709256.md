# Can Large Language Models Improve the Adversarial Robustness of Graph Neural Networks?

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph neural networks (GNNs) are vulnerable to adversarial attacks, especially for topology perturbations, and many methods that improve the robustness of GNNs have received considerable attention.
- Proposes an LLM-based robust graph structure inference framework, LLM4RGNN.
- By presenting the empirical results, find that despite that LLMs can improve the robustness of GNNs, there is still an average decrease of 23.1% in accuracy, implying that the GNNs remain extremely vulnerable against.
