# Low Rank Multi-Dictionary Selection at Scale

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The sparse dictionary coding framework represents signals as a linear combination of a few predefined dictionary atoms.
- Proposes a multi-dictionary atom selection technique for low-rank sparse coding named LRMDS.
- Reports the scalability and quality of LRMDS in both synthetic and real-world datasets and for a range of coding dictionaries.
