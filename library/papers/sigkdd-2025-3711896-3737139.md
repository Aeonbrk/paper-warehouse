# Surface-based Molecular Design with Multi-modal Flow Matching

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Therapeutic peptides show promise in targeting previously undruggable binding sites, with recent advancements in deep generative models enabling full-atom peptide co-design for specific protein receptors.
- Proposes an omni-design peptides generation paradigm, called SurfFlow, a surface-based generative algorithm that enables comprehensive co-design of sequence, structure, and surface for peptides.
- SurfFlow employs a multi-modality conditional flow matching (CFM) architecture to learn distributions of surface geometries and biochemical properties, enhancing peptide binding accuracy.
