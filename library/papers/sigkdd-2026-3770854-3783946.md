# Effective Online 3D Bin Packing with Lookahead Parcels Using Monte Carlo Tree Search

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online 3D Bin Packing (3D-BP) with robotic arms is crucial for reducing transportation and labor costs in modern logistics.
- Formulates online 3D-BP with lookahead parcels as a Model Predictive Control (MPC) problem and adapt the Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) framework to solve it.
- Experiments on real-world datasets show that our method consistently outperforms baselines, achieving over 10% gains under distributional shifts, 4% average improvement in online deployment.
