# NMRTrans: Structure Elucidation from Experimental NMR Spectra via Set Transformers

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) spectroscopy is fundamental for molecular structure elucidation, yet interpreting spectra at scale remains time-consuming and highly expertise-dependent.
- To address these issues, build NMRSpec, a large-scale corpus of experimental 1H and 13C NMR spectra mined from chemical literature.
- To the best of our knowledge, NMRTrans is the first NMR Transformer trained solely on large-scale experimental spectra and achieves performance on experimental benchmarks, improving Top-10 Accuracy over the strongest baseline by +17.82 points.
