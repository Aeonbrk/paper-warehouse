# Dependence and Model Selection in LLP: The Problem of Variants

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The problem of Learning from Label Proportions (LLP) has received considerable research attention and has numerous practical applications.
- While a number of algorithmic approaches to learning in this context have been proposed, very little work has addressed the model selection problem for LLP.
- Reports how this leads to improved methods of model selection that demonstrate outperform the existing over a wide range of datasets and LLP algorithms.
