# Multi-Behavior Collaborative Filtering with Partial Order Graph Convolutional Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Representing information of multiple behaviors in the single graph collaborative filtering (CF) vector has been a long-standing challenge.
- This approach often results in the joint embedding performing well on the main tasks but poorly on the auxiliary ones.
- Offline experiments conducted on three public benchmark datasets demonstrate that POGCN outperforms multi-behavior baselines across all types of behaviors.
