# Parallel Complex Diffusion for Scalable Time Series Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Diffusion models learn data distributions indirectly through denoising, making the difficulty of generative modeling closely tied to the dependency structure of data.
- Proposes PaCoDi (Parallel Complex Diffusion), a frequency-native diffusion framework that constructs the diffusion path in the spectral domain while replacing the complex-valued estimator with parallel real-valued estimators for real and imaginary components.
