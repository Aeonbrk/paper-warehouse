# Sampling Random Graphs from the Colored Configuration Model

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- A fundamental step in knowledge discovery is statistically assessing data mining results.
- Develops Sirius-B, a simple baseline adapting the Metropolis-Hastings approach, and Sirius, a refined algorithm tailored to preserve the CDM, thus achieving provably faster mixing.
- Our main motivation is the study of online social networks, where the color of a user represents their side in a debate.
