# Propagation Structure-Aware Graph Transformer for Robust and Interpretable Fake News Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The rise of social media has intensified fake news risks, prompting a growing focus on leveraging graph learning methods such as graph neural networks (GNNs) to understand post-spread patterns of news.
- Presents a relational propagation graph as a position encoding for the graph Transformer, enabling the model to capture both propagation depth and distance relationships of users.
- Furthermore, they inadequately capture intricate patterns inherent in long-sequence dependencies of news propagation due to their use of shallow GNNs aimed at avoiding the over-smoothing issue, consequently diminishing their overall accuracy.
