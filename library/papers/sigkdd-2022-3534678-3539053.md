# EXTR: Click-Through Rate Prediction with Externalities in E-Commerce Sponsored Search

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Click-Through Rate (CTR) prediction, estimating the probability of a user clicking on items, plays a key fundamental role in sponsored search.
- Proposes EXternality TRansformer (EXTR) which regards target ad with all slots as query and external items as key&value to model externalities in all exposure situations in parallel.
- Experimental results on Alibaba datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of externalities in the task of CTR prediction and illustrate that our proposed approach can bring profits to the real-world e-commerce platform.
