# MARLP: Time-series Forecasting Control for Agricultural Managed Aquifer Recharge

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The rapid decline in groundwater around the world poses a challenge to sustainable agriculture.
- Designs a two-stage forecasting framework.
- Real-world experiments show that MARLP reduces the oxygen deficit ratio by 86.8% while improving the recharging amount in unit time by 35.8%, compared with the previous four years.
