# Efficient Privacy Auditing for Generative Model via Local Information

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Diffusion models have become the dominant approach for text-to-image generation, but their ability to memorize training data raises increasing concerns about privacy leakage.
- Proposes an empirical privacy assessment framework that leverages local image information, instead of treating images as indivisible wholes, to improve the distinguishability of privacy leakage signals.
