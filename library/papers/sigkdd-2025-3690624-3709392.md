# Improving Synthetic Image Detection Towards Generalization: An Image Transformation Perspective

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With recent generative models facilitating photo-realistic image synthesis, the proliferation of synthetic images has also engendered certain negative impacts on social platforms, thereby raising an urgent imperative to develop detectors.
- Proposes SAFE, a lightweight and detector with three simple image transformations.
- Our pipeline achieves a new performance, with improvements of 4.5% in accuracy and 2.9% in average precision against existing methods.
