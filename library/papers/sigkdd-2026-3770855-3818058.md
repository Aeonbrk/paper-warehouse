# AgentsKG: A Hierarchical Multi-Agent Framework for Open-Domain Knowledge Graph Construction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Knowledge Graph Construction (KGC) is essential for transforming unstructured text into structured knowledge representations.
- Proposes AgentsKG, a hierarchical multi-agent framework that decouples semantic perception from structural integration.
- Reports evaluation showing AgentsKG outperforms training-free baselines in both extraction accuracy and structural quality, offering a robust approach to open-domain knowledge graph construction without additional training.
