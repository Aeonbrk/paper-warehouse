# How does the Memorization of Neural Networks Impact Adversarial Robust Models?

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent studies suggest that "memorization" is one necessary factor for overparameterized deep neural networks (DNNs) to achieve optimal performance.
- Proposes Benign Adversarial Training (BAT) which can facilitate adversarial training to avoid fitting "harmful" atypical samples and fit as more "benign" atypical samples as possible.
- Adversarially trained models always suffer from poor generalization, with both relatively low clean accuracy and robustness on the test set.
