# Data-Efficient Brain Connectome Analysis via Multi-Task Meta-Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Brain networks characterize complex connectivities among brain regions as graph structures, which provide a means to study brain connectomes.
- Most brain network datasets are limited in sample sizes due to the relatively high cost of data collection, which hinders the deep learning models from sufficient training.
- Inspired by meta-learning that learns new concepts fast with limited training examples, this paper studies data-efficient training strategies for analyzing brain connectomes in a cross-dataset setting.
