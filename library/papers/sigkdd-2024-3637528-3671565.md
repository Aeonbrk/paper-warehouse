# Understanding the Ranking Loss for Recommendation with Sparse User Feedback

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Click-through rate (CTR) prediction is a crucial area of research in online advertising.
- Introduces a perspective on the effectiveness of the auxiliary ranking loss in CTR prediction: it generates larger gradients on negative samples.
- Additionally, successfully integrate the ranking loss into Tencent's online advertising system, achieving notable lifts of 0.70% and 1.26% in Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) for two main scenarios.
