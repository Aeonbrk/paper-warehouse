# Unlocking the Power of Diffusion Models in Sequential Recommendation: A Simple and Effective Approach

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Focuses on the often-overlooked issue of embedding collapse in existing diffusion-based sequential recommendation models and propose ADRec, an framework designed to mitigate this problem.
- Proposes a three-stage training strategy: (1) pre-training the embedding weights(2) aligning these weights with the ADRec backbone, and (3) fine-tuning the model.
- Our comprehensive empirical evaluation across six datasets underscores the effectiveness of ADRec in enhancing both the accuracy and efficiency of diffusion-based sequential recommendation systems.
