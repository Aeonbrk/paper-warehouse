# Testing Statistical Dependence in Labeled Graphs under Mismatches

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Many real-world systems—ranging from protein structures to financial networks—are naturally represented as labeled graphs, where both topology and node attributes carry critical information.
- Proposes a and practical framework for dependence testing in labeled graphs via mutual information over a structure-weighted joint label distribution.
- Provide theoretical guarantees with explicit error bounds and validate our method on both synthetic and real-world datasets, including protein structures from the lipocalin family, and recurring motifs in the Cora citation network.
