# MAP: A Model-agnostic Pretraining Framework for Click-through Rate Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the widespread application of online advertising systems, click-through rate (CTR) prediction has received more and more attention and research.
- Proposes a Model-agnostic Pretraining (MAP) framework that applies feature corruption and recovery on multi-field categorical data, and more specifically, derive two practical algorithms: masked feature prediction (MFP) and replaced feature detection (RFD).
- Our experiments on two real-world million-level datasets (i.e., Avazu, Criteo) demonstrate the advantages of these two methods over several strong baselines, and achieve new in terms of both performance and efficiency for CTR prediction.
