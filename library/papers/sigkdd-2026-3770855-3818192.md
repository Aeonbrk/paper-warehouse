# Neuro-SPO: Physically-Grounded and Affect-Aligned EEG-to-Keyword Decoding

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Decoding semantic information from non-invasive EEG remains a formidable challenge in Brain-Computer Interfaces due to the low signal-to-noise ratio and the complex biophysics of neural dynamics.
- Proposes the Neural Spectral-Physical Operator (Neuro-SPO), a robust framework that reframes EEG-to-Keyword decoding as a continuous representation learning problem.
- Experiments on the ZuCo and ChineseEEG benchmarks demonstrate that Neuro-SPO outperforms methods in ranking metrics and retrieval accuracy.
