# Simulated Infectious Diseases Datasets with Controlled Data Bias

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Massive datasets related to infectious diseases became available after the COVID-19 pandemic, supporting data-driven approaches in modeling and forecasting infectious diseases.
- These approaches are known to exacerbate data biases present in the training data such as having certain demographic groups being over or underrepresented in the data.
- For each dataset, provide not only the full (simulated ground truth) of the disease spread in terms of when, where.
