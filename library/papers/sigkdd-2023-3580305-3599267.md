# Capacity Constrained Influence Maximization in Social Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Influence maximization (IM) aims to identify a small number of influential individuals to maximize the information spread and finds applications in various fields.
- Proposes a new IM variant called capacity constrained influence maximization (CIM), which aims to select a limited number of influential friends for each initial adopter such that the promotion can reach more users.
- Extensively evaluate the performance of 9 approaches on 6 real-world networks, and our solutions outperform all competitors in terms of result quality and running time.
