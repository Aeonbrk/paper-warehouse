# Two Heads Are Better Than One: Generalized Cross-Domain Federated Learning via Dual-Prototype

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Cross-domain federated learning aims to collaboratively train a generalized model across clients with heterogeneous domain distributions without sharing data.
- Their performance is limited by the suboptimal ability to balance the learning of generalized and domain-specific features.
- The experimental results on three datasets demonstrate that our FedOrthrus can achieve up to 14.56% and 3.96% accuracy improvement compared to traditional and prototype-based FL methods.
