# Deep Learning for Prognosis Using Task-fMRI: A Novel Architecture and Training Scheme

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Most existing brain imaging work focuses on resting-state fMRI (rs-fMRI) data where the subject is at rest in the scanner typically for disease diagnosis problems.
- Proposes a deep multi-model architecture to encode multi-view brain activities from t-fMRI data and a multi-layer perceptron ensemble model to combine these view models and make subject-wise predictions.
