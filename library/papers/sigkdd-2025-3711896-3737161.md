# Towards Collaborative Fairness in Federated Learning Under Imbalanced Covariate Shift

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Collaborative fairness is a crucial challenge in federated learning.
- Provide a theoretical analysis of this setting, which motivates the design of FedAKD (Federated Asynchronous Knowledge Distillation) - a simple yet approach that balances accurate prediction with collaborative fairness.
- Experimental results on both public datasets (FashionMNIST and CIFAR10) and a real-world Electronic Health Records (EHR) dataset demonstrate that FedAKD improves collaborative fairness, enhances predictive accuracy.
