# Workplace Recommendation with Temporal Network Objectives

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Workplace communication software such as Microsoft Teams, Slack, and Google Workspace have become integral to workplace collaboration, especially due to the rise of remote work.
- Designs an offline model-based evaluation pipeline to estimate the effects of recommendations on the temporal communication network.
- Develops tractable measures of temporal information flow and design efficient online recommendation algorithms that jointly optimize for relevance and cross-team information flow.
