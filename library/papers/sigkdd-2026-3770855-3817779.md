# DIREC: Diffusion-Based Review-Embedding Generation for Accurate Cross-Domain Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The goal of Cross-domain Recommender System (CDRS) is to recommend items in a target domain for users who have no target-domain interactions by leveraging their source-domain interaction histories.
- Proposes øurs, a conditional-diffusion-based CDRS that generates a target-domain review embedding and then predicts the corresponding rating from the generated embedding.
- Experiments on three cross-domain scenarios show that øurs~ consistently outperforms nine competitors, reducing MAE by up to 14.2%.
