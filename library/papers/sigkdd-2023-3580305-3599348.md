# FedPseudo: Privacy-Preserving Pseudo Value-Based Deep Learning Models for Federated Survival Analysis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Survival analysis, aka time-to-event analysis, has a wide-ranging impact on patient care.
- Addresses these critical challenges by introducing FedPseudo, a pseudo value-based deep learning framework for FSA.
- Conducted experiments on synthetic and real-world survival datasets to demonstrate that our FedPseudo framework achieves better performance than other FSA approaches and performs similarly to the best centrally trained deep survival model.
