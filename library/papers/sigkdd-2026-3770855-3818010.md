# G2PRO: Gradient-guided Graph Prompt Optimization for LLM-based POI Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown strong potential for sequential reasoning, creating new opportunities for next Point-of-Interest (POI) recommendation.
- Proposes G2PRO, a collaborative framework that combines the structural perception of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) with the reasoning capability of LLMs.
- Experiments on four real-world datasets show that G2PRO consistently outperforms traditional and LLM-based baselines.
