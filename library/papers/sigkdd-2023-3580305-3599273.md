# CFGL-LCR: A Counterfactual Graph Learning Framework for Legal Case Retrieval

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Legal case retrieval, which aims to find relevant cases based on a short case description, serves as an important part of modern legal systems.
- Proposes a counterfactual graph learning framework for legal case retrieval.
- Experiments based on two publicly available legal benchmarks demonstrate that our CFGL-LCR can outperform previous methods in legal case retrieval.
