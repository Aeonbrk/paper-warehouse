# Algorithmic Fairness Generalization under Covariate and Dependence Shifts Simultaneously

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The endeavor to preserve the generalization of a fair and invariant classifier across domains, especially in the presence of distribution shifts, becomes a and intricate challenge in machine learning.
- Introduces a simple but approach that aims to learn a fair and invariant classifier by simultaneously addressing both covariate and dependence shifts across domains.
- Empirical studies on four benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach surpasses methods.
