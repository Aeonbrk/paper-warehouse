# DESCN: Deep Entire Space Cross Networks for Individual Treatment Effect Estimation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Causal Inference has wide applications in various areas such as E-commerce and precision medicine, and its performance heavily relies on the accurate estimation of the Individual Treatment Effect (ITE).
- DESCN captures the integrated information of the treatment propensity, the response, and the hidden treatment effect through a cross network in a multi-task learning manner.
- Experiments are conducted on a synthetic dataset and a large-scaled production dataset from the E-commerce voucher distribution business.
