# Mixture-of-Residuals Meets LLM-Semantic Gene Modulation: Spatial Transcriptomic Inference from H&E Images

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spatial transcriptomics (ST) enables the measurement of gene expression in cells while preserving their spatial context within tissues.
- Proposes MiraST, a region-aware framework that combines a graph attention encoder with a Mixture-of-Residual module to capture latent tissue-state-dependent deviations.
- The high cost of ST technologies limits their real-world application, motivating the development of computational methods that infer cellular gene expression from low-cost H&E images that only capture the spatial location of cells.
