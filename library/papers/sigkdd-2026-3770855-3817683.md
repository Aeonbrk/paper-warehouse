# RePatch: Learning Entropy-Guided Patch Structures with Quantized Representations for Time Series Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multivariate time series forecasting is a fundamental yet challenging task due to non-stationary dynamics, evolving temporal structures, and limited generalization across scenarios.
- Proposes RePatch, a two-stage framework that learns adaptive and reusable temporal representations for time series forecasting.
- Experiments across 14 real-world datasets demonstrate that RePatch achieves performance on most benchmarks and consistently generalizes well across diverse transfer scenarios.
