# DPHGNN: A Dual Perspective Hypergraph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Message passing on hypergraphs has been a standard framework for learning higher-order correlations between hypernodes.
- Analyzes the impact of change in hypergraph topology on the suboptimal performance of HGNNs and propose DPHGNN, a dual-perspective HGNN that introduces equivariant operator learning to capture lower-order semantics by inducing topology-aware spatial and.
- Benchmark DPHGNN over eight benchmark hypergraph datasets for the semi-supervised hypernode classification task and obtain performance compared to seven baselines.
