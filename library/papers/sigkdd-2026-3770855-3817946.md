# CMF-ELN: A Cross-Modal-Fused End-to-end Learning Network for Cold-Start Drug-Drug Interaction Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Cold-start drug–drug interactions (DDIs) prediction of new drugs is critical for minimizing unexpected adverse drug reactions.
- To address these issues, this paper proposes a Cross-Modal-Fused End-to-end Learning Network (CMF-ELN) with three components.
- Experiments on two real datasets demonstrate that CMF-ELN achieves higher prediction accuracy and more comprehensive interpretability of mechanisms than its peers.
