# Ask to Know More: Generating Counterfactual Explanations for Fake Claims

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Automated fact-checking systems have been proposed that quickly provide veracity prediction at scale to mitigate the negative influence of fake news on people and on public opinion.
- Proposes elucidating fact-checking predictions using counterfactual explanations to help people understand why a specific piece of news was identified as fake.
- Experiments are conducted on the FEVER dataset for both system and human evaluations.
