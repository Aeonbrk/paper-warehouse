# Causal Estimation of Share-Induced Engagement with Flywheel Effects

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Sustainable user growth in online platforms depends not only on acquiring new users but also on reactivating and engaging existing ones through social sharing features.
- Develops a framework for experiments on sharing features that accounts for interference caused by the flywheel effect and targets a global treatment effect on share-induced engagement.
- Reports the approach on a real-world large-scale online platform and discuss empirical implications for evaluating sharing feature designs.
