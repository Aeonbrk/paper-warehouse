# Stochastic Primal-Dual Decoding for Multiobjective Generative Recommender Systems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent advances in recommender systems (RS) have shown substantial performance gains through generative modelling.
- Proposes a lightweight, inference-time decoding layer that augments autoregressive generative RS to support multiobjective slate generation without modifying or retraining the underlying model.
- Provide theoretical guarantees on constraint violation and regret, and evaluate the proposed approach through offline experiments and a large-scale online A/B experiment in a real-world recommender system.
