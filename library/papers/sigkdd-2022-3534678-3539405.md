# SMORE: Knowledge Graph Completion and Multi-hop Reasoning in Massive Knowledge Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Knowledge graphs (KGs) capture knowledge in the form of head--relation--tail triples and are a crucial component in many AI systems.
- Here present Scalable Multi-hOp REasoning (SMORE), the first general framework for both single-hop and multi-hop reasoning in KGs.
- The key to SMORE's runtime performance is a bidirectional rejection sampling that achieves a square root reduction of the complexity of online training data generation.
