# CodeCAD: A Parametric CAD Dataset for Programmatic 3D Model Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Generative models have recently made progress in generating 3D content from image or text data, yet existing methods mostly rely on implicit or discretized geometric representations that lack parametric structure or design intent.
- These limitations make them poorly suited for computer-aided design (CAD).
- Presents a dataset of over 95,000 native OpenSCAD models, primarily containing mechanical parts and engineering components, designed specifically for the programmatic generation of 3D models.
