# SaFRO: Satisfaction-Aware Fusion via Dual-Relative Policy Optimization for Short-Video Search

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multi-Task Fusion plays a role in industrial short-video search systems by aggregating heterogeneous prediction signals into a unified ranking score.
- Then introduce Dual-Relative Policy Optimization (DRPO), an efficient policy learning method that updates the fusion policy through relative preference comparisons within groups and across batches.
- Offline evaluations and large-scale online A/B tests on Kuaishou short-video search platform demonstrate that SaFRO outperforms baselines, delivering substantial gains in both short-term ranking quality and long-term user retention.
