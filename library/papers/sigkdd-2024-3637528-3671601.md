# Cross-Domain LifeLong Sequential Modeling for Online Click-Through Rate Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Lifelong sequential modeling (LSM) has advanced recommendation systems on social media platforms.
- Proposes the Lifelong Cross Network (LCN), a approach for cross-domain LSM.
- Experiments conducted on both a public dataset and an industrial dataset from the WeChat Channels platform reveal that the LCN outperforms current methods in terms of prediction accuracy and online performance metrics.
