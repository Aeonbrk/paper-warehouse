# MemNovo: Look Back at the Spectrum for Balanced De Novo Peptide Sequencing from Mass Spectrometry

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- De novo peptide sequencing from tandem mass spectrometry is in proteomics, enabling identification of peptides without reference databases.
- Proposes MemNovo, a training-free and plug-and-play mechanism that re-balances peptide and spectral contributions at inference time.
- Experiments on the Nine Species benchmark with two representative baselines, Casanovo and InstaNovo, demonstrate that MemNovo consistently improves both amino acid precision and peptide precision, achieving up to 39.1% relative improvement in peptide precision.
