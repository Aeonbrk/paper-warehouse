# Learning Causal Networks from Episodic Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In numerous real-world domains, spanning from environmental monitoring to long-term medical studies, observations do not arrive in a single batch but rather over time in episodes.
- Proposes the CONTINENT algorithm which, taking inspiration from continual learning, discovers the causal model in an online fashion without having to re-learn the model upon arrival of each new episode.
