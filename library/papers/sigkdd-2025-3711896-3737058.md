# MTM: A Multi-Scale Token Mixing Transformer for Irregular Multivariate Time Series Classification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Irregular multivariate time series (IMTS) is characterized by the lack of synchronized observations across its different channels.
- Proposes a channel-wise token mixing mechanism which proactively chooses important tokens from one channel and mixes them with other channels, to further boost the channel-wise learning of our model.
- Reports that MTM consistently achieves the best performance on all the benchmarks, with improvements of up to 3.8% in AUPRC for classification.
