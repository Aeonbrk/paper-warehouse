# Lightweight Wrappers for Adapting Time Series Foundation Models to Regional Drought Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) demonstrate strong zero-shot forecasting capabilities across diverse domains.
- Introduces a lightweight, black-box adaptation framework (requiring no access to backbone parameters and no backbone fine-tuning) that enhances frozen TSFMs at inference time through two plug-and-play wrappers: SMR2 (Stationarity aware multi-resolution Residual).
