# DuARE: Automatic Road Extraction with Aerial Images and Trajectory Data at Baidu Maps

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The task of road extraction has aroused attention due to its critical role in facilitating urban development and up-to-date map maintenance, which has widespread applications such as navigation and autonomous driving.
- Presents an automatic road extraction solution named DuARE, which is designed to exploit the multimodal knowledge for underlying road extraction in a fully automatic manner.
- Collect a large-scale real-world dataset for paired aerial image and trajectory data, covering over 33,000 km2 in more than 80 cities.
