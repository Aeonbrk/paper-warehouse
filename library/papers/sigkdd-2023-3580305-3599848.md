# Interactive Generalized Additive Model and Its Applications in Electric Load Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Electric load forecasting is an indispensable component of electric power system planning and management.
- As high-stakes decision-making usually follows after load forecasting, model interpretability is crucial for the adoption of forecasting models.
- In both public benchmark and electricity datasets, our interactive GAM outperforms current methods and demonstrates good generalization ability in the cases of extreme weather events.
