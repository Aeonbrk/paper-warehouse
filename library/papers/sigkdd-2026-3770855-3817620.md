# Regime-Adaptive Continual Learning for Portfolio Management

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Financial markets are inherently non-stationary, exhibiting frequent regime shifts and structural changes that render traditional Portfolio Management (PM) approaches ineffective.
- Proposes Regime-aware Continual Adaptive Portfolio management (ReCAP), a framework that integrates CL into PM to address the challenges of dynamic financial environments.
- Existing remedies, such as rolling-window retraining and naive online fine-tuning, are hindered by high computational costs and insufficient knowledge utilization, respectively, resulting in low returns and limited adaptability.
