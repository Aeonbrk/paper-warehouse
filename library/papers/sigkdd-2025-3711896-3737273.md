# VLM as Policy: Common-Law Content Moderation Framework for Short Video Platform

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Exponentially growing short video platforms (SVPs) face challenges in moderating content detrimental to users' mental health, particularly for minors.
- Further proposes our common-law content moderation framework named KuaiMod to address these challenges.
- Offline experiments and large-scale online A/B test demonstrates the superiority of KuaiMod: KuaiMod achieves the best moderation performance on our benchmark.
