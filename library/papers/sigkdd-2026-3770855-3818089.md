# Concept-Residual Representation Expansion for Robustness to Spurious Correlations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Models trained with empirical risk minimization (ERM) are prone to relying on spurious correlations to make predictions.
- Proposes Concept-Residual eXpansion (CRX), a concept-augmented framework that improves robustness by expanding the set of candidate predictive features.
- As a result, models can appear to achieve strong performance by exploiting these correlations, yet fail when the correlation changes or disappears.
