# Parametric Social Identity Injection and Diversification in Public Opinion Simulation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large language models (LLMs) have recently been adopted as synthetic agents for public opinion simulation, offering a alternative to costly and slow human surveys.
- Proposes Parametric Social Identity Injection (PSII), a general framework that injects explicit, parametric representations of demographic attributes and value orientations directly into intermediate hidden states of LLMs.
- Experiments on the World Values Survey using multiple open-source LLMs show that PSII improves distributional fidelity and diversity, reducing KL divergence to real-world survey data while enhancing overall diversity.
