# Modeling Dual Period-Varying Preferences for Takeaway Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Takeaway recommender systems, which aim to accurately provide stores that offer foods meeting users' interests, have served billions of users in our daily life.
- Proposes a time-based decomposition module as well as a time-aware gating mechanism.
- Offline and online experiments demonstrate that our model outperforms methods on real-world datasets and it is capable of modeling the dual period-varying preferences.
