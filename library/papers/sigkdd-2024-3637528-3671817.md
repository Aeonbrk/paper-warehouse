# Counteracting Duration Bias in Video Recommendation via Counterfactual Watch Time

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In video recommendation, an ongoing effort is to satisfy users' personalized information needs by leveraging their logged watch time.
- Introduces the counterfactual watch time (CWT), the potential watch time a user would spend on the video if its duration is sufficiently long.
- Experiments on three real video recommendation datasets and online A/B testing demonstrated that CWM effectively enhanced video recommendation accuracy and counteracted the duration bias.
