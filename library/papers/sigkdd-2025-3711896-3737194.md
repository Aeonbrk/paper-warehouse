# ASTNet: Asynchronous Spatio-Temporal Network for Large-Scale Chemical Sensor Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The chemical industry is faced with the urgent challenge of effectively harnessing the vast amounts of time-series data generated by thousands of sensors.
- ASTNet integrates an asynchronous spatiotemporal modeling framework that combines temporal and spatial encoders, enabling concurrent learning of temporal and spatial dependencies while reducing computational latency.
- Experiments on three real-world chemical sensor datasets demonstrate that ASTNet outperforms SOTA methods in terms of both prediction accuracy and computational efficiency, making ASTNet successfully deployed in chemical engineering industrial scenarios.
