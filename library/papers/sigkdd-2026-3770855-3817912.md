# SCALE: Style-Causal Disentanglement with Adaptive Lifelong Expert for Online Latent-domain Anomaly Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Time-series anomaly detection in real-world streams is often challenged by evolving operating conditions, where distribution shifts can be easily mistaken for anomalies.
- Style–Causal Disentanglement with Adaptive Lifelong Expert (SCALE) is introduced as an online framework that disentangles each window into domain-invariant causal features and domain-specific style features, supports continual adaptation with a dynamic expandable style-expert pool.
- Experiments are conducted on diverse real-world datasets, together with a new multi-domain time-series anomaly detection benchmark for systematically evaluating online latent-domain scenarios.
