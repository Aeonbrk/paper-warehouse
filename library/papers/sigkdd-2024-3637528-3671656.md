# Face4Rag: Factual Consistency Evaluation for Retrieval Augmented Generation in Chinese

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The prevailing issue of factual inconsistency errors in conventional Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) motivates the study of Factual Consistency Evaluation (FCE).
- To fix this issue, further proposes a new method called L-Face4RAG with two designs of logic-preserving answer decomposition and fact-logic FCE.
- Our benchmark consists of a synthetic dataset built upon a carefully designed typology for factuality inconsistency error and a real-world dataset constructed from six commonly used LLMs, enabling evaluation of FCE methods on specific.
