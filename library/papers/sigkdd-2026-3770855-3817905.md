# UniAd: Unified Adversarial Alignment for Unsupervised Cross-Domain Industrial Anomaly Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In practical applications, early Industrial Anomaly Detection (IAD) methods are typically trained on specific scenarios with abundant labeled samples.
- Proposes a Unified Adversarial (UniAd) alignment model for IAD, which is the first unsupervised domain adaptation approach specifically designed for industrial anomaly detection.
- Experiments conducted on three public IAD benchmark datasets demonstrate that our proposed UniAd achieves state–of–the–art (SOTA) performance.
