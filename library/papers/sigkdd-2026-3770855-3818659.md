# The Decision Twin: Metaverse Patient Digital Twins as Executable Clinical Reality

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Clinical AI is no longer bottlenecked only by model performance; it is bottlenecked by the accountable interaction loop through which clinicians and patients inspect evidence, test alternatives, and remain responsible for decisions under uncertainty.
- Argue that today's digital-twin systems, XR interfaces, and foundation-model copilots each address part of this loop, but they fail when deployed as separate products: predictions are not replayable across time, XR becomes descriptive visualization.
