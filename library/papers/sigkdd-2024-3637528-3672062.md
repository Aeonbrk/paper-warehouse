# RHiOTS: A Framework for Evaluating Hierarchical Time Series Forecasting Algorithms

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Introduces the Robustness of Hierarchically Organized Time Series (RHiOTS) framework, designed to assess the robustness of hierarchical time series forecasting models and algorithms on real-world datasets.
- This approach allows an in-depth analysis of algorithm and model behavior under diverse conditions.
- Current empirical evaluations of forecasting methods are often limited to a small set of benchmark datasets, offering a narrow view of algorithm behavior.
