# Temporal Prototype-Aware Learning for Active Voltage Control on Power Distribution Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Active Voltage Control (AVC) on the Power Distribution Networks (PDNs) aims to stabilize the voltage levels to ensure efficient and reliable operation of power systems.
- Proposes a temporal prototype-aware learning method, abbreviated as TPA, to learn time-adaptive AVC under short-term training trajectories.
- Due to the dynamic nature of load demands and renewable energy, the operation states of real-world PDNs may exhibit distribution shifts across varying timescales (e.g., daily and seasonal changes).
