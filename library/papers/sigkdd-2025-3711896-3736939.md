# Equivariant and Invariant Message Passing for Global Subseasonal-to-seasonal Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate weather forecasting on Subseasonal-to-Seasonal (S2S) timescale is critical to human society such as agriculture planning and extreme weather preparation.
- Proposes a graph-based Equivariant and Invariant Message Passing (EIMP) framework that directly processes spherical grid data.
- Experiments on the Earth Reanalysis 5 (ERA5) reanalysis dataset of 41 years demonstrate the proposed model achieves improvement over advanced data-driven models and skillful numerical ECMWF systems.
