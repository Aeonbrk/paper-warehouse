# Adapting Large Vision-Language Models to Visually-Aware Conversational Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Conversational recommender systems engage users in dialogues to refine their needs and provide more personalized suggestions.
- Proposes LaViC (Large Vision-Language Conversational Recommendation Framework), a approach that integrates compact image representations into dialogue-based recommendation systems.
- To support rigorous evaluation of visually-aware conversational recommendation, construct a new dataset by aligning Reddit conversations with Amazon product listings across multiple visually oriented categories (e.g., fashion, beauty, and home).
