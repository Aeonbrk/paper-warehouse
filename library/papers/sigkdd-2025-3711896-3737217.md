# Evaluating Decision Rules Across Many Weak Experiments

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Technology firms conduct randomized controlled experiments (''A/B tests'') to learn which actions to take to improve business outcomes.
- Develops a cross-validation estimator that is much less biased than the naive plug-in estimator under conditions realistic to digital experimentation.
- To effectively scale experimentation, firms rely on decision rules: standard operating procedures for mapping the results of an experiment to a choice of treatment arm to launch to the general user population.
