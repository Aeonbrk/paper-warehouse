# Interdependent Causal Networks for Root Cause Localization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The goal of root cause analysis is to identify the underlying causes of system problems by discovering and analyzing the causal structure from system monitoring data.
- Proposes REASON, a framework that enables the automatic discovery of both intra-level (i.e., within-network) and inter-level (i.e., across-network) causal relationships for root cause localization.
- Existing methods mainly focus on the construction of a single isolated causal network, whereas many real-world systems are complex and exhibit interdependent structures (i.e., multiple networks of a system are interconnected by cross-network links).
