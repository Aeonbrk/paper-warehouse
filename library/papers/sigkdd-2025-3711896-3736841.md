# An Efficient White-box LLM Watermarking for IP Protection on Online Market Platforms

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online market platforms serve as a central hub for sharing and deploying AI models among researchers, developers, and companies.
- This method treats LLMs as multi-layered matrices while embedding watermarks only relies on modifying the model's weights.
- Notably, all phases are training-free with low hardware resources, making it efficient for online platforms.
