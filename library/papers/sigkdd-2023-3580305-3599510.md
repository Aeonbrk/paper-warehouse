# Spatial Heterophily Aware Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have been broadly applied in many urban applications upon formulating a city as an urban graph whose nodes are urban objects like regions or points of interest.
- Proposes a Spatial Heterophily Aware Graph Neural Network (SHGNN), to tackle the spatial diversity of heterophily of urban graphs.
- Experiments on three real-world urban datasets report higher performance for our SHGNN over several its competitors.
