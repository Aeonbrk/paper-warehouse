# HOBA: Hierarchical On-Policy Bidding Agents for Adaptive Online Advertising

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online advertising bidding systems typically deploy multiple offline-trained expert models (e.g., PID controllers, model predictive control, offline RL policies) but face two critical limitations: lack of online adaptability to non-stationary auction markets.
- Proposes HOBA (Hierarchical On-policy Bidding Agents), a hierarchical reinforcement learning framework that decouples strategic reasoning, model selection, and bid execution across three time scales.
- This design confines online learning to discrete expert selection rather than continuous bid optimization, reducing exploration risk while maintaining adaptability.
