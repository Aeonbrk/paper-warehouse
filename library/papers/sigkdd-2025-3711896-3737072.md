# Pairwise Sample Complexity for Fair Active Ranking with Cascaded Norm Objectives

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Ranking systems based on pairwise comparisons are fundamental in decision-making applications, yet fairness concerns remain largely unaddressed, particularly in active ranking frameworks.
- Proposes a fairness-aware active ranking approach that adaptively queries pairwise preferences to construct rankings that are both probably approximately correct (PAC) and fair.
- Empirical evaluations on real-world datasets, including COMPAS and German Credit, demonstrate the efficiency of our approach, reducing sample complexity while achieving fairer rankings.
