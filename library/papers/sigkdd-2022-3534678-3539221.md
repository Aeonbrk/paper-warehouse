# CausalInt: Causal Inspired Intervention for Multi-Scenario Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Building appropriate scenarios to meet the personalized demands of different user groups is a common practice.
- First do analysis on multi-scenario modeling with causal graph from the perspective of users and modeling processes, and then propose the Causal Inspired Intervention (CausalInt) framework for multi-scenario recommendation.
- Offline experiments over two real-world dataset and online A/B test are conducted to report higher performance for CausalInt.
