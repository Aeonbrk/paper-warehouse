# Realistic Simulation and Evaluation of E-CNY Lifecycle Transactions for Anti-Money Laundering

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) are a transformative innovation in global finance, with China leading the way through the Digital RMB (E-CNY).
- Introduces SimECNY, a synthetic simulation framework that models the full E-CNY account lifecycle from creation to transaction and closure.
- Existing anti-money laundering (AML) synthetic and real-world datasets, mainly in cryptocurrency and traditional banking, focus on transaction records while ignoring the behaviors of account creation and closure.
