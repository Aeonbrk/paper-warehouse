## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Heterogeneous graph learning aims to capture complex relationships and diverse relational semantics among entities in a heterogeneous graph to obtain meaningful representations for nodes and edges.
- Proposes HiGPT, a general large graph model with Heterogeneous graph instruction-tuning paradigm.
