# X-MethaneWet: A Cross-scale Global Wetland Methane Emission Benchmark Dataset for Advancing Science Discovery with AI

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Methane (CH4) is the second most greenhouse gas after carbon dioxide and plays a crucial role in climate change due to its high global warming potential.
- Introduces the first-of-its-kind cross-scale global wetland methane benchmark dataset (X-MethaneWet), which synthesizes physics-based model simulation data from TEM-MDM and the real-world observation data from FLUXNET-CH4.
