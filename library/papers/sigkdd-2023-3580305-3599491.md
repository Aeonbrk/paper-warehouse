# Robust Positive-Unlabeled Learning via Noise Negative Sample Self-correction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Learning from positive and unlabeled data is known as positive-unlabeled (PU) learning in literature and has attracted much attention in recent years.
- Proposes a new robust PU learning method with a training strategy motivated by the nature of human learning: easy cases should be learned first.
- Experimental validations over a wide range of learning tasks show that this approach can effectively improve the accuracy and stability of learning with positive and unlabeled data.
