# Modeling User Retention through Generative Flow Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recommender systems aim to fulfill the user's daily demands.
- Regard the retention signal as an overall estimation of the user's end-of-session satisfaction and propose to estimate this signal through a probabilistic flow.
- Verify the effectiveness of our method through both offline empirical studies on two public datasets and online A/B tests in an industrial platform.
