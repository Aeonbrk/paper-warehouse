# Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic Autonomous Vehicle Navigation at Intelligent Intersections

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent years have witnessed the rapid development of the Cooperative Vehicle Infrastructure System (CVIS).
- Designs a graph-enhanced multi-agent decentralized bi-directional hierarchical reinforcement learning framework by regarding TLs as manager agents and AVs as worker agents.
- Finally, experiments on one synthetic and two real-world network-level datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed framework in terms of improving travel efficiency.
