# Task Negative Sampling Enhanced Graph Few-shot Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Few-Shot Node Classification (GFSNC) has emerged as a approach to address the challenge of learning with limited labeled data in graph-structured networks.
- By incorporating samples from classes outside the current meta-task, our framework enables the model to gradually learn the global distribution of the graph data.
- Experiments on four benchmark datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of TaskNS, achieving average improvements of 4.6% in accuracy (ACC) and 4.9% in F1-score over methods.
