# A Unified Online-Offline Framework for Co-Branding Campaign Recommendations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Co-branding has become a vital strategy for businesses aiming to expand market reach within recommendation systems.
- Presents a theoretical analysis of our approach, establishing a highly nontrivial sublinear regret bound for online learning in the complex co-branding problem, and enhancing the approximation guarantee for the NP-hard offline budget allocation optimization.
- Provide the first systematic study of this problem and proposes a unified online-offline framework to enable co-branding recommendations.
