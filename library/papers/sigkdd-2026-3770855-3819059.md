# Stabilizing Physics-Informed Consistency Models via Structure-Preserving Training

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes a physics-informed consistency modeling framework for solving partial differential equations (PDEs) via fast, few-step generative inference.
- Introduces a structure-preserving two-stage training strategy that decouples distribution learning from physics enforcement by freezing the coefficient decoder during physics-informed fine-tuning.
- Reports that forward solutions can be obtained via a projection-based zero-shot inpainting procedure, achieving consistent accuracy of diffusion baselines with orders of magnitude reduction in computational cost.
