# Condition-Gated Reasoning for Context-Dependent Biomedical Question Answering

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Current biomedical question answering (QA) systems often assume that medical knowledge applies uniformly, yet real-world clinical reasoning is inherently conditional: nearly every decision depends on patient-specific factors such as comorbidities and contraindications.
- Proposes Condition-Gated Reasoning (CGR), a framework that constructs condition-aware knowledge graphs and selectively activates or prunes reasoning paths based on query conditions.
- Proposes CondMedQA, the first benchmark for conditional biomedical QA, consisting of multi-hop questions whose answers vary with patient conditions.
