# LGFlow: Long-term Fluid Simulation with Adaptive Spatio-Temporal Reduction on Mesh Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Fluid simulation is of great importance to many real-world science and engineering applications.
- Traditional Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) solvers suffer from substantial computational and memory costs, caused by long-term horizons with many time steps and a large number of discretized mesh elements to model the fluid space.
- Though Mesh-based Graph Networks (MGNs) perform as surrogate models of fluid simulations with fast inference time and competitive accuracy, they still struggle with expensive spatial representation and limited temporal receptive field.
