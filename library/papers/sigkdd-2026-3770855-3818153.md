# HyperEdit: Mitigating Hallucinations of Large Language Models via Hyperbolic Representation Editing

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large language models (LLMs) often generate hallucinations, undermining the reliability of their outputs.
- Proposes HyperEdit, a inference-time intervention method that encodes truthful and untruthful content as an entailment hierarchy and performs representation editing in the hyperbolic space to activate the truthfulness of LLMs.
