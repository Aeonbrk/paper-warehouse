## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Foundation Models (GFMs) have emerged as a research topic in graph machine learning.
- Proposes DCGFM, a plug-and-play approach for Data-Centric GFM that incorporates the idea of data pruning to remove redundant and less informative subgraphs from the pre-training data, thereby improving both efficiency and effectiveness.
