# Towards Adaptive Neighborhood for Advancing Temporal Interaction Graph Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Temporal Graph Networks (TGNs) have demonstrated their performance in modeling temporal interaction graphs.
- Presents SEAN (Selective Encoding for Adaptive Neighborhood), a flexible plug-and-play model that can be seamlessly integrated with existing TGNs, effectively boosting their performance.
- Conduct experiments by integrating SEAN into three representative TGNs, evaluating their performance on four public datasets and one financial benchmark dataset introduced in this paper.
