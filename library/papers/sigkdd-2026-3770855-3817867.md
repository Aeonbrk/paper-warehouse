# PrismFed: Joint Optimization via Dynamic Bayesian Persuasion for Multi-Task Federated Learning under Incomplete Information

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Efficiently allocating limited communication resources in multi-task federated learning is a fundamental challenge.
- Proposes PrismFed, a joint optimization framework via dynamic Bayesian persuasion for multi-task federated learning under incomplete information.
- Reports evaluation showing PrismFed improves task-weighted accuracy and system revenue across two task combinations, achieving gains of 2.51% over the strongest baseline.
