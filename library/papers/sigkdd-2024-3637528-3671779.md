# Neural Manifold Operators for Learning the Evolution of Physical Dynamics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modeling the evolution of physical dynamics is a foundational problem in science and engineering, and it is regarded as the modeling of an operator mapping between infinite-dimensional functional spaces.
- Operator learning methods, learning the underlying infinite-dimensional operator in a high-dimensional latent space, have shown potential in modeling physical dynamics.
- NMO achieves performance in statistical and physical metrics and gains 23.35% average improvement on three real-world scenarios and four equation-governed scenarios across a wide range of multi-disciplinary fields.
