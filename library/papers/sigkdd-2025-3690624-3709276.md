# Mixed Blessing: Class-Wise Embedding guided Instance-Dependent Partial Label Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In partial label learning (PLL), every sample is associated with a candidate label set comprising the ground-truth label and several noisy labels.
- The conventional PLL assumes the noisy labels are randomly generated (instance-independent).
- Experimental comparisons with twelve methods on six benchmark data sets, including four fine-grained data sets, demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
