# GraphSHA: Synthesizing Harder Samples for Class-Imbalanced Node Classification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Class imbalance is the phenomenon that some classes have much fewer instances than others, which is ubiquitous in real-world graph-structured scenarios.
- Are naturally inspired to enlarge the decision boundaries of minor classes and proposes a general framework GraphSHA by Synthesizing HArder minor samples.
- Empirically, GraphSHA shows its effectiveness in enlarging the decision boundaries of minor classes, as it outperforms various baseline methods in class-imbalanced node classification with different GNN backbone encoders over seven public benchmark datasets.
