# Granger Causal Chain Discovery for Sepsis-Associated Derangements via Continuous-Time Hawkes Processes

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modern health care systems are conducting continuous, automated surveillance of the electronic medical record (EMR) to identify adverse events with increasing frequency.
- Proposes a linear multivariate Hawkes process model, coupled with ReLU link function, to recover a Granger Causal (GC) graph with both exciting and inhibiting effects.
