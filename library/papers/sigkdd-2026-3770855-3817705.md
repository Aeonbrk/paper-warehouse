# VideoExplorer: Advancing Long-Horizon Video Understanding via Hierarchical Orchestration

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Current agentic frameworks for Long-Video Understanding (LVU) remain limited by two critical problems: ineffective control.
- Proposes VideoExplorer, a agentic system designed to advance long-video reasoning on top of structured control and trajectory-level optimization.
- Experiments on MLVU, LVBench, and MH-NIAH demonstrate that VideoExplorer consistently outperforms monolithic baselines in both accuracy and efficiency, validating the effectiveness of structured control and trajectory-level optimization in long-video reasoning.
