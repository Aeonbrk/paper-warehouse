# Smallholder Agricultural Landscape Understanding at a National Scale

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Comprehensive agricultural landscape understanding is critical for addressing global challenges in food security, climate change, and resource management.
- Previous efforts to develop such land use maps have been limited by a narrow focus on methods for field delineation only, and also do not develop robust post-processing steps essential for real-world deployment.
- Our system is refined for real-world application using post-processing heuristics to ensure map consistency and accuracy, and is validated through a rigorous, multi-faceted evaluation process.
