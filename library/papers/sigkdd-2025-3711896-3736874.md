# Causal Discovery through Synergizing Large Language Model and Data-Driven Reasoning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Revealing the underlying causal mechanisms in the real world is critical for scientific and technical progress.
- Proposes a causal modeling framework, LLM-CD, which integrates the metadata-based reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) with the data-driven modeling abilities of TCDA for causal discovery.
- Utilize a large-scale de-identified real patient dataset provided by a hospital, a new dataset extracted from MIMIC-IV about the same disease (lung cancer), and two benchmark datasets to comprehensively evaluate LLM-CD.
