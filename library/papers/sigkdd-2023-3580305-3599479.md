# QTIAH-GNN: Quantity and Topology Imbalance-aware Heterogeneous Graph Neural Network for Bankruptcy Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes a bankruptcy prediction model called the Quantity and Topology Imbalance-Aware Heterogeneous Graph Neural Network (QTIAH-GNN) to boost the final performance.
- In experiments, evaluate the proposed QTIAH-GNN on two large-scale, real-world datasets.
