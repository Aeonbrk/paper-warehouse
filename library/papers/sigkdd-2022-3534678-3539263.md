# On-Device Learning for Model Personalization with Large-Scale Cloud-Coordinated Domain Adaption

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Cloud-based learning is currently the mainstream in both academia and industry.
- Proposes a new device-cloud collaborative learning framework under the paradigm of domain adaption, called MPDA, to break the dilemmas of purely cloud-based learning and on-device training.
- Extensively evaluate over the public MovieLens 20M and Amazon Electronics datasets, as well as an industrial dataset collected from Mobile Taobao over a period of 30 days.
