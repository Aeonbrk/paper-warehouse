# Rethinking Time Series Anomaly Detection from a Dynamic Perspective: Temporal-Frequency-Curvature Fusion

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Time series anomaly detection (TSAD) plays a role in domains ranging from industrial automation to IT operations and healthcare monitoring.
- Introduces TFC, a unified TSAD framework that, to our knowledge, is the first to integrate a curvature perspective alongside established temporal and frequency analyses.
- Across six benchmarks and baselines, TFC achieves existing average Best-F1 and Event-F1, yielding a 10.8% average improvement over a strong baseline while maintaining stable performance across window lengths and favorable accuracy.
