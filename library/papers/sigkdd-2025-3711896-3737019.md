# Learning Generalized and Flexible Trajectory Models from Omni-Semantic Supervision

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The widespread adoption of mobile devices and data collection technologies has led to an exponential increase in trajectory data, presenting challenges in spatio-temporal data mining, particularly for efficient and accurate trajectory retrieval.
- Proposes OmniTraj, a generalized and flexible omni-semantic trajectory retrieval framework that integrates four complementary modalities or semantics--raw trajectories, topology, road segments, and regions--into a unified system.
- Experiments on two real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of OmniTraj in handling large-scale data, providing flexible, multi-modality queries, and supporting downstream tasks and applications.
