# Temporal Dynamics-Aware Adversarial Attacks on Discrete-Time Dynamic Graph Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Real-world graphs such as social networks, communication networks, and rating networks are constantly evolving over time.
- Presents a theoretically-motivated Projected Gradient Descent approach for dynamic graphs to find perturbations under the TDAP constraint.
- Extend our approach to a more practical online setting where graphs become available in real-time and show up to 5x performance over baselines show that our approach successfully evades neural approaches for anomaly detection.
