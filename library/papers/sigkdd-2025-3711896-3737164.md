# Towards Trajectory Anomaly Detection: a Fine-Grained and Noise-Resilient Framework

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Trajectory anomaly detection aims to identify patterns in trajectory data that deviate from normal behavior, such as taxi detours, and plays a crucial role in urban computing.
- Proposes an LLM-driven approach, FOTraj, for fine-grained and noise-resilient trajectory anomaly detection.
- Real-world trajectories are inherently complex, containing diverse anomalies and unavoidable noise.
