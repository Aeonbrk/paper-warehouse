# Generalizable Multi-Pass Training of Ads Recommendation Models with Foundation Model Guidance

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- High-capacity Click-Through Rate (CTR) models for ads recommendation often exhibit pronounced one-epoch overfitting: performance peaks after a single training pass (epoch) over the data.
- Proposes TeMPO, a principled framework for generalizable multi-pass training of recommendation models that leverages supervision from a teacher recommendation Foundation Model (FM).
