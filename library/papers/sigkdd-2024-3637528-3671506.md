# Future Impact Decomposition in Request-level Recommendations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In recommender systems, reinforcement learning solutions have shown results in optimizing the interaction sequence between users and the system over the long-term performance.
- Proposes a model-based re-weighting framework with adversarial learning that further boost the performance and investigate its correlation with the reward-based strategy.
- Support this claim by comparing the performance of standard request-level methods with the proposed item-level actor-critic framework in both simulation and online experiments.
