# Adaptive Learning for Weakly Labeled Streams

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In plenty of real-world applications, data are collected in a streaming fashion, and their accurate labels are hard to obtain.
- Proposes an adaptive learning algorithm for weakly labeled data streams via model reuse and effectively alleviate the negative influence of label noise with unlabeled data.
- Proposes a noise transition matrix estimation approach for data streams with scarce noisy labels by online anchor points identification.
