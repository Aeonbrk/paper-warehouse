# ARIA: A Causal-Aware Framework for Rescuing LLM Reasoning in Trustworthy Materials Discovery

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Generative models have revolutionized the process of materials discovery, yet they often fail to satisfy underlying physical causality.
- Introduces ARIA, a causal-aware framework that conditions knowledge use on mechanistic completeness.
- ARIA mitigates contextual tunneling, improves over unaugmented and naive KG-augmented baselines, and provides further gains when an online literature search is used for evidence enrichment.
