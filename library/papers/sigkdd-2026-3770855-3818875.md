# BulkShield: A Proactive Framework for Detecting Bulk Refund Booking in Ticketing Systems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Bulk Refund Booking (BRB) is a system-level demand distortion phenomenon in railway ticketing systems, where repeated strategic booking--refund behaviors create artificial seat shortages and revenue loss despite sufficient physical capacity.
- Proposes BulkShield, an end-to-end proactive framework that combines unsupervised risk pseudo-labeling, booking-time sequence modeling, and LLM-assisted explanations grounded in model-identified behavioral evidence.
- To address these challenges, conduct the first systematic empirical study of BRB using large-scale real-world railway ticketing logs and show that BRB risk emerges as a cumulative, sequential behavioral process rather than as isolated.
