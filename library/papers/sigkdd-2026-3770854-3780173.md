# Rethinking Temporal Knowledge Graph Representation Learning: From Entities to Evolutionary Event-Centric Clusters

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Existing research on Temporal Knowledge Graph (TKG) representation learning focuses on decomposing events into entities and relations, then employing various approaches to learn entity and relation representations in a low-dimensional vector space.
- Proposes a Heterogeneous evolutionary Event cluster Aware Representation learning approach in Temporal Knowledge Graphs (HEART), which is the first event-centric approach in TKGs.
- Experimental results on seven real-world datasets demonstrate that HEART achieves the performance, outperforming the runner-up by an average of 3.89%, 8.14%, 5.97%, and 6.92% in MRR, Hits@1, Hits@3, and Hits@10, respectively.
