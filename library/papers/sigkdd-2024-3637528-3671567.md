# Optimizing Smartphone App Usage Prediction: A Click-Through Rate Ranking Approach

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Over the past decade, smartphones have become indispensable personal mobile devices, experiencing a surge in software apps.
- To address these challenges, this paper proposes a click-through rate (CTR) ranking-based method for predicting app usage.
- The results demonstrate that our approach is able to outperform methods, with improvements over 4.93% in top-3 accuracy and 6.64% in top-5 accuracy.
