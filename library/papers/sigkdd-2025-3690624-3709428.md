# Producer-Side Experiments Based on Counterfactual Interleaving Designs for Online Recommender Systems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recommender systems play a crucial role in online platforms, providing personalized recommendations for purchases, content consumption, and interpersonal connections.
- While consumer-side experiments are relatively straightforward to design and commonly employed to assess ranking changes' effects on the behavior of consumers (buyers, viewers, etc.), designing producer-side experiments for an online recommender/ranking system is notably.
- To optimize online recommender systems, A/B tests serve as the golden standard for comparing different ranking models and evaluating their impacts on both sides.
