# Uncovering Insights of Compound Flooding with Data-Driven AI

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Compound flooding, driven by nonlinear interactions between multiple hydrometeorological factors, poses a challenge to hazard prevention.
- By framing data-driven models as tools for scientific inquiry rather than prediction alone, this study offers new insights into the mechanisms of compound flooding and informs the design of more physically grounded early-warning systems.
- Our dataset and code are publicly available at https://github.com/AslanDing/SFBench.
