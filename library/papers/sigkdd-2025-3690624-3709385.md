# BackdoorMBTI: A Backdoor Learning Multimodal Benchmark Tool Kit for Backdoor Defense Evaluation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Over the past few years, the emergence of backdoor attacks has presented challenges to deep learning systems, allowing attackers to insert backdoors into neural networks.
- Introduces BackdoorMBTI, the first backdoor learning toolkit and benchmark designed for multimodal evaluation across three representative modalities from eleven commonly used datasets.
