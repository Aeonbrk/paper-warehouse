# Counterfactual Fairness Through Transforming Data Orthogonal to Bias

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Machine learning models have demonstrated exceptional capabilities in solving complex problems across a variety of domains.
- Proposes a data pre-processing algorithm, Orthogonal to Bias (OB), which is designed to eliminate the influence of a group of continuous sensitive variables, thereby promoting counterfactual fairness in machine learning applications.
- Empirical evaluations on both simulated and real-world datasets-spanning scenarios with both discrete and continuous sensitive variables-demonstrate that our method effectively promotes fairer outcomes without compromising predictive accuracy.
