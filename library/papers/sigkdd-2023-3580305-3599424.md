# Mastering Stock Markets with Efficient Mixture of Diversified Trading Experts

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Quantitative stock investment is a fundamental financial task that highly relies on accurate prediction of market status and profitable investment decision making.
- Proposes AlphaMix, a three-stage mixture-of-experts (MoE) framework for quantitative investment to mimic the efficient bottom-up hierarchical trading strategy design workflow of successful trading companies.
- One major limitation of existing works is that investment decisions are made based on one individual neural network predictor with high uncertainty, which is inconsistent with the workflow in real-world trading firms.
