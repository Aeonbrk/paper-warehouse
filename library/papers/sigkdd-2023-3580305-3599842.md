# iETA: A Robust and Scalable Incremental Learning Framework for Time-of-Arrival Estimation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Time-of-arrival estimation or Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) has become an indispensable building block of modern intelligent transportation systems.
- Proposes a robust and scalable incremental ETA learning framework, iETA, to continuously exploit spatio-temporal traffic patterns from massive floating-car data and thus achieve better estimation performances.
- While many efforts have been made for time-of-arrival estimation, most of them have scalability and robustness issues when dealing with real-world large-scale ETA scenarios.
