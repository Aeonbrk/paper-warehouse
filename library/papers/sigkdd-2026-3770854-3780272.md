# SEW: Strengthening Robustness of Black-box DNN Watermarking via Specificity Enhancement

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- To ensure the responsible distribution and use of open-source deep neural networks (DNNs), DNN watermarking has become a crucial technique to trace and verify unauthorized model replication or misuse.
- Introduces Specificity-Enhanced Watermarking (SEW), a new method that improves specificity by reducing the association between the watermark and approximate keys.
- Explores black-box DNN watermarking specificity, which refers to the accuracy of a watermark's response to a key.
