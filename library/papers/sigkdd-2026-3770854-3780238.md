# EmoRAG: Evaluating RAG Robustness to Symbolic Perturbations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems are increasingly central to robust AI, enhancing large language model (LLM) faithfulness by incorporating external knowledge.
- Proposes targeted defenses, analyzing their strengths and limitations in mitigating emoticon-based perturbations.
- Our experiment across general question-answering and code domains, using a range of retrievers and generators, reveals three key findings: (I) Single-Emoticon Disaster: Minimal emoticon injections cause maximal disruptions, with a single emoticon almost 100%.
