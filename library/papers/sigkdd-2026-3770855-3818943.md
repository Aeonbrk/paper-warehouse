# PaperRepro: Automated Computational Reproducibility Assessment for Social Science Papers

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Computational reproducibility is essential for the credibility of scientific findings, particularly in the social sciences, where findings often inform real-world decisions.
- Proposes PaperRepro, a two-stage, multi-agent approach that separates execution from evaluation.
- On REPRO-Bench, a social science reproducibility assessment benchmark, PaperRepro achieves the best overall performance, with a 21.9% relative improvement in score-agreement accuracy over the strongest prior baseline.
