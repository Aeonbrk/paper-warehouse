# ERNet: Unsupervised Collective Extraction and Registration in Neuroimaging Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Brain extraction and registration are important preprocessing steps in neuroimaging data analysis.
- Proposes a unified end-to-end framework, called ERNet (Extraction-Registration Network), to jointly optimize the extraction and registration tasks, allowing feedback between them.
- Experiment results on real-world datasets show that our proposed method can effectively improve the performance on extraction and registration tasks in neuroimaging data.
