# Evading Community Detection via Counterfactual Neighborhood Search

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Community detection techniques are useful for social media platforms to discover tightly connected groups of users who share common interests.
- Addresses the challenge of community membership hiding, which involves strategically altering the structural properties of a network graph to prevent one or more nodes from being identified by a given community detection algorithm.
- Reports evaluation showing our method outperforms existing baselines, striking the best balance between accuracy and cost.
