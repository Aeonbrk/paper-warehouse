# Making Temporal Betweenness Computation Faster and Restless

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Buss et al [KDD 2020] recently proved that the problem of computing the betweenness of all nodes of a temporal graph is computationally hard in the case of foremost and fastest paths.
- A new algorithm for temporal betweenness computation is introduced in this paper.
- Investigates six public transit networks including Berlin, Rome, and Paris.
