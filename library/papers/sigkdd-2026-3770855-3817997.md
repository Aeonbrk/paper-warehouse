# Find Tailored Step Example for Next Step: a Targeted Step-wise Retrieval Framework for Guiding LLM Reasoning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large language models (LLMs) have shown strong performance in mathematical reasoning, supported by approaches such as In-Context Learning (ICL) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- Proposes Step-wise Training for In-context Reasoning (STIR) to provide step-synchronized and logically targeted guidance to enhance the model's mathematical reasoning capabilities.
- Experiments on seven benchmarks demonstrate that STIR achieves accuracy improvements ranging from 1.86% to 17.96%, maintaining lower token efficiency than baselines.
