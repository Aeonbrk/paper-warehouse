# Spectral-Inspired Neural Operator Learning with Limited Data and Unknown Physics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Learning PDE dynamics from limited data with unknown physics is challenging.
- Proposes Spectral-Inspired Neural Operator (SINO), which can model complex systems from just 5 trajectories without requiring explicit PDE terms.
- Experiments on both 2D and 3D PDE benchmarks demonstrate that SINO achieves performance, with improvements of 1-2 orders of magnitude in accuracy.
