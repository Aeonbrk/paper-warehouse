# Breaking Bad Molecules: Are MLLMs Ready for Structure-Level Molecular Detoxification?

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Toxicity remains a leading cause of early-stage drug development failure.
- Proposes an automated evaluation framework, ToxiEval, which integrates toxicity endpoint prediction, synthetic accessibility, drug-likeness, and structural similarity into a high-throughput evaluation chain for repair success.
- Introduces ToxiMol, the first benchmark task for general-purpose Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) focused on molecular toxicity repair.
