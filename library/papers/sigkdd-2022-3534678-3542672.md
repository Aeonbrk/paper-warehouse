# Scalable Online Disease Diagnosis via Multi-Model-Fused Actor-Critic Reinforcement Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes a Multi-Model-Fused Actor-Critic (MMF-AC) RL framework that consists of a generative actor network and a diagnostic critic network.
- Conduct experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets for empirical evaluations.
