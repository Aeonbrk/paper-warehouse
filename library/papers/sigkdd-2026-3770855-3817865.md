# Directional Time Series Editing via Retrieval-Guided Jacobian-Vector Inference

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Time Series Editing (TSE) synthesizes realistic time series by modifying existing trajectories under user-specified conditions, with growing importance across many applications.
- Introduces a new TSE setting for continuous, magnitude-aware condition transitions and propose JAVELIN, a retrieval-guided framework for directional editing via JAcobian-VEctor Latent INference.
- Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets show that JAVELIN produces high-fidelity, condition-coherent, and controllable edits, substantially outperforming existing generation and editing baselines.
