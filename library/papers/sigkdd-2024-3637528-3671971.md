# PATE: Proximity-Aware Time Series Anomaly Evaluation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Evaluating anomaly detection algorithms in time series data is critical as inaccuracies can lead to flawed decision-making in various domains where real-time analytics and data-driven strategies are essential.
- Introduces Proximity-Aware Time series anomaly Evaluation (PATE), a evaluation metric that incorporates the temporal relationship between prediction and anomaly intervals.
- Using these weights, PATE computes a weighted version of the area under the Precision and Recall curve.
