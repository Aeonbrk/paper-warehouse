# Sample-Efficient Kernel Mean Estimator with Marginalized Corrupted Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Estimating the kernel mean in a reproducing kernel Hilbert space is central to many kernel-based learning algorithms.
- Proposes to corrupt data examples with noise from known distributions and present a new kernel mean estimator, called the marginalized kernel mean estimator, which estimates kernel mean under the corrupted distributions.
