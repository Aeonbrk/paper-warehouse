# CLARE: A Semi-supervised Community Detection Algorithm

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Community detection refers to the task of discovering closely related subgraphs to understand the networks.
- It aims to seek other similar communities in the network with few labeled communities as training data.
- This limits its applicability in real-world networks, e.g., distinguishing fraud groups from normal ones in transaction networks.
