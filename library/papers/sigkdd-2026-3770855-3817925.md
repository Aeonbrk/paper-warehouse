# On the Push-Based Asynchronous Federated Learning: A Bias-Correction Aggregation Approach

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Asynchronous decentralized federated learning (ADFL) eliminates central coordination and global synchronization, making it attractive for large-scale and heterogeneous systems.
- Proposes PushCen-ADFL, a communication-efficient ADFL framework that enables stable training under asymmetric communication and delayed client participation.
- Experiments on vision datasets demonstrate that PushCen-ADFL improves accuracy under data heterogeneity by up to 6% while reducing per-push communication cost by more than 80%, achieving a favorable accuracy-communication trade-off.
