# Rethinking Learner Modeling: A Feedback-Centric Cognitive Disentanglement Perspective

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the rise of web-based technologies, online tutoring platforms have emerged to provide personalized learning services by modeling learners' engagement behaviors, improving both convenience and efficiency in academic progress.
- Proposes DISCD, a feedback-centric DIS entangled Cognitive Diagnosis framework for enhancing and interpretable learner modeling.
- Most existing studies fail to explicitly disentangling the multiple interdependent factors that influence learner's response feedback during the problem-solving process, both in web-based environments and real-world contexts.
