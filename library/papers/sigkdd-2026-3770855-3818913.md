# Machine Learning for Depression Screening and Intervention: an Original Circadian Rhythm Score-based Methodology

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Depression screening from large-scale behavioral data is challenged by fragmented circadian indicators, limited interpretability, and the lack of intervention-oriented analysis.
- Develops an interpretable depression screening framework based on gradient-boosted trees and SHAP analysis, revealing nonlinear and saturation-like associations between circadian rhythm and depression risk.
- Experiments on the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study (CHARLS, n=15,233), demonstrate robust screening performance (ROC-AUC=0.825) and identify actionable behavioral thresholds, including a minimum exercise dose of approximately 300 MET-min/week and an optimal restorative.
