# Perturbed Public Voices (P^2V): A Dataset for Robust Audio Deepfake Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Current audio deepfake detectors cannot be trusted.
- Introduces Perturbed Public Voices (P2V), an IRB-approved dataset capturing three critical aspects of malicious deepfakes: (1) identity-consistent transcripts via LLMs, (2) environmental and adversarial noise, and (3) voice cloning (2020–2025).
- Experiments reveal vulnerabilities of 22 recent audio deepfake detectors: models trained on current datasets lose 43% performance when tested on P2V, with performance measured as the mean of F1 score on deepfake audio, AUC.
