# Learning Hierarchical Knowledge in Text-Rich Networks with Taxonomy-Informed Representation Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Hierarchical knowledge structures are ubiquitous across real-world domains and play a vital role in organizing information from coarse to fine semantic levels.
- Proposes TIER (Hierarchical Taxonomy-Informed Representation Learning on Text-Rich Networks), which first constructs an implicit hierarchical taxonomy and then integrates it into the learned node representations.
- By learning representations that respect both fine-grained and coarse-grained semantics, TIER enables more interpretable and structured modeling of real-world TRNs.
