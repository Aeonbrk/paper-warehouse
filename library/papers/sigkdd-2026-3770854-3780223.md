# HAL: Accurate, Private, and Efficient Sample Alignment for Multimodal Federated Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Vertical multimodal federated learning (VMFL) enables multiple clients holding data from different modalities to conduct collaboratively model training.
- Proposes hashing-based alignment (HAL), a new VMFL framework that works without pre-aligned samples.
- Experimental results on two real-world datasets show that compared with five (SOTA) baselines, HAL improves the cross-modal retrieval accuracy by over 63%, while also achieving up to 154× speedup.
