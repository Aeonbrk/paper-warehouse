# SBFRec: Semantic-Behavioral Fusion with Trajectory Smoothing for Generative Sequential Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Generative diffusion/flow-based models have recently shown strong potential for sequential recommendation, but their performance can be brittle when conditional signals from user histories are weak and semantic augmentation or routing is unreliable.
- Proposes SBFRec, a Semantic-Behavioral Flow Matching framework for generative sequential recommendation.
- Experiments on four benchmark datasets show that SBFRec achieves an average 6.52% improvement over methods on Top-K ranking metrics.
