# Fair and Interpretable Models for Survival Analysis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Survival analysis aims to predict the risk of an event, such as death due to cancer, in the presence of censoring.
- Introduces fairness definitions based on survival functions and censoring.
- Experiments on three real-world survival datasets demonstrate that our proposed fair survival models show improvement over existing survival techniques in terms of accuracy and fairness measures.
