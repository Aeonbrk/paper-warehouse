# Few-shot Low-resource Knowledge Graph Completion with Multi-view Task Representation Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Despite their capacity to convey knowledge, most existing knowledge graphs (KGs) are created for specific domains using low-resource data sources, especially those in non-global languages, and thus unavoidably suffer from the incompleteness problem.
- Proposes a few-shot learning framework empowered by multi-view task representation generation.
- Experiments conducted on several real-world low-resource KGs validate the effectiveness of our proposed method.
