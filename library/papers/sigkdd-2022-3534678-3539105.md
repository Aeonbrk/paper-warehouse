# Task-optimized User Clustering based on Mobile App Usage for Cold-start Recommendations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Reports our recent practice of recommending articles to cold-start users at Tencent.
- Proposes a tailored Dual Alignment User Clustering (DAUC) model, which applies a sample-wise contrastive alignment to eliminate the gap between active users' mobile app usage and article reading behavior, and a distribution-wise adversarial alignment.
- High-dimensional embeddings are cumbersome for online usage, thus limiting the adoption.
