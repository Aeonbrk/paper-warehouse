# Continual-GraphLLM: Dynamic Graph Large Language Model with Invariance Regularized Adaptive Multi-Scale Experts

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Dynamic text-attributed graphs (DyTAGs) exhibit coupled textual and structural dynamics, and existing mainstream approaches for DyTAGs extend conventional large language models (LLMs) to capture both dynamics, thereby giving rise to dynamic graph LLMs.
- Proposes a Continual Learning Dynamic Graph LLM framework (Continual-GraphLLM) to continually adapt to incoming patterns by routing them to experts specialized in similar past patterns.
- Experiments on real-world DyTAGs report higher performance for our method over competitive baselines, highlighting its effectiveness in adapting to emerging DyTAG patterns.
