# SigFJProp: Lightweight and Scalable Signed Graph Learning via Opinion Dynamics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Learning on signed graphs poses unique challenges due to the presence of both positive and negative links, which represent complex social dynamics such as trust and conflict.
- Revisit message passing from the perspective of opinion dynamics, and propose SigFJProp, a and lightweight SGNN framework inspired by the Friedkin–Johnsen model.
- Experimental results on seven benchmark datasets, including one with over 750K nodes and 10M edges, demonstrate that SigFJProp achieves competitive or performance compared to methods, while reducing computational overhead.
