# Multi-Agent Debate based Concept Augmentation for Enhanced Cognitive Diagnosis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Cognitive Diagnosis (CD) models are constrained by the data quality of students' response logs.
- Proposes Multi-Agent based Concept Augmentation for Cognitive Diagnosis (MACA-CD), a approach that enhances CD by generating and fusing reliable concept descriptions and relations based solely on concept names.
- Ensuring the reliability and accuracy of LLM-generated annotations remains a challenge.
