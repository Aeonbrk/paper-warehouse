# A Framework for Multi-stage Bonus Allocation in Meal Delivery Platform

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online meal delivery is undergoing explosive growth, as this service is becoming increasingly popular.
- This framework consists of a semi-black-box acceptance probability model, a Lagrangian dual-based dynamic programming algorithm, and an online allocation algorithm.
- To verify the effectiveness and efficiency of our framework, both offline experiments on a real-world data set and online A/B tests on the Meituan meal delivery platform are conducted.
