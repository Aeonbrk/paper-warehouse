# Weather Knows What Will Occur: Urban Public Nuisance Events Prediction and Control with Meteorological Assistance

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Urban public nuisance events, like garbage exposure, illegal parking, facilities damage, and etc., impair the quality of life for city residents.
- Proposes Spatial-Temporal Two-Tower Transformer (ST-T3), which simultaneously considers spatial data and further improves the prediction accuracy.
- Employ meteorology predictions that are integrated in Hawkes processes to enhance the accuracy of predicting the category and timing of these nuisances.
