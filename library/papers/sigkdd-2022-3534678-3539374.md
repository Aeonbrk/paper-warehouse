# Model Degradation Hinders Deep Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have achieved great success in various graph mining tasks.
- Presents Adaptive Initial Residual (AIR), a plug-and-play module compatible with all kinds of GNN architectures, to alleviate the model degradation issue and the over-smoothing issue simultaneously.
- Experimental results on six real-world datasets demonstrate that GNNs equipped with AIR outperform most GNNs with shallow architectures owing to the benefits of both large DD_p$ and Dt.
