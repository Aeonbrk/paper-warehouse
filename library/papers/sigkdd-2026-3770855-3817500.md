# AlphaForgeBench: Benchmarking End-to-End Trading Strategy Design with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) has catalyzed the proliferation of diverse financial benchmarks, progressively evolving from static knowledge evaluation to increasingly sophisticated interactive trading simulations.
- Introduces AlphaForgeBench, a principled evaluation framework that reconceptualizes the role of LLMs from stochastic execution agents to quantitative researchers capable of systematic financial reasoning.
- These deficiencies collectively undermine the validity and trustworthiness of numerous existing online and offline financial trading benchmarks, rendering their evaluations unreliable, non-reproducible, and uninformative for meaningful model comparison.
