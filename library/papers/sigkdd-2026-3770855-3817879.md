# Can Fine-Tuning Erase Edits? On the Fragile Coexistence of Knowledge Editing and Fine-tuning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Knowledge editing (KE) offers a lightweight alternative to retraining for updating large language models (LLMs).
- Despite their widespread adoption, these two post-training interventions have been studied in isolation, leaving open a crucial question: if fine-tune an edited model, do the edits survive?
- AlphaEdit exhibits the greatest decay on the zsRE benchmark when applied to GPT-J, where 25.27% of previously successful edits become unsuccessful after fine-tuning.
