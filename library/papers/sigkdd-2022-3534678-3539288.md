# Source Localization of Graph Diffusion via Variational Autoencoders for Graph Inverse Problems

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph diffusion problems such as the propagation of rumors, computer viruses, or smart grid failures are ubiquitous and societal.
- Proposes a probabilistic model that leverages the forward diffusion estimation model along with deep generative models to approximate the diffusion source distribution for quantifying the uncertainty.
- Experiments are conducted on $7$ real-world datasets to report higher performance for SL-VAE in reconstructing the diffusion sources by excelling the state-of-the-arts on average 20% in AUC score.
