# Certified Defense on the Fairness of Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have emerged as a prominent graph learning model in various graph-based tasks over the years.
- Proposes a principled framework named ELEGANT and present a detailed theoretical certification analysis for the fairness of GNNs.
- Verify the satisfactory effectiveness of ELEGANT in practice through experiments on real-world datasets across different backbones of GNNs and parameter settings.
