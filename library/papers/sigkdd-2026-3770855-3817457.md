# MM-BRIGHT: A Multi-Task Multimodal Benchmark for Reasoning-Intensive Retrieval

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Existing retrieval benchmarks primarily consist of text-based queries where keyword or semantic matching is usually sufficient.
- Introduces MM-BRIGHT, the first multimodal benchmark for reasoning-intensive retrieval.
- Our dataset consists of 2,803 real-world queries spanning 29 diverse technical domains, with four tasks of increasing complexity: text-to-text, multimodal-to-text, multimodal-to-image, and multimodal-to-multimodal retrieval.
