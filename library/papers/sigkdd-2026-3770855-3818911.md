# AnyPPG: An ECG-Guided PPG Foundation Model Trained on Over 100,000 Hours of Recordings for Holistic Health Profiling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Photoplethysmography (PPG) is widely used as a non-invasive and accessible modality for continuous health monitoring.
- Presents AnyPPG, a foundation model-based framework designed to reveal the broader health-profiling potential of PPG.
- More importantly, in the phenome-wide analysis, AnyPPG exhibits meaningful discriminative capability (AUC ≥ 0.70) for 307 phenotypes across 16 distinct phecode chapters, including 230 non-circulatory conditions such as dementia, chronic kidney disease, hyperkalemia.
