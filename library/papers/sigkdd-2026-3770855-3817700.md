# AGMark: Attention-Guided Dynamic Watermarking for Large Vision-Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Watermarking has emerged as a solution for content traceability and intellectual property protection in large vision language models (LVLMs).
- This design fails to account for dynamic changes in visual dependence during generation and may introduce low-quality tokens in the long tail.
- Our framework maintains highly competitive detection performance (at least 99.36% AUC) and robust attack resilience (at least 88.61% AUC) without sacrificing inference efficiency, taking a step toward reliability-preserving multimodal watermarking.
