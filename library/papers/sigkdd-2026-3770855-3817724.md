# Beyond Semantic Understanding: Preserving Collaborative Frequency Components in LLM-based Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recommender systems in concert with Large Language Models (LLMs) present avenues for generating semantically-informed recommendations.
- Introduces FreLLM4Rec, an approach designed to balance semantic and collaborative information from a spectral perspective.
- Experiments on four benchmark datasets demonstrate that FreLLM4Rec successfully mitigates collaborative signal attenuation and achieves competitive performance, with improvements of up to 8.00% in NDCG@10 over the best baseline.
