# Separating Graph Generative Models via Degree Distributions

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Analyzes the power and limitations of a family of graph generative models, including the original Stochastic Kronecker Graph (SKG) models, often used to ensure privacy of network data.
- The SKG model cannot always generate graphs with degree distribution that follows a power-law or log-normal distribution (Seshadhri, Pinar, and Kolda, J.
- As a result, variants of the SKG model have been proposed to generate graphs which approximately follow degree distributions, without any oscillations.
