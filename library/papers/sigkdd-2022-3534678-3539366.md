# Causal Attention for Interpretable and Generalizable Graph Classification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In graph classification, attention- and pooling-based graph neural networks (GNNs) prevail to extract the critical features from the input graph and support the prediction.
- Proposes the Causal Attention Learning (CAL) strategy, which discovers the causal patterns and mitigates the confounding effect of shortcuts.
- Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of CAL.
