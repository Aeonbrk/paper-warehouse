# ArnoldiGCL: Graph Contrastive Learning via Learnable Arnoldi-Based Guided Spectral Chebyshev Polynomial Filters

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Contrastive Learning (GCL) emerged as a paradigm in self-supervised graph representation learning.
- Proposes ArnoldiGCL, a algorithm that enables the application of complex spectral filters for Graph Contrastive Learning (GCL).
- Experiments on real-world datasets confirm that ArnoldiGCL outperforms GCL algorithms on both homophilic and heterophilic graphs, showcasing its robustness and versatility.
