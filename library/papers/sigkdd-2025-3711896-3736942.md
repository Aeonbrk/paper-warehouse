# ESCFD: Probabilistic Flow Diffusion Model for Accelerated High-Quality Single-Cell RNA-seq Data Synthesis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) technology has emerged as a tool for studying complex biological systems and diseases with high resolution.
- Proposes a Efficient Single-Cell RNA-seq Flow Diffusion (named ESCFD) method based on the Diffusion Model, capable of synthesizing large-scale, high-quality scRNA-seq samples and generating either ''holistic'' or specific cellular subpopulations within a single framework.
