# GDPTN: Econometrics-Informed Deep Generative Forecasting for Large-Scale Censored Panel Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- For many critical microeconomic problems, modeling large-scale censored panel data is of central importance.
- Proposes an econometrics-informed deep generative framework, termed the Generative Dynamic Panel Tobit Network (GDPTN), for large-scale censored panel data.
- Existing approaches typically rely on parametric specifications and strong distributional assumptions, which limits their applicability in high-dimensional, nonlinear, and heterogeneous real-world settings.
