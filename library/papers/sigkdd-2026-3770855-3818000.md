# Joint Global-Local Representations via Relation-Entity Pair Encoding for Hyper-Relational Knowledge Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Hyper-relational knowledge graphs (HKGs) extend knowledge graphs with qualifiers to represent complex n-ary facts.
- Proposes GLoRE (Global-Local Representations via Relation--Entity pair encoding), a joint global-local model that treats relation--entity pairs as the modeling primitive to preserve relation identity and within-fact roles.
