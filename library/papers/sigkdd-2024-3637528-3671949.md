# Relevance Meets Diversity: A User-Centric Framework for Knowledge Exploration Through Recommendations

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Providing recommendations that are both relevant and diverse is a key consideration of modern recommender systems.
- Proposes a probabilistic user-behavior model in which users keep interacting with the recommender system as long as they receive relevant suggestions, but they may stop if the relevance of the recommended items drops.
