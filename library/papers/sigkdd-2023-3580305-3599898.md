# Revisiting Personalized Federated Learning: Robustness Against Backdoor Attacks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies whether personalization could bring robustness benefits to backdoor attacks.
- Based on our findings, further proposes a lightweight defense method, Simple-Tuning, which empirically improves defense performance against backdoor attacks.
- Conduct the first study of backdoor attacks in the pFL framework, testing 4 widely used backdoor attacks against 6 pFL methods on benchmark datasets FEMNIST and CIFAR-10, a total of 600 experiments.
