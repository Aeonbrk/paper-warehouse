# Spectral Subspace Clustering for Attributed Graphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Subspace clustering seeks to identify subspaces that segment a set of n data points into k (k« n) groups.
- These works either demand computational overhead for constructing the nxn self-expressive matrix, or fail to incorporate graph topology and attribute data into the subspace clustering framework effectively, and thus, compromise result quality.
- Our experiments, comparing S2CAG and M-S2CAG against 17 competitors over 8 benchmark datasets, exhibit that our solutions outperform all baselines in terms of clustering quality measured against the ground truth while delivering high efficiency.
