# R^2MR: Review and Rewrite Modality for Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the explosive growth of online multimodal content, multimodal recommender systems(MRSs) have brought benefits to multimedia platforms.
- Proposes a framework R2MR: Review and Rewrite Modality for Recommendation to tackle this issue.
- Comprehensive experiments across three benchmark datasets reveal that R2MR substantially outperforms methods, achieving an average improvement of 9.20%.
