# Rapid Image Labeling via Neuro-Symbolic Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The success of Computer Vision (CV) relies heavily on manually annotated data.
- Proposes a neuro-symbolic approach called RAPID, which infers image labeling rules from a small amount of labeled data provided by domain experts and automatically labels unannotated data using the rules.
- RAPID achieves a labeling accuracy of 83.33% to 88.33% on four image labeling tasks with only 12 to 39 labeled samples.
