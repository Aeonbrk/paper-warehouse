# Decoupling and Damping: Structurally-Regularized Gradient Matching for Multimodal Graph Condensation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In multimodal graph learning, graph structures that integrate information from multiple sources, such as vision and text, can more comprehensively model complex entity relationships.
- Proposes Structural Regularized Gradient Matching (SR-GM), a condensation framework for multimodal graphs.
- Experiments on four multimodal graph datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of SR-GM, highlighting its performance and cross-architecture generalization capabilities in multimodal graph dataset condensation.
