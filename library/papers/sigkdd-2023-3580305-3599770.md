# AdSEE: Investigating the Impact of Image Style Editing on Advertisement Attractiveness

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online advertisements are important elements in e-commerce sites, social media platforms, and search engines.
- Proposes Advertisement Style Editing and Attractiveness Enhancement (AdSEE), which explores whether semantic editing to ads images can affect or alter the popularity of online advertisements.
- Through a large collected dataset named QQ-AD, containing 20,527 online ads, perform offline tests to study how different semantic directions and their edit coefficients may impact click rates.
