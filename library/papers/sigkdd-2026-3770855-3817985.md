# ReCATS: Replay-Free Continual Anomaly Detection for Non-Stationary Multivariate Time Series

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multivariate time series anomaly detection is critical in safety-critical domains such as industrial monitoring and financial systems.
- Proposes ReCATS, a replay-free framework for continual anomaly detection in non-stationary multivariate time series.
- Experiments on six real-world benchmark datasets demonstrate that ReCATS consistently outperforms methods in terms of detection accuracy, knowledge retention, and transferability, as evaluated by standard continual learning metrics, including Backward Transfer and Forward Transfer.
