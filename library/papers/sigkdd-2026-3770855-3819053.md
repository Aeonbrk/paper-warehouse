# ReLU: Refined Chunk Embeddings Learning for Ultra-long SNP Genomic Prediction in Crop Breeding

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Genomic prediction can accelerate crop breeding by predicting phenotypes from genome-wide SNP sequences.
- Presents ReLU, Refined Chunk Embeddings Learning for Ultra-long SNP modeling, a scalable framework that refines chunk embeddings progressively for genomic prediction in crop breeding.
- Experiments on two real-world datasets, Rice3K with 440K SNPs and Maize1404 with 1M SNPs, demonstrate that ReLU outperforms baselines across multiple phenotypes.
