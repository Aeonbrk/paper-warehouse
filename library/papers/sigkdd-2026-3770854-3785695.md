# GUI-Robust: A Comprehensive Dataset for Testing GUI Agent Robustness in Real-World Anomalies

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The development of high-quality datasets is crucial for benchmarking and advancing research in Graphical User Interface (GUI) agents.
- Introduces GUI-Robust, a dataset designed for comprehensive GUI agent evaluation, explicitly incorporating seven common types of anomalies observed in everyday GUI interactions.
- Despite their importance, existing datasets are often constructed under idealized conditions, overlooking the diverse anomalies frequently encountered in real-world deployments.
