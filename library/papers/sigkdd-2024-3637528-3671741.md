# Balanced Confidence Calibration for Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Delves into the confidence calibration in prediction when using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), which has emerged as a notable challenge in the field.
- Introduces a framework called Balanced Calibrated Graph Neural Network (BCGNN), specifically designed to establish a balanced calibration between over-confidence and under-confidence in GNNs' prediction.
- Despite their capabilities in processing graph-structured data, GNNs are prone to exhibit lower confidence in their predictions than what the actual accuracy warrants.
