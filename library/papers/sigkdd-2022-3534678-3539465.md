# A Generalized Backward Compatibility Metric

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Retraining a classifier with new data is inseparable from ML/AI applications, but most of the existing ML methods do not take into account the backward compatibility of predictions.
- Several metrics and learning methods for backward compatibility have been actively studied recently.
- Formulates a learning objective based on the GBC metric and derive the estimation error bound, and the result is applied to one of the existing methods.
