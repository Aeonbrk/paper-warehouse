# RGVisNet: A Hybrid Retrieval-Generation Neural Framework Towards Automatic Data Visualization Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent years have witnessed the burgeoning of data visualization (DV) systems in both the research and the industrial communities since they provide vivid and tools to convey the insights behind the massive data.
- To the best of our knowledge, RGVisNet is the first framework that seamlessly integrates the retrieval- with the generative-based approach for the text-to-vis task.
- Experimental evaluation on the public NVBench dataset validates that RGVisNet can outperform existing generative text-to-vis models such as ncNet, by up to 74.28% relative improvement in terms of overall accuracy.
