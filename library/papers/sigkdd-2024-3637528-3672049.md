# Path-Specific Causal Reasoning for Fairness-aware Cognitive Diagnosis

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Cognitive Diagnosis (CD), which leverages students and exercise data to predict students' proficiency levels on different knowledge concepts, is one of fundamental components in Intelligent Education.
- Employ causal reasoning and design a Path-Specific Causal Reasoning Framework (PSCRF) to achieve this goal.
- Experiments over real-world datasets (e.g., PISA dataset) demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed PSCRF.
