# An Automatic Graph Construction Framework based on Large Language Models for Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph neural networks (GNNs) have emerged as methods to learn from graph-structured data for recommendation.
- Most existing GNN-based recommendation methods focus on the optimization of model structures and learning strategies based on pre-defined graphs, neglecting the importance of the graph construction stage.
- Experiments on three real-world datasets demonstrate the efficacy and efficiency of AutoGraph compared to existing baseline methods.
