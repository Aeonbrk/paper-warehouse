# FedCP: Separating Feature Information for Personalized Federated Learning via Conditional Policy

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Personalized federated learning (pFL) has attracted increasing attention in privacy protection, collaborative learning, and tackling statistical heterogeneity among clients, e.g., hospitals, mobile smartphones, etc.
- Proposes the Federated Conditional Policy (FedCP) method, which generates a conditional policy for each sample to separate the global information and personalized information in its features and then processes them by a global head.
