# Learning Supplementary NLP Features for CTR Prediction in Sponsored Search

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In sponsored search engines, pre-trained language models have shown performance improvements on Click-Through-Rate (CTR) prediction.
- Thereafter the output of these fine-tuned models, i.e., the final score or intermediate embedding generated by language model, is used as a new Natural Language Processing (NLP) feature into CTR prediction baseline.
- A widely used approach for utilizing pre-trained language models in CTR prediction consists of fine-tuning the language models with click labels and early stopping on peak value of the obtained Area Under the ROC.
