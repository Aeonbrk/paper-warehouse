# From Swath to Full-Disc: Advancing Precipitation Retrieval with Multimodal Knowledge Expansion

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurate near-real-time precipitation retrieval has been enhanced by satellite-based technologies.
- Introduces Multimodal Knowledge Expansion, a two-stage pipeline with the proposed PRE-Net model.
- Infrared-based algorithms have low accuracy due to weak relations with surface precipitation, whereas passive microwave and radar-based methods are more accurate but limited in range.
