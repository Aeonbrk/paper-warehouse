# GREPO: A Benchmark for Graph Neural Networks on Repository-Level Bug Localization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Repository-level bug localization—the task of identifying where code must be modified to fix a bug—is a critical software engineering challenge.
- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) offer a alternative due to their ability to model complex, repository-wide dependencies; however, their application has been hindered by the lack of a dedicated benchmark.
- As a result, various retrieval methods are commonly used, including keyword matching, text similarity, and simple graph-based heuristics such as Breadth-First Search.
