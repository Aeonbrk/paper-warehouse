# FoodGPT: Reinforcement Post-Training of Large Language Models in the Food Delivery Domain

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- On-demand Food Delivery (OFD) platforms, such as Ele.me and Meituan, have transformed daily life by offering convenient ordering services.
- Proposes an efficient reinforcement post-training framework that combines Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with an additional rule-based reward signal.
- For instance, it achieved a 0.57% increase in click-through rate and a 0.32% increase in user visit-to-purchase rate in the ITG online experiment.
