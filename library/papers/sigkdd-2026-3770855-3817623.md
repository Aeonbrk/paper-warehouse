# Neural Stick-Breaking for Cascade Forensics

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Characterising transmission laws in epidemic cascades is essential for outbreak forensics, yet neural models with finite support fail to capture heavy-tailed phenomena like superspreading.
- Proposes the Neural Stick-Breaking (NSB) process, a neuro-symbolic-spectral duality that learns infinite-support offspring distributions via a recurrent mapping with theoretical guarantees.
- Empirically, the NSB and its gated variants outperform neural baselines and classical estimators on the '' outbreaktrees '' (SARS/MERS-Cov) dataset for cascade modelling.
