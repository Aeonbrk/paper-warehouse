# Non-stationary Time-aware Kernelized Attention for Temporal Event Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Modeling sequential data is essential to many applications such as natural language processing, recommendation systems, time series predictions, anomaly detection, etc.
- Presents a non-stationary time-aware kernelized attention approach for input sequences of neural networks.
- Though prevalent and in many applications, conventional approaches such as RNNs and Transformers, struggle with handling the non-stationary characteristics (i.e., such temporal-correlation among events would change over time).
