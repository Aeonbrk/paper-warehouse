# Decoupling Spatio-Temporal Prediction: When Lightweight Large Models Meet Adaptive Hypergraphs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Spatio-temporal prediction is a task with broad applications in traffic management, climate monitoring, energy scheduling, etc.
- Proposes STH-SepNet (Spatio-Temporal Hypergraph Separation Networks), a framework that decouples temporal and spatial modeling to enhance both efficiency and precision.
- Existing methodologies often struggle to balance model expressiveness and computational efficiency, especially when scaling to large real-world datasets.
