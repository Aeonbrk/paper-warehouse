# Discovering Dynamic Causal Space for DAG Structure Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Discovering causal structure from purely observational data (i.e., causal discovery), aiming to identify causal relationships among variables, is a fundamental task in machine learning.The recent invention of differentiable score-based DAG learners is a crucial.
- Proposes a dynamic csusal space for DAG structure learning, coined CASPER, that integrates the graph structure into the score function as a new measure in the causal space to faithfully reflect the causal distance.
- Experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets clearly report comparisons with our CASPER over the causal discovery methods in terms of accuracy and robustness.
