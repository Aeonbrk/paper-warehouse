# Going Where, by Whom, and at What Time: Next Location Prediction Considering User Preference and Temporal Regularity

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Next location prediction is a crucial task in human mobility modeling, and is for many downstream applications like location-based recommendation and transportation planning.
- Proposes a Multi-Context aware Location Prediction model (MCLP) to predict next locations for individuals, where it explicitly models user preference and the next arrival time as context.
- Experimental results on two real-world mobility datasets show that our proposed MCLP outperforms baseline methods.
