# TimeRadar: A Domain-Rotatable Foundation Model for Time Series Anomaly Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Current time series foundation models (TSFMs) primarily focus on learning prevalent and regular patterns within a predefined time or frequency domain to enable supervised downstream tasks (\eg, forecasting).
- To allow TimeRadar to model local abnormality that is not captured by the global data reconstruction, further introduce a Contextual Deviation Learning (CDL) component to model the local deviation of the input relative to.
- Experiments on eight popular TSAD benchmarks demonstrate that TimeRadar consistently outperforms a variety of conventional and TSFM-based competing methods, delivering average gains of 10.5% and 29.4% in AUC-R and AUC-P, respectively.
