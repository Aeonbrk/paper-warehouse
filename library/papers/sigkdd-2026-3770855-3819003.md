# ReplicatorBench: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Replicability in Social and Behavioral Sciences

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The literature has witnessed an emerging interest in developing and evaluating AI agents for automated assessment of research claims in scientific papers.
- Introduces ReplicatorBench, an end-to-end benchmark, including human-verified replicable and non-replicable research claims in social and behavioral sciences, for evaluating AI agents in research replication across three stages: (1) extraction of relevant information and retrieval.
