# EBES: Easy Benchmarking for Event Sequences

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Event Sequences (EvS ) refer to sequential data characterized by irregular sampling intervals and a mix of categorical and numerical features.
- Presents EBES, a comprehensive benchmark for EvS classification with sequence-level targets.
- Additionally, it includes the largest collection of EvS datasets, featuring 10 curated datasets, including a synthetic dataset and real-world data with the largest publicly available banking dataset.
