# ThermEval: A Structured Benchmark for Evaluation of Vision-Language Models on Thermal Imagery

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Vision language models (VLMs) achieve strong performance on RGB imagery, but their ability to generalize to thermal images remains poorly understood.
- Introduces ThermEval-B, a structured benchmark of more than 70,000 thermal visual question answering (VQA) pairs designed to assess the foundational primitives required for thermal vision language understanding.
