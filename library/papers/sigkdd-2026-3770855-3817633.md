# Road-Constrained Vehicle Trajectory Recovery from Traffic Video Using Spatio-Temporal Voxel Representation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Road-constrained trajectory recovery from traffic surveillance videos has become a critical component in applications ranging from intelligent transportation to urban planning.
- Proposes a trajectory recovery framework based on spatio-temporal voxel representation.
- Finally, experiments across multiple datasets demonstrate that our method achieves trajectory recovery accuracy, with an average F1-score improvement of 14.7%.
