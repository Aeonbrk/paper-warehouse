# When Deepfake Detection Meets Graph Neural Network: a Unified and Lightweight Framework

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The proliferation of generative video models has made detecting AI-generated and manipulated videos an urgent challenge.
- Introduces SSTGNN, a lightweight Spatial-Spectral-Temporal Graph Neural Network framework that represents videos as structured graphs, enabling joint reasoning over spatial inconsistencies, temporal artifacts, and spectral distortions.
- Experiments on diverse benchmark datasets demonstrate that SSTGNN not only achieves performance in both in-domain and cross-domain settings, but also offers strong efficiency and resource allocation.
