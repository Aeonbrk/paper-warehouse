# How Transitive Are Real-World Group Interactions? - Measurement and Reproduction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Many real-world interactions (e.g., researcher collaborations and email communication) occur among multiple entities.
- Proposes a principled hypergraph transitivity measure HyperTrans, which satisfies all the proposed axioms, with a fast computation algorithm Fast-HyperTrans.
- To the best of our knowledge, there has been no examination regarding the transitivity of real-world group interactions.
