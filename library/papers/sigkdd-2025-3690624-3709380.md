# Large-scale Human Mobility Data Regeneration for Open Urban Research

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large-scale human mobility data contains rich spatial and temporal information for urban sensing, crowd flow modeling, and urban planning.
- Particularly, first proposes a heatmap diffusion module to generate a probability heatmap that produces plausible trajectories at both the individual and city scales.
- Constructs a high-quality human mobility dataset by generating large-scale citizen trajectories based on massive cellular signaling data.
