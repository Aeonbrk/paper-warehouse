# Parameter-free Spikelet: Discovering Different Length and Warped Time Series Motifs using an Adaptive Time Series Representation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Over the last two decades, time series motif discovery has emerged as a useful primitive for many downstream analytical tasks, including clustering, classification, rule discovery, segmentation, and summarization.
- Proposes a method to discover "warped" motifs whose lengths may differ.
- Reports that our method is scalable enough for real-world datasets and enables us to find variable-length and "warped" motifs that would otherwise escape the attention of conventional algorithms.
