# Optimizing Long-Term Efficiency and Fairness in Ride-Hailing via Joint Order Dispatching and Driver Repositioning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The ride-hailing service offered by mobility-on-demand platforms, such as Uber and Didi Chuxing, has greatly facilitated people's traveling and commuting, and become increasingly popular in recent years.
- Proposes a multi-agent reinforcement learning framework, referred to as JDRL, to help drivers make distributed order selection and repositioning decisions.
- Experiments are conducted with three public real-world ride-hailing order datasets, including over 2 million orders in Haikou, China, over 5 million orders in Chengdu, China.
