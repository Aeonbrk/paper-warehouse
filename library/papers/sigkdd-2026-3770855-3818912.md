# MetaRanker: Human-in-the-loop Active Ranking for Metalens Image Quality

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Image quality in modern imaging systems emerges from the coupled effects of the sensor, optics, and computational reconstruction.
- Introduces MetaRanker, a human-in-the-loop active ranking framework that formalizes metalens image quality in terms of semantic interpretability, defined as the degree to which humans can reliably recognize objects and structures in the presence of.
- Across real-world and synthetic metalens datasets with distinct degradation profiles, MetaRanker produces rankings that align most closely with human assessments.
