# When Box Meets Graph Neural Network in Tag-aware Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Last year has witnessed the re-flourishment of tag-aware recommender systems supported by the LLM-enriched tags.
- Proposes a framework, called BoxGNN, to perform message aggregation via combinations of logical operations, thereby incorporating high-order signals.
- Experiments on two publicly available datasets and one LLM-enhanced e-commerce dataset have validated the superiority of BoxGNN compared with various baselines.
