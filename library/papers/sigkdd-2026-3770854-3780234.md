# Multi-View Urban Region Embedding via Commonality-Specificity Disentanglement

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multi-view region embedding has become an important technique to be pursued due to the increasing availability of diverse urban sensing data.
- Proposes ComSRE, a region embedding framework that explicitly disentangles shared and distinctive components in multi-view region representations.
- Experiments on three real-world urban datasets demonstrate that ComSRE consistently outperforms methods across multiple downstream tasks, achieving predictive accuracy and representation quality.
