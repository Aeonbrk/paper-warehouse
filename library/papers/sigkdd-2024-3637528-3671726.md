# Efficient Discovery of Time Series Motifs under both Length Differences and Warping

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Over the past two decades, time series motif discovery has become a crucial subroutine for many time series data mining tasks.
- Proposes a approach that allows us to find motifs under both length differences and warping.
- Empirical studies demonstrate that our method scales effectively across various real-world datasets and efficiently identifies DTW motif pairs of different lengths.
