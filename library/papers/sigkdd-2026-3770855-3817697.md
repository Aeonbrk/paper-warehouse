# Mag-Mamba: Modeling Coupled Spatio-temporal Asymmetry for POI Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Next Point-of-Interest (POI) recommendation is a critical task in location-based services, yet it faces the fundamental challenge of coupled spatiotemporal asymmetry inherent in urban mobility.
- Proposes Mag-Mamba, a framework whose core insight lies in modeling spatiotemporal asymmetry as phase-driven rotational dynamics in the complex domain.
- Experiments on three real-world datasets demonstrate that Mag-Mamba achieves performance improvements over baselines.
