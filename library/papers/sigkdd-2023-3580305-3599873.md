# MUSER: A MUlti-Step Evidence Retrieval Enhancement Framework for Fake News Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The ease of spreading false information online enables individuals with malicious intent to manipulate public opinion and destabilize social stability.
- Proposes a framework for fake news detection based on MUlti- Step Evidence Retrieval enhancement (MUSER).
- Conducted experiments on real-world datasets in different languages, and the results demonstrate that our proposed model outperforms baseline methods for detecting fake news by at least 3% in F1-Macro and 4% in F1-Micro.
