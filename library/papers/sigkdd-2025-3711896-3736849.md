# AtomR: Atomic Operator-Empowered Large Language Models for Heterogeneous Knowledge Reasoning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Despite the outstanding capabilities of large language models (LLMs), knowledge-intensive reasoning still remains a challenging task due to LLMs' limitations in compositional reasoning and the hallucination problem.
- Introduces AtomR, a framework for LLMs to conduct accurate heterogeneous knowledge reasoning at the atomic level.
- Introduce BlendQA, a challenging benchmark specially tailored for heterogeneous knowledge reasoning.
