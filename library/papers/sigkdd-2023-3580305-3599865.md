# MIDLG: Mutual Information based Dual Level GNN for Transaction Fraud Complaint Verification

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- "Transaction fraud" complaint verification, i.e., verifying whether a transaction corresponding to a complaint is fraudulent, is particularly critical to prevent economic loss.
- Proposes the mutual information based dual level graph neural network, namely MIDLG, which defines a complaint as a super-node consisting of involved individuals, and characterizes the individual over node-level and super-node-level.
- MIDLG achieves SOTA results through experiments in complaint verification on WeChat Finance, one online payment service serving more than 600 million users in China.
