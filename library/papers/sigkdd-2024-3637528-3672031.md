# Scalable Differentiable Causal Discovery in the Presence of Latent Confounders with Skeleton Posterior

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Differentiable causal discovery has made advancements in the learning of directed acyclic graphs.
- Proposes SPOT (Skeleton Posterior-guided OpTimization), a two-phase framework that harnesses skeleton posterior for differentiable causal discovery in the presence of latent confounders.
- Its application to real-world datasets remains restricted due to the ubiquity of latent confounders and the requirement to learn maximal ancestral graphs (MAGs).
