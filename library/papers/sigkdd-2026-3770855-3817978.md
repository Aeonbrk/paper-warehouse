# CAAD: Causality-Aware Multivariate Time Series Anomaly Detection via Multi-Scale Alignment and Structural Causal Consistency

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The operational integrity of complex industrial systems relies on precise anomaly detection and diagnosis.
- Proposes a framework (CAAD) that reframes anomaly detection as the continuous verification of Granger causality consistency through exogenous variables.
- Experiments on real-world industrial datasets demonstrate that the CAAD achieves high-precision anomaly detection, outperforming most baselines.
