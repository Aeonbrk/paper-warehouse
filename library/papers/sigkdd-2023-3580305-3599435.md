# MixupExplainer: Generalizing Explanations for Graph Neural Networks with Data Augmentation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have received increasing attention due to their ability to learn from graph-structured data.
- Proposes a graph mixup method, MixupExplainer, with a theoretical guarantee to resolve the distribution shifting issue.
- Conduct experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets to validate the effectiveness of our proposed mixup approach over existing approaches.
