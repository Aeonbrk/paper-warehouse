# Revisiting Self-Attention for Cross-Domain Sequential Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Sequential recommendation is a popular paradigm in modern recommender systems.
- Introduces a Pareto-optimal self-attention and formulate the cross-domain learning as a multi-objective problem, where optimize the recommendation task while dynamically minimizing the cross-domain attention scores.
- AutoCDSR on average improves Recall@10 for SASRec and Bert4Rec by 9.8% and 16.0% and NDCG@10 by 12.0% and 16.7%, respectively.
