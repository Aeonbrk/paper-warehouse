# Unleashing The Power of Pre-Trained Language Models for Irregularly Sampled Time Series

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Pre-trained Language Models (PLMs), such as ChatGPT, have advanced the field of natural language processing.
- Proposes a unified PLM-based framework, named ISTS-PLM, to address diverse ISTS analytical tasks.
- Finally, experiments on a comprehensive benchmark demonstrate that the ISTS-PLM, utilizing a structured and series-based representation for ISTS, consistently achieves performance across various analytical tasks, such as classification, interpolation, extrapolation, few-shot and zero-shot learning.
