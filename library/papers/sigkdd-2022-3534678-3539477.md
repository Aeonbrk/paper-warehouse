# ProActive: Self-Attentive Temporal Point Process Flows for Activity Sequences

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Any human activity can be represented as a temporal sequence of actions performed to achieve a certain goal.
- Presents ProActive, a neural marked temporal point process (MTPP) framework for modeling the continuous-time distribution of actions in an activity sequence while simultaneously addressing three high-impact problems - next action prediction, sequence-goal prediction.
- Experiments on sequences derived from three activity recognition datasets show the accuracy boost of ProActive over the in terms of action and goal prediction, and the first-ever application of end-to-end action sequence generation.
