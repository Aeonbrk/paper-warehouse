# Generalized Matrix Local Low Rank Representation by Random Projection and Submatrix Propagation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Matrix low rank approximation is an method to reduce or eliminate the statistical redundancy of its components.
- Develops a matrix computational framework called RPSP (Random Probing based submatrix Propagation) that provides an solution for the general matrix local low rank representation problem.
- On real-world datasets, RPSP also demonstrates its effectiveness in identifying interpretable local low rank matrices.
