# When to Trust Whom: A Context-Aware Graph Routing Mechanism for Information Diffusion Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Information diffusion prediction forecasts future participants from an observed cascade prefix, enabling proactive intervention in applications such as viral marketing and misinformation mitigation.
- Proposes CARD, a context-aware routing framework that replaces static fusion with step-wise evidence arbitration.
- Experiments on four real-world datasets show that CARD achieves accuracy and stronger robustness.
