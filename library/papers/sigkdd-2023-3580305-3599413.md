# Less is More: SlimG for Accurate, Robust, and Interpretable Graph Mining

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Studies semi-supervised node classification across graph structures, including settings with noisy features.
- Proposes SlimG, a sparse linear model designed for accuracy, robustness, scalable training, and interpretability across graph scenarios.
- Reports comparisons on 13 real-world datasets, faster training on million-scale graphs, and ablation studies of graph neural network design choices.
