# Multi-Agent Graph Convolutional Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic Electric Vehicle Charging Pricing

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Electric Vehicles (EVs) have been emerging as a low-carbon transport target.
- Proposes a Multi-Agent Graph Convolutional Reinforcement Learning (MAGC) framework to enable CSOs to achieve more use of these stations by providing dynamic pricing for each of the continuously arising charging requests with optimizing multiple.
- Experiments on two real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of MAGC and empirically show that the overall use of stations can be improved if all the charging stations in a charging market embrace our dynamic.
