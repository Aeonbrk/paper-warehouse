# Efficient Continuous Space Policy Optimization for High-frequency Trading

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- High-frequency trading is an extraordinarily intricate financial task, which is normally treated as a near real-time sequential decision problem.
- Compared with the traditional two-phase approach, forecasting equity's trend and then weighting them by combinatorial optimization, deep reinforcement learning (DRL) methods have shown advances in reward chasing with optimal policies.
- Reports that our proposed DRPO outperforms benchmark methods.
