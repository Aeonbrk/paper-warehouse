# DisCo: Towards Harmonious Disentanglement and Collaboration between Tabular and Semantic Space for Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recommender systems play important roles in various applications such as e-commerce, social media, etc.
- Proposes 1) a dual-side attentive network to capture the intra-domain patterns and the inter-domain patterns, 2) a sufficiency constraint to preserve the task-relevant information of each representation space and filter out the noise.
- Experiment results report comparisons with our method against different models and the compatibility of DisCo over different backbones.
