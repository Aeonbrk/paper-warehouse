# TSMixer: Lightweight MLP-Mixer Model for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Transformers have gained popularity in time series forecasting for their ability to capture long-sequence interactions.
- Proposes TSMixer, a lightweight neural architecture exclusively composed of multi-layer perceptron (MLP) modules.
- Reports the challenges involved in adapting Vision MLP-Mixer for time series and introduce empirically validated components to enhance accuracy.
