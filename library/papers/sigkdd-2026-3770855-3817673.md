# EvoFEND: Dual Memory-Driven Self-Evolving Fake News Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Real-world fake news is inherently dynamic: evidence within an event accumulates and conflicts over time, while deceptive tactics shift across events.
- Proposes active, non-parametric evolution for fake news detection: instead of relying on repeated parametric updates (i.e., retraining) to handle changes, the model evolves by updating external memories as evidence and environments shift.
- To enable reliable evaluation under streaming conditions, construct XNews-25, a benchmark that supports evaluation with recent streaming data.
