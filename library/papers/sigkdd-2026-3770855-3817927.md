# GS-Fuse: Granger-Supervised Gated Fusion and Multi-Granularity Alignment for Event-Driven Financial Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Proposes GS-Fuse, a multimodal event-based forecasting framework that employs (i) a Granger-supervised, causal-aware gated fusion module.
- Experiments on real-world financial datasets show that GS-Fuse consistently outperforms time-series and multimodal baselines across multiple assets and forecasting horizons.
