# CoRAL: Collaborative Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models Improve Long-tail Recommendation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The long-tail recommendation is a challenging task for traditional recommender systems, due to data sparsity and data imbalance issues.
- Proposes to find the optimal interaction set through a sequential decision-making process and develop a retrieval policy learned through a reinforcement learning (RL) framework, CoRAL.
- Since most LLM-based systems rely on items' semantic meaning as the sole evidence for reasoning, the collaborative information of user-item interactions is neglected.
