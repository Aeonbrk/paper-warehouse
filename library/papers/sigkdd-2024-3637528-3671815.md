## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Graph partitioning aims to divide a graph into k disjoint subsets while optimizing a specific partitioning objective.
- First, by leveraging a reinforcement learning-based framework over node representations derived from a graph neural network and positional features, NeuroCut can accommodate any optimization objective, even those with non-differentiable functions.
