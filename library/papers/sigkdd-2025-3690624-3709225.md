# From Missteps to Mastery: Enhancing Low-Resource Dense Retrieval through Adaptive Query Generation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Document retrieval, designed to recall query-relevant documents from expansive collections, is essential for information-seeking tasks, such as web search and open-domain question-answering.
- Introduces iGFT, a framework aimed at enhancing low-resource dense retrieval by integrating a three-phase process --- Generation, Filtering, and Tuning --- coupled with an iterative optimization strategy.
- Finally, experiments conducted on a series of publicly available retrieval benchmark datasets have demonstrated the effectiveness of the proposed iGFT.
