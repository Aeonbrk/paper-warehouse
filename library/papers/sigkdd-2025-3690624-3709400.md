# Efficient Multi-Expert Tabular Language Model for Banking

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Pre-training large Tabular Language Models (TaLMs) on tabular data has shown effectiveness for table understanding tasks.
- Presents an efficient multi-expert TaLM architecture and training method tailored for multi-domain databases and modest infrastructure.
- Compared with previous baselines, our model achieves +29.3% in precision@0.6% on risk assessment and +16.5% in accuracy on information prediction, showing great effectiveness and profitability of our model.
