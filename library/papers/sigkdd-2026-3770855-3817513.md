# From Outcomes to Mechanisms: A Benchmark for Diagnosing LLM's Mechanism Hallucination on Data Inference Externalities

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as decision-making agents in environments with strategic interaction and tightly coupled causal mechanisms.
- Proposes a mechanism-understanding oriented benchmarking framework for LLM-as-agent decision competence in computable economic mechanisms.
- Across 12 frontier models from the GPT, DeepSeek, and Qwen families, find that terminal metrics can substantially overestimate mechanism understanding: several models attain near-zero welfare deviation at the benchmark point yet violate mechanism-implied comparative.
