# Avoiding Biases due to Similarity Assumptions in Node Embeddings

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Node embeddings are vectors, one per node, that capture a graph's structure.
- Our proposed embedding, called NEWS, makes no similarity assumptions, avoiding potential risks to privacy and fairness.
- These gains from avoiding assumptions do not affect accuracy, as show via comparisons against several existing methods on $21$ real-world networks.
