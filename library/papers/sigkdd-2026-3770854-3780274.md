# Non-overlapped Frequency based Episode Significance under Markov Null Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Frequent episode mining is a popular method for discovering temporal dependencies in symbolic time series data.
- Presents a method to characterize the distribution of non-overlapped occurrences of an episode and hence to assess its significance under a Markovian null hypothesis.
- This is the first such result.
