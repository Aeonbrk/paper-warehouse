# Fair Allocation Over Time, with Applications to Content Moderation

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In today's digital world, interaction with online platforms is ubiquitous, and thus content moderation is important for protecting users from content that do not comply with pre-established community guidelines.
- Introduces new extensions of the various fair allocation approaches for the single-time period setting, and show that many properties extend in essence, albeit with some modifications.
- Given the vast volume of content generated online daily, having an efficient content moderation system throughout every stage of planning is particularly important.
