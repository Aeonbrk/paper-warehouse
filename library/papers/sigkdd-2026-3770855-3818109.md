# A Geometric Information Bottleneck for Activation Steering

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Activation-based steering methods for large language models often induce broad, entangled changes in model behavior, inadvertently altering capabilities unrelated to the intended behavior, which limits their reliability for fine-grained behavioral control.
- Proposes a disentanglement-based intervention framework, termed IB-ACT, that identifies both where and how to intervene by exploiting the layer-wise geometry of representation spaces to isolate behavior-relevant information without disturbing other dimensions.
