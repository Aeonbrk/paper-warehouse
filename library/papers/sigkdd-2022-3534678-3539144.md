# Multi Armed Bandit vs. A/B Tests in E-commence - Confidence Interval and Hypothesis Test Power Perspectives

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- An emerging dilemma that faces practitioners in large scale online experimentation for e-commerce is whether to use Multi-Armed Bandit (MAB) algorithms for testing or traditional A/B testing (A/B).
- Proposes two new MAB algorithms that combine the strengths of traditional MAB and A/B together, with higher (or equal) test power and higher (or equal) expected rewards than A/B testing under certain common conditions.
- Last, evaluate and compare the performance among the classical MAB algorithms, our newly proposed MAB algorithms.
