# Prices Do Matter: Modeling Price Competitiveness for Online Hotel Industry

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Broad adoption of Online Travel Platforms (OTPs) has led to increasing interest in accurately predicting users' hotel purchase behavior, with price being a key influencer in user decision-making and receiving focus.
- Furthermore, a Hotel Price Competitiveness-aware Purchase Prediction Model (HP3M) is constructed by incorporating MHPC and demand dynamics into a multi-task learning framework, featuring three distinct submodules to encompass the tri-dimensional facets of MHPC.
- Being independent of specific user preferences, MHPC can be applied to and improve various downstream operations in the online hotel industry, such as hotel ranking and pricing, ultimately benefiting hoteliers, users, and OTPs.
