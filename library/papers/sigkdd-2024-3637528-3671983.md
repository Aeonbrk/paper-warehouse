# Influence Maximization via Graph Neural Bandits

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Considers a ubiquitous scenario in the study of Influence Maximization (IM), in which there is limited knowledge about the topology of the diffusion network.
- Proposes the framework IM-GNB (Influence Maximization with Graph Neural Bandits), where provide an estimate of the users' probabilities of being influenced by influencers (also known as diffusion seeds).
- Reports the effectiveness of IM-GNB compared with other baseline methods, improving the spread outcome of such diffusion campaigns, when the underlying network is unknown.
