# CityWeave: Weaving User Needs and World Constraints for Personalized and Reliable Mobility Planning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Urban door-to-door (D2D) mobility planning is a core task for AI-powered smart cities, requiring models to capture individual mobility behavior and generate optimized plans under real-world urban constraints such as network connectivity and service.
- Proposes CityWeave, a VLM-based framework for urban D2D mobility planning that integrates the Who--When--Where--How (3W1H) reasoning paradigm with a two-stage training scheme.
- A dataset of 180,000 real-world samples from 80,000 users is constructed to support training and evaluation.
