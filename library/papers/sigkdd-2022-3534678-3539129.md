# Company-as-Tribe: Company Financial Risk Assessment on Tribe-Style Graph with Hierarchical Graph Neural Networks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Company financial risk is ubiquitous and early risk assessment for listed companies can avoid considerable losses.
- Proposes a Hierarchical Graph Neural Network (TH-GNN) for Tribe-style graphs via two levels, with the first level to encode the structure pattern of the tribes with contrastive learning.
- Experiments on the real-world company dataset show that our method achieves improvements on financial risk assessment over previous competing methods.
