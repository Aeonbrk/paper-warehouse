# FedTT: A Federated Spatio-Temporal Learning Framework for Cross-City Traffic Knowledge Transfer

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Traffic prediction aims to forecast future traffic conditions from historical spatio-temporal data.
- Proposes FedTT, a federated spatio-temporal learning framework for cross-city traffic knowledge transfer.
- Experiments on four real-world traffic datasets demonstrate that FedTT consistently outperforms 18 baselines, achieving improved prediction accuracy while maintaining strong empirical resistance to attacks.
