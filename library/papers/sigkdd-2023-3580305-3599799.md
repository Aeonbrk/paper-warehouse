# Deep Landscape Forecasting in Multi-Slot Real-Time Bidding

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Real-Time Bidding (RTB) has shown success in display advertising and has been employed in other advertising scenarios, e.g., sponsored search advertising with multiple ad slots.
- Proposes a deep recurrent model to predict the distribution of the winning price as well as the winning probability for each position.
- Experiments on two public semi-synthetic datasets and one private industrial dataset demonstrate the effectiveness of our method.
