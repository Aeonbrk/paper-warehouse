# STEMO: Early Spatio-temporal Forecasting with Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accuracy and timeliness are indeed often conflicting goals in prediction tasks.
- Proposes an early spatio-temporal forecasting model based on Multi-Objective reinforcement learning that can either implement an optimal policy given a preference or infer the preference based on a small number of samples.
- Consequently, finding a balance between accuracy and timeliness is crucial.
