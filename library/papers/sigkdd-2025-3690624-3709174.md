# The $k$-Trine Cohesive Subgraph and Its Efficient Algorithms

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Introduces and study a cohesive subgraph model, named k-trine, to address the defects in the classical k-core and k-truss models.
- Our analysis shows that the k-trine is a more feasible model for capturing cohesive subgraphs by containing the strongly connected vertices.
- Experiments on real-world networks validate the effectiveness of the k-trine model and the efficiency of our algorithms.
