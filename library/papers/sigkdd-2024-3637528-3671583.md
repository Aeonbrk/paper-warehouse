# Know Your Needs Better: Towards Structured Understanding of Marketer Demands with Analogical Reasoning Augmented LLMs

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Explores a new way for user targeting, where non-expert marketers could select their target users solely given demands in natural language form.
- Proposes ARALLM (i.e., Analogical Reasoning Augmented Large Language Models) consisting of two modules: Analogical Reasoning based Prompting and Reasoning-Augmented Multi-Task Model Distillation.
