# Training Data Attribution: Was Your Model Secretly Trained On Data Created By Mine?

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The emergence of text-to-image models has recently sparked interest, but the attendant is a looming shadow of potential infringement by violating user terms.
- Besides, proposes a statistical-level attribution method, utilizing the shadow model technique to train an attribution discriminator.
- Reports evaluation showing the attribution accuracy and AUC scores of our methods are over 80% even when the infringing model only uses a small proportion of generated data.
