# PlatformBid: An Auto-Bidding Benchmark from a Unified Advertising Platform's Perspective

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Real-time bidding is central to computational advertising, comprising three elements: Supply Side Platform (SSP) selling ad impressions, Demand Side Platform (DSP) bidding for advertisers, and Ad Exchange conducting auctions between them.
- Besides these methods, further proposes a auto-bidding method based on flow-matching, termed BidFlow, which leverages the flow-matching method's expressive policy representation to effectively handle dynamic competitive environments.
- Proposes PlatformBid - the first comprehensive benchmark designed from a unified ad platform's perspective.
