# GradMix: Gradient-based Selective Mixup for Robust Data Augmentation in Class-Incremental Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In the context of continual learning, acquiring new knowledge while maintaining previous knowledge presents a challenge.
- Then propose GradMix, a robust data augmentation method specifically designed for mitigating catastrophic forgetting in class-incremental learning.
- Our experiments on various real datasets show that GradMix outperforms data augmentation baselines in accuracy by minimizing the forgetting of previous knowledge.
