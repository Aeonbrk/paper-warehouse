# Tackling Instance-Dependent Label Noise with Class Rebalance and Geometric Regularization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In label-noise learning, accurately identifying the transition matrix is crucial for developing statistically consistent classifiers.
- To tackle these challenges, our paper introduces a Class Rebalance and Geometric Regularization-based Framework (CRGR).
- Experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate CRGR's superiority over existing methods, boosting classification accuracy and showcasing its effectiveness in handling instance-dependent noise.
