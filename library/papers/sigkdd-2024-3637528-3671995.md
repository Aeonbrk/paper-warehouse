# Quantifying and Estimating the Predictability Upper Bound of Univariate Numeric Time Series

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The intrinsic predictability of a given time series indicates how well an (ideal) algorithm could potentially predict it when trained on the time series data.
- Call the intrinsic predictability the predictability upper bound ¶imax and proposes a method for quantifying and estimating it for univariate numeric time series.
- Give formal proofs and validate our arguments experimentally by comparing ¶imax with the prediction accuracy of different models on various real-world datasets from different domains.
