# Beyond Locations: A Motion Range-Aware Similarity Join

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- With the proliferation of GPS-enabled devices such as smartphones, the querying of moving objects has attracted substantial attention, with studies covering joins, range and kNN queries, similarity queries, etc.
- Proposes Intersection Similarity Join (IS-Join), a approach that identifies object pairs based on the overlap of their motion ranges rather than location-based proximity.
- Experiments on two real-world trajectory datasets demonstrate that IS-Join outperforms well-designed baselines, achieving up to a 3x reduction in runtime.
