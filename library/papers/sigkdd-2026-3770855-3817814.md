# Generating Graph-Like Logical Rules for Knowledge Graph Reasoning via Diffusion Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Logical rules constitute a cornerstone of knowledge graph (KG) reasoning, valued for their interpretability and ability to model relational patterns.
- Meanwhile, generative approaches such as diffusion models, despite their success in other domains, cannot be directly applied to rule mining because their training objectives are not aligned with the goal of learning high-quality rules.
- Experiments on six benchmark datasets show that GRiD achieves competitive performance on KG completion tasks.
