# One Fits All: Learning Fair Graph Neural Networks for Various Sensitive Attributes

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent studies have highlighted fairness issues in Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), where they produce discriminatory predictions against specific protected groups categorized by sensitive attributes such as race and age.
- Proposes a graph fairness framework based on invariant learning, namely FairINV, which enables the training of fair GNNs to accommodate various sensitive attributes within a single training session.
- Experimental results on several real-world datasets demonstrate that FairINV outperforms fairness approaches, underscoring its effectiveness.
