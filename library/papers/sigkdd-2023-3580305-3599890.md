# QTNet: Theory-based Queue Length Prediction for Urban Traffic

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Smart traffic management is the cornerstone of Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS).
- Proposes aQueueing-theory-based Neural Network (QTNet), which combines data-driven STGNN methods with queueing-theory-based domain knowledge of traffic engineering in order to achieve accurate and explainable predictions.
- In our queue length prediction experiments using a real-world dataset collected in urban areas of Tokyo, QTNet outperformed the baseline methods including the STGNNs by 12.6% in RMSE and 9.9% MAE.
