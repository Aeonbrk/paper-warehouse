# Web Scale Graph Mining for Cyber Threat Intelligence

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Defending against today's increasingly sophisticated and large-scale cyberattacks demands accurate, real-time threat intelligence.
- Introduces Threat Intelligence Tracking via Adaptive Networks (Titan), an industry-scale graph mining framework that generates cyber threat intelligence at unprecedented speed and scale.
- With an impressive average macro-F1 score of 0.89 and a precision-recall AUC of 0.94, Titan identifies millions of high-risk entities each week, enabling a 6x increase in non-file threat intelligence.
