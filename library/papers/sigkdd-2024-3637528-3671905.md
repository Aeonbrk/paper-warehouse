# MAML-en-LLM: Model Agnostic Meta-Training of LLMs for Improved In-Context Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Adapting large language models (LLMs) to unseen tasks with incontext training samples without fine-tuning remains an important research problem.
- Proposes MAML-en-LLM, a method for meta-training LLMs, which can learn truly generalizable parameters that not only performs well on disjointed tasks but also adapts to unseen tasks.
- Exhaustive experiments across 7 task settings along with two data settings demonstrate that models trained with MAML-en-LLM outperform SOTA meta-training approaches.
