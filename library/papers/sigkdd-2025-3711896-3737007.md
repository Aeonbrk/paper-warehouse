# Imputation via Domain Adaptation: Rethinking Variable Subset Forecasting from Knowledge Transfer

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multivariate time series forecasting in practical deployment faces a critical challenge termed Variable Subset Forecasting (VSF), where certain variables accessible during training are entirely missing during inference.
- To address these challenges, redefine VSF as a cross-domain knowledge transfer problem and propose VIDA, a framework that systematically transfers Variable Invariant knowledge from complete to partial observations through Domain Adaptation.
- Experiments across four real-world datasets show that VIDA outperforms imputation methods by 25% on average with partially observed variables.
