# Are LLMs Socially Adaptive? Contrasting Belief Evolution in Large Language Models and Humans

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- As large language models (LLMs) increasingly engage in complex social interactions, ensuring that their behaviors align with human ethical principles and intentions, known as value alignment, has become a critical scientific challenge.
- Introduces the Belief-Reward Alignment Behavior Evolution Model (BREM), a probabilistic framework that formalizes decision-making as a dynamic trade-off between maximizing extrinsic rewards and upholding intrinsic beliefs.
- Proposes FairMindSim, a realistic simulation benchmark rooted in social psychology that evaluates alignment through continuous economic games.
