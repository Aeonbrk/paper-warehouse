## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Feature selection is indispensable for mitigating overfitting and reducing feature redundancy in high-dimensional scenarios.
- Although product manifolds provide a natural geometric framework to model distinctive manifolds for different classes, their potential in feature selection is largely unexplored, e.g., theoretical guarantee on spectral convergence.
