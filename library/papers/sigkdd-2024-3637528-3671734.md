# Revisiting Reciprocal Recommender Systems: Metrics, Formulation, and Method

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Reciprocal recommender systems~(RRS), conducting bilateral recommendations between two involved parties, have gained increasing attention for enhancing matching efficiency.
- By utilizing the potential outcome framework, further develop a model-agnostic causal reciprocal recommendation method that considers the causal effects of recommendations.
- Experiments on two real-world datasets from recruitment and dating scenarios demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed metrics and approach.
