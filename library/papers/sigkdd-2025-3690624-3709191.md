# Classifying Treatment Responders: Bounds and Algorithms

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Treatment responders are individuals whose outcomes would change from negative to positive if treated, and learning a classifier to predict responders would help causal decision-making in real applications.
- Based on these findings, further proposes a Classifying Treatment Responder Learning (CTRL) algorithm to accurately identify the treatment responders, and theoretically report higher performance for jointly learning over two-stage learning.
- Experiments on semi-synthetic and real-world datasets show that our method better predicts treatment responders and adaptively trades off false-positives and false-negatives with varying weight coefficients.
