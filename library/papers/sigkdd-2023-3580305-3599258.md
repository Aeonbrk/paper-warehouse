# Anomaly Detection with Score Distribution Discrimination

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Recent studies give more attention to the anomaly detection (AD) methods that can leverage a handful of labeled anomalies along with abundant unlabeled data.
- Proposes to optimize the anomaly scoring function from the view of score distribution.
- Experimental results indicate that Overlap loss based AD models outperform their counterparts, and achieve better performance on different types of anomalies.
