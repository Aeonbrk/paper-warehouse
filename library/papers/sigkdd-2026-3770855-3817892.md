# CoPersona: Collaborative Persona Graphs for Robust LLM Personalization

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Real-world LLM personalization is often constrained by sparse and skewed user histories: most users provide only a handful of interactions, while even frequent users' logs capture an incomplete and biased view of their preferences.
- Presents CoPersona, a graph-based collaborative personalization framework that completes sparse user profiles by borrowing signals from behaviorally similar peers.
- As a result, weakly observed user attributes are difficult to infer, leading to brittle personalization when test-time requests shift toward under-supported facets.
