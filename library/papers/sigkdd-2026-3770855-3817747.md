# HURST: Heterogeneity-Adaptive Urban Foundation Models for Spatiotemporal Prediction via Self-Partitional Mixture-of-Spatial-Experts

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Urban foundation models (UFMs) are pre-trained spatiotemporal (ST) prediction models with the ability to generalize to different tasks.
- Proposes HURST, a Heterogeneity-Adaptive URban Foundation Model for Spatio-Temporal Prediction, that is capable of capturing the spatial pattern of heterogeneity underlying the urban setting to enhance the UFM's performance.
