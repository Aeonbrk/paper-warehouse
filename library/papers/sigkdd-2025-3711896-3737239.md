# Large Language Models can Deliver Accurate and Interpretable Time Series Anomaly Detection

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Time series anomaly detection (TSAD) plays a crucial role in various industrial applications.
- Proposes LLMAD, a TSAD method that employs Large Language Models (LLMs) to deliver accurate and interpretable TSAD results.
- Experiments conducted on both offline datasets and through online deployment on a large-scale cloud system at Microsoft indicate that our LLMAD achieves detection performance comparable to deep learning methods.
