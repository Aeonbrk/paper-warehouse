# Graph2Route: A Dynamic Spatial-Temporal Graph Neural Network for Pick-up and Delivery Route Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Pick-up and delivery (P&D) services such as food delivery have achieved explosive growth in recent years by providing customers with daily-life convenience.
- Formulates the Pick-up and Delivery Route Prediction task (PDRP task for short) from the graph perspective for the first time, then proposes a dynamic spatial-temporal graph-based model, named Graph2Route.
- Offline experiments over two real-world industry-scale datasets under different P&D services (i.e., food delivery and package pick-up) and online A/B test report higher performance for our proposed model.
