# GaussTrap: Stealthy Backdoor Attacks on 3D Gaussian Splatting for Targeted Scene Misperception

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has recently emerged as a paradigm for real-time scene representation and view synthesis, gaining traction in safety-critical applications such as autonomous driving and AR/VR systems.
- Proposes GaussTrap, a stealthy poisoning framework tailored for 3DGS.
- Experiments on both synthetic and real-world benchmarks demonstrate that GaussTrap reliably induces targeted visual corruption under trigger conditions while maintaining high rendering fidelity elsewhere, highlighting a critical security blind spot in modern 3D rendering.
