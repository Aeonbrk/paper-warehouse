# Unsupervised Learning Style Classification for Learning Path Generation in Online Education Platforms

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Online education, which educates students that cannot be present at school, has become an important supplement to traditional education.
- Learning Style Classification (LSC) is proposed to analyze the learning behavior patterns of online learning users, based on which personalized learning paths are generated to help them learn and maintain their interests.
- Without the direct supervision and instruction of teachers, online education is always concerned with potential distractions and misunderstandings.
