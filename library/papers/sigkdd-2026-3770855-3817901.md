# Scale-Invariant Lifelong Cross-Modal Hashing via Gradient Equilibrium

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Real-world multimedia streams exhibit severe task-scale imbalance, from social media bursts to long-tail surveillance events.
- Proposes Scale-Invariant Lifelong Cross-Modal Hashing (SILCH), a principled framework that achieves gradient equilibrium regardless of task-size ratios.
- Theoretical analysis gives an exact scale-invariance result for the inverse-scale weights and explains the stabilizing role of smoothing.
