# Frequency-Domain Multi-Modality Transportation Modeling

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Multi-modality transportation refers to urban systems composed of multiple transportation modes, such as traffic flow and public transit, whose dynamics are coupled by shared temporal patterns.
- Proposes a lightweight yet Frequency-Domain Multi-Modality modeling (FreMo) that explicitly exploits the frequency domain to enable adaptive and selective cross-modality synergy.
- Experiments on real-world datasets show that FreMo consistently outperforms baselines, with performance and generalization across diverse forecasting scenarios.
