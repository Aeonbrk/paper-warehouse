# GUIDER: Generative User Interest Discovery via Explicit Reasoning with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs) has driven a paradigm shift in sequential recommendation from discriminative ranking to generative modeling.
- Proposes GUIDER (Generative User Interest Discovery & Explicit Reasoning), a framework that fundamentally decouples intent planning from item matching by reformulating sequential modeling within a rigorous closed-set semantic interest space.
- Offline evaluations and large-scale online A/B testing on the Kuaishou platform validate that GUIDER not only achieves accuracy but also yields consistent gains in average app duration and effectively facilitates out-of-cocoon interest exploration.
