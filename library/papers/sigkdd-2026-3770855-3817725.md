# Label Annotation for Tabular Anomaly Detection with Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Tabular Anomaly Detection (TAD) plays a fundamental role in securing real-world applications.
- Develops TaDFM, a foundation model tailored to internalize generalizable deductive logic for zero-shot annotation.
- Experiments on both public and large-scale real-world TAD datasets report higher performance for TaDFM over representative methods, with its practical value further validated by an industrial case study.
