# Statistical Models of Top-k Partial Orders with Variable k

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In many contexts involving ranked preferences, agents submit partial orders over available alternatives.
- Introduces and taxonomize approaches for jointly modeling distributions over top-k partial orders and list lengths k, considering two classes of approaches: composite models that view a partial order as a truncation of a total.
- Methods from this work have implications on the simulation and evaluation of real-world social systems that solicit ranked preferences.
