# One Step Closer to Ground Truth: A Multi-Scale Residual-Aware Representation Learning Pipeline for Predicting Time Series Data

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Transformer-based models have emerged as leading paradigms in time-series forecasting in recent years, employing self-attention mechanisms to capture long-range dependencies.
- Introduces a two-stage, model-agnostic framework that explicitly decouples forecasting and residual learning into distinct stages of representation learning.
- Evaluated on eight popular benchmark datasets using established protocols, our approach achieves performance, with improvements in standard metrics (MSE, MAE).
