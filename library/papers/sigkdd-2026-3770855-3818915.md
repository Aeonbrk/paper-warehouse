# On Model Selection for Time to Event Tasks

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The task of time-to-event prediction supports decision making across domains ranging from medical research to industrial manufacturing.
- Proposes a unified evaluation framework for optimized selection of both regression-based RUL and SA models, which allows analysts to exploit their complementary strengths for accurate time-to-event prediction.
- Existing studies typically focus on a single paradigm and evaluate it using limited metrics, thus overlooking cases where one may outperform the other.
