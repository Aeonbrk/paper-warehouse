# Adversarial Signed Graph Learning with Differential Privacy

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Signed graphs with positive and negative edges can model complex relationships in social networks.
- Presents ASGL, a privacy-preserving adversarial signed graph learning method that preserves high utility while achieving node-level DP.
- Experiments on real-world datasets show that ASGL achieves favorable privacy-utility trade-offs across multiple downstream tasks.
