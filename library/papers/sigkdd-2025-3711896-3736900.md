# CryptoMixer: Fine-grained market information-aware MLP Networks for Individual Cryptocurrency Trading Prediction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Accurately predicting user trading behavior in decentralized exchanges is essential for investors to mitigate risks and optimize their trading strategies.
- Proposes CryptoMixer, a lightweight fine-grained market information-aware multilayer perceptron (MLP)-based model for high-frequency cryptocurrency trading behavior prediction.
- Evaluates CryptoMixer on real-world user trading datasets from the Uniswap decentralized finance platform.
