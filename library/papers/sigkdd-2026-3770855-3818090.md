# Spectral Signatures of Large Language Models

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- The rapidly growing repository of publicly available large language models (LLMs) presents challenges for systematic management and quantification at scale, such as model lineage tracing, licensing, and evaluation.
- Our approach uses the shape information of the weight empirical spectral density as a compact spectral signature of each model.
- Moreover, curate a large and diverse model corpus consisting of major open-source LLM families, and use it to systematically benchmark spectral and non-spectral metrics across models and downstream tasks.
