# Spending Programmed Bidding: Privacy-friendly Bid Optimization with ROI Constraint in Online Advertising

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Privacy policies have disrupted the multi-billion dollar online advertising market by making real-time and precise user data untraceable, which poses challenges to the optimization of Return-On-Investment (ROI) constrained products in the online advertising industry.
- Introduces a Spending Programmed Bidding (SPB) framework to navigate these challenges.
- Find that when privacy-constrained channels are present, SPB is to bidding methods in both offline datasets and online experiments on a large ad platform.
