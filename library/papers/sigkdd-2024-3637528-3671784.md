# Warming Up Cold-Start CTR Prediction by Learning Item-Specific Feature Interactions

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- In recommendation systems, new items are continuously introduced, initially lacking interaction records but gradually accumulating them over time.
- Addressing this, our work introduces EmerG, a approach that warms up cold-start CTR prediction by learning item-specific feature interaction patterns.
- Experiments on benchmark datasets validate that EmerG consistently performs the best given no, a few and sufficient instances of new items.
