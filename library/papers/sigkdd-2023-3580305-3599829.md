# FS-REAL: Towards Real-World Cross-Device Federated Learning

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Federated Learning (FL) aims to train high-quality models in collaboration with distributed clients while not uploading their local data, which attracts increasing attention in both academia and industry.
- Proposes an efficient and scalable prototyping system for real-world cross-device FL, FS-REAL.
- There is still a considerable gap between the flourishing FL research and real-world scenarios, mainly caused by the characteristics of heterogeneous devices and its scales.
