# SepsisCalc: Integrating Clinical Calculators into Early Sepsis Prediction via Dynamic Temporal Graph Construction

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Sepsis is an organ dysfunction caused by a deregulated immune response to an infection.
- Proposes to mimic clinicians' workflow with a framework SepsisCalc to integrate clinical calculators into the predictive model, yielding a clinically transparent and precise model for utilization in clinical settings.
- Experimental results on real-world datasets show that the proposed model outperforms methods on sepsis prediction tasks.
