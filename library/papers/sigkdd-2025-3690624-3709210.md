# Proactive Model Adaptation Against Concept Drift for Online Time Series Forecasting

## Abstract-derived synopsis

- Time series forecasting always faces the challenge of concept drift, where data distributions evolve over time, leading to a decline in forecast model performance.
- Presents Proceed, a proactive model adaptation framework for online time series forecasting.
- Experiments on five real-world datasets across various forecast models demonstrate that Proceed brings more performance improvements than the online learning methods, facilitating forecast models' resilience against concept drifts.
